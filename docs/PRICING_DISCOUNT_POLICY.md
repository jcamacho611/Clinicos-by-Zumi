# Discount policy V1

Prefer structured incentives over ad-hoc discounting:
- 100% credit-forward of qualifying analysis/blueprint fees within stated terms.
- Up to ~15% annual recurring software commitment savings.
- Founding-customer concessions may exchange discount for case-study/reference rights, longer commitment, faster decision, limited scope, or strategic network value, documented explicitly.
- Do not automatically discount implementation, migration, premium connectors, vendor pass-through, usage packs, or transaction costs.
- Sales cannot invent a permanent price; exceptions require an auditable commercial override.
