# Pricing risk register

1. Production deploy currently unhealthy. Mitigation: no merge until deployment root cause is resolved and exact head passes Quality.
2. Price too low for implementation burden. Mitigation: minimum implementation anchors and track hours/margin.
3. Price too high before proof. Mitigation: $500 paid analysis entry and credit-forward funnel.
4. Vendor usage destroys margin. Mitigation: reservation-before-spend and customer-funded waterfall.
5. Grid paid entry suppresses liquidity. Mitigation: free basic supply, optional Pro, success-event economics.
6. Percentage fees create regulatory exposure. Mitigation: class-specific configuration and legal review; support flat/zero/negotiated fees.
7. Checkout claims exceed connected payment capability. Mitigation: verify processor product/amount and keep manual reconciliation/pending states.
8. Duplicated prices drift. Mitigation: canonical commercial source + QA search.
9. Discounting erodes value. Mitigation: prefer credit-forward and annual commitment incentives over arbitrary monthly discounts.
10. Enterprise complexity leaks into standard plans. Mitigation: custom scope, connector, migration, governance, and capacity pricing.
