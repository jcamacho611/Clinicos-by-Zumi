# Pricing guardrails

- Do not sell a feature merely because a UI exists.
- Do not activate paid production capability from account creation alone.
- Do not front unbounded API/vendor cost.
- Do not use payment as authorization for regulated action.
- Do not call a paid integration connected until readiness is verified.
- Do not charge a universal percentage across healthcare resource classes without legal review.
- Do not bury processor/pass-through economics in a promise of unlimited usage.
- Do not create separate specialty codebases merely to justify different prices; use configuration/presets unless architecture requires otherwise.
- Do not underprice high-touch implementation to imitate commodity practice-management SaaS.
- Do not force every Grid participant to pay before the network has liquidity.
