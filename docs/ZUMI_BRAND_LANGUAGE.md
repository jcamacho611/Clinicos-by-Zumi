# Zumi Brand Language

Version: `2026-08-12.1`
Status: `CANONICAL MARKETING LANGUAGE`

This document defines recurring public-facing language for Zumi. It complements, but does not replace, Klinikos product truth, safety, legal, security, or capability-state documentation.

## Primary recurring campaign phrase

# Zumi Everywhere

`Zumi Everywhere` is an official recurring Klinikos catchphrase and campaign platform.

It communicates the long-term product idea that Zumi is not a chatbot hidden on one page. Zumi is the intelligence layer that should be available wherever legitimate Klinikos work happens, across approved roles, surfaces, workflows, devices, and future experiences.

Use it as a short, memorable declaration rather than a detailed product claim.

Preferred display forms:

- `Zumi Everywhere.`
- `ZUMI. EVERYWHERE.`
- `Zumi, everywhere Klinikos works.`

## Supporting lines

Use these as rotating secondary lines beneath or around `Zumi Everywhere`:

- One intelligence across the Klinikos experience.
- Ask once. Move across the system.
- Your Klinikos intelligence, wherever the work happens.
- From question to action, across Klinikos.
- One conversation. Every authorized workflow.
- Wherever the work moves, Zumi moves with it.
- Your operating intelligence, always within reach.
- Ask Zumi. Keep moving.
- Zumi goes where the work goes.
- Klinikos connects the system. Zumi moves through it with you.

## Product-language hierarchy

`Zumi Everywhere` is a campaign phrase, not the only Zumi tagline.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Product/company truth: `Klinikos operates the clinic. Zumi coordinates the work. Grid connects qualified people and resources around it.`
2. Zumi campaign platform: `Zumi Everywhere.`
3. Contextual proof line: describe the exact surface or capability currently available.
4. Call to action: `Ask Zumi`, `Open Zumi`, `Talk to Zumi`, or another truthful action.

## Where to use it

Strong uses include:

- social campaigns;
- product launch creative;
- demo openings and closings;
- homepage or landing-page sections;
- pitch decks;
- conference/event visuals;
- onboarding moments;
- product loading or transition states;
- app-store or mobile creative when those surfaces exist;
- founder storytelling;
- teaser videos;
- campaign merchandise or environmental graphics.

`ONE INTELLIGENCE. ACROSS KLINIKOS.`

### Zumi Everywhere: Future

`WHEREVER KLINIKOS GOES, ZUMI GOES.`

A forward-looking line for approved future mobile, voice-first, patient, provider, wearable, vehicle, kiosk, and other surfaces.

## Brand behavior

The phrase should feel:

- inevitable;
- expansive;
- simple;
- premium;
- confident;
- technologically advanced;
- useful rather than gimmicky.

Avoid over-explaining it in the headline. The surrounding product experience should prove the promise.

## Truth boundary

`Zumi Everywhere` expresses the product direction and approved campaign idea. It must not be used to falsely imply that every possible device, workflow, external service, patient surface, public surface, sensor, connector, or operating system is already live.

When a specific capability matters to a buying or operational decision, pair the campaign phrase with truthful current-state language.

Do not use the phrase to imply covert monitoring, always-listening microphones, unrestricted computer control, autonomous clinical authority, or access that has not been explicitly connected and authorized.
