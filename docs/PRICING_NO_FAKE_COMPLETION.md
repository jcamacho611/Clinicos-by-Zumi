# No fake completion

A price on a page is not a working commercial system.

Do not mark pricing complete until the paid state can be verified and enforced at the server boundary. Do not mark a recurring plan active until recurring billing/provisioning is real or a documented human contract/reconciliation process is the authoritative activation path. Do not mark Grid payouts active until money can actually be settled through an approved processor. Do not mark an integration active because it was purchased.
