# Klinikos Pixel-Reference Reconstruction

The supplied approved desktop reference is the visual source of truth for the first-visit Living Home.

## Acceptance law

“Exact” means visually identical to the approved reference as far as a responsive functional web product can reasonably be made.

Do not reinterpret, modernize, simplify, substitute generic equivalents, or stop at the same vibe. Treat every visible design decision as deliberate.

The only intentional content departure is the car imagery inside the wide bottom strip. Preserve that strip as part of the same first-screen composition, but replace the car imagery with the approved first-visit starting surface. Do not remove the strip, push it into a later marketing section, or turn it into a large secondary section.

Copy may be adjusted narrowly when necessary to align with truthful functionality already built in Klinikos, but geometry, hierarchy, typography, color, lighting, borders, visual rhythm, and composition remain reference-locked.

## First-screen composition

The desktop first-screen composition includes, together:

- orbital K mark and stylized KLINIKOS wordmark;
- centered primary navigation: DASHBOARDS, GRID, CARE, EDU, INTELLIGENCE;
- right profile/auth control;
- dominant centered rose environment;
- KLINIKOS INTELLIGENCE eyebrow;

- supporting copy;
- composer;
- overlapping Zumi orb and subtitle;
- left truthful intelligence rail: LISTENING, UNDERSTANDING, CONNECTING, PREPARING, READY;
- right action rail: PATIENTS, GRID, CARE, BILLING, INSIGHTS;
- one shallow row of four cards;
- wide low-profile cinematic starting strip directly beneath the cards.

All of these belong to the same first-visit composition.

## Brand assets

- Reproduce/use the approved orbital K mark as closely as possible, preferably vector.
- Reproduce/use the stylized KLINIKOS lettering from the approved reference rather than a generic uppercase text rendering.
- Preserve high-DPI crispness.

## Rose

The rose is the central visual environment, not a glow.

Match visible petal structure, silhouette, scale, crop, center position, burgundy/red tone, dark edge falloff, depth, shadow, highlight placement, vignette, and foreground-opacity relationship.

At desktop width the rose must occupy most of the central composition as in the reference.

## Color family

Use obsidian black, near-black, black cherry, oxblood, deep burgundy, dusty rose, muted coral, ember pink, warm ivory, and muted rose-gray.

Converted reference surfaces must not be dominated by cyan, teal, or cool blue.

## Typography

Calibrate the hero, eyebrow, supporting copy, nav, rails, cards, CTA labels, and brand lettering separately. Match thin headline weight, line-height, tracking, line breaks, and proportions.

## Geometry

Treat the reference as a measurement map. Match relative positions and dimensions of header, hero, rose, rails, composer, Zumi overlap, card row, and wide starting strip instead of relying on arbitrary spacing tokens.

## Surfaces and lighting

Match the warm rose atmosphere, low-opacity panels, border tint, subtle inner light, composer depth, Zumi halo, active rail glow, and card darkness. Avoid generic glassmorphism, gray SaaS cards, excessive shadowing, and unrelated gradients.

## Functionality law

The finished reconstruction remains functional and truthful.

- Composer submits to the real public intent resolver.
- Progress states correspond to real application processing states.
- Navigation and rail actions lead to real governed destinations.
- Cards lead to real implemented workspaces.
- No fake clinical, payment, credential, marketplace, or external-integration completion may be introduced for visual fidelity.
- Existing security, auth, tenant, RBAC, PHI, payment, and clinical-governance boundaries remain intact.

## Visual difference loop

For every implementation iteration:

1. render at 1440px desktop width;
2. capture a screenshot;
3. compare side-by-side with the approved reference;
4. identify the largest visible differences;
5. correct them;
6. repeat.

Priority order: rose, wordmark/logo, hero typography, geometry, composer/Zumi, rails, cards, then color/lighting micro-adjustments.

Repeat on a larger desktop width and mobile.

Do not mark the pass complete while obvious differences remain in the rose, logo, KLINIKOS lettering, typography, color, geometry, composer, Zumi, rails, cards, or wide starting strip.
