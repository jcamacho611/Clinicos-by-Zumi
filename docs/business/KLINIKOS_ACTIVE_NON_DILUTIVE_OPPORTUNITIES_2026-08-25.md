## P0 - Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network

Opportunity type: public procurement / workforce program contract
Current truth: ACTIVE PROCUREMENT PIPELINE
Prime respondent: KLINIKOS, INC.

### Material new clarification from SCWDB

SCWDB's 2026-08-24 formal Q&A materially reduces the qualification ambiguity that existed around Klinikos being a newly formed legal entity.

SCWDB states that it may consider clearly documented relevant experience of key personnel, affiliated entities, or predecessor organizations when evaluating the three-year experience requirement, particularly where meaningful continuity of leadership, personnel, operations, or service delivery exists.

The proposal must clearly distinguish:
- work actually performed by KLINIKOS, INC.;
- work performed by an individual;
- work performed by an affiliate or predecessor.

Prior work must not be falsely attributed to the new entity.

### Appendix C references

SCWDB states that Appendix C references may include relevant comparable engagements performed by key personnel, affiliated organizations, or predecessor entities when relied upon to demonstrate the qualifications and experience of the proposed project team.

Each reference should identify:
- who actually performed the work;
- relationship to the current respondent;
- role of proposed key personnel;
- nature and scope of comparable services.

Appendix C requires three references for comparable work completed within the last five years, but SCWDB's Q&A says it does not expressly require all three contracts to have been held directly by the current prime legal entity.

### Teaming / subcontractors

SCWDB states that a respondent may use teaming or subcontracting to demonstrate the experience and capacity of the proposed delivery team.

An experienced organization is not necessarily required to be the prime solely because it supplies qualifying technical or instructional experience.

The prime still must demonstrate sufficient organizational, administrative, financial, management, and contract-management capacity and remains accountable for performance, compliance, records, and payment.

### Out-of-state prime

SCWDB confirms that an out-of-state U.S. for-profit corporation may serve as prime contractor. A Kentucky physical office is not required, provided the respondent can satisfy the RFP's delivery, staffing, financial/administrative, reporting, accessibility, security, and compliance requirements, including in-person delivery when requested.

### Pricing / contract ceiling

SCWDB states that no final contract ceiling or target budget has been established.

The RFP's approximately 980 unique participants is a planning capacity figure only. It is not a guaranteed minimum number of participants, completions, classes, referrals, or payments.

Therefore:
- keep pricing defensible and scalable;
- do not treat 980 participants as guaranteed revenue;
- do not treat current illustrative proposal scenarios as booked revenue.

Priority: P0

1. Reconcile the prepared Kentucky proposal against the 2026-08-24 Q&A.
2. Make founder/key-person/affiliate experience attribution explicit everywhere.
3. Ensure all three Appendix C references clearly identify actual performer, relationship, key-person role, and comparable scope.
