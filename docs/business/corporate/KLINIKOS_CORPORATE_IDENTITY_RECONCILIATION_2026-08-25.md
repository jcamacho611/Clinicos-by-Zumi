Current company/procurement evidence names **Justin R. Camacho** as Founder/CEO/authorized representative.

These fields are not interchangeable.

IRS primary-source rules state that the responsible party is the person the IRS recognizes as ultimately controlling, managing, or directing the entity and disposition of its funds; if that person changes, Form 8822-B is used and responsible-party changes must be reported within 60 days.

Primary sources:

- https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/responsible-parties-and-nominees
- https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8822-b
