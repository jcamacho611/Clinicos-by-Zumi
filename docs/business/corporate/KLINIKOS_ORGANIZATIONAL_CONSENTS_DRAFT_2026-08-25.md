RESOLVED, that the Chief Executive Officer/President, once duly appointed, is authorized to execute ordinary-course commercial agreements consistent with Board-approved policy, budget, approved offers, and applicable law, excluding matters reserved below.

### Reserved matters requiring specific Board approval

- material borrowing or guarantees;
- liens/security interests;
- issuance, transfer, repurchase, or redemption of equity;
- stock options/equity compensation;
- mergers/acquisitions;
- sale or exclusive license of material IP;
- parent/subsidiary restructuring;
- material related-party agreements;
- material settlements;
- tax elections outside ordinary filing administration;
- transactions outside ordinary approved budget/policy thresholds.
