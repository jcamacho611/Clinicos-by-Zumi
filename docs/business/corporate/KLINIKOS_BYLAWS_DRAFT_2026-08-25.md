# BY-LAWS OF KLINIKOS, INC.

**DRAFT FOR REVIEW AND EXECUTION — NOT ADOPTED / NOT SIGNED**

Prepared: 2026-08-25

These draft by-laws are for a New York business corporation based on currently available Klinikos formation evidence. They do not establish directors, officers, ownership, or share issuances until properly adopted and the related corporate actions are executed.

Primary New York authorities reviewed:

- BCL §404: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/404
- BCL §601: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/601
- BCL Article 6: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/A6
- BCL Article 7: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC/A7

## ARTICLE I — OFFICES

### Section 1. Principal Office

The principal business office of the Corporation shall be at such location as the Board of Directors may determine from time to time.

### Section 2. New York Office / Records Location

The Corporation shall maintain such office, registered agent/transfer-agent arrangement, or records location in New York as applicable law requires. Corporate records may be maintained electronically if they can be converted into written form within a reasonable time as permitted by law.

---

## ARTICLE II — SHAREHOLDERS

### Section 1. Annual Meeting

An annual meeting of shareholders shall be held at a date and time determined by the Board for the election of directors and transaction of other proper business.

### Section 2. Special Meetings

Special meetings may be called as permitted by the New York Business Corporation Law, the Certificate of Incorporation, these By-Laws, or a valid Board resolution.

### Section 3. Place / Remote Participation

Shareholder meetings may be held within or outside New York at a place fixed by the Board and may use remote-participation methods to the extent permitted by then-current law and any applicable Board procedures.

### Section 4. Notice

Notice shall be given in the time and manner required by the New York Business Corporation Law unless validly waived.

### Section 5. Quorum and Voting

Unless the Certificate of Incorporation, these By-Laws, or applicable law requires a greater amount, quorum and voting requirements shall be those provided by the New York Business Corporation Law.

### Section 6. Written Consent

Shareholders may act without a meeting only to the extent and in the manner permitted by BCL §615 and the Certificate of Incorporation.

### Section 7. Proxies

A shareholder may vote by proxy to the extent permitted by law.

---

## ARTICLE III — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### Section 1. General Authority

The business and affairs of the Corporation shall be managed under the direction of its Board of Directors, subject to the Certificate of Incorporation, these By-Laws, and applicable law.

### Section 2. Number

The Board shall initially consist of **one (1) director**, unless and until the number is changed by amendment of these By-Laws or other action permitted by law.

**Execution note:** this one-director structure is a proposed founder-stage simplification authorized by BCL §702. It does not appoint the initial director. The incorporator action must do that separately.

### Section 3. Qualifications

Each director shall meet the qualifications required by law. Additional qualifications may be adopted in the Certificate of Incorporation or these By-Laws.

### Section 4. Election and Term

Directors shall be elected and hold office as provided by the New York Business Corporation Law, the Certificate of Incorporation, and these By-Laws.

### Section 5. Resignation and Removal

A director may resign or be removed as provided by applicable law and governing documents.

### Section 6. Vacancies

Vacancies and newly created directorships shall be filled in the manner permitted by law and these By-Laws.

### Section 7. Regular and Special Meetings

Regular meetings may be held at times determined by the Board. Special meetings may be called by the Chair, President, or any director while the Board consists of one director, and otherwise as the Board may provide.

### Section 8. Notice and Waiver

Board-meeting notice and waiver shall comply with BCL §711 and any procedures adopted by the Board.

### Section 9. Quorum and Vote

Unless a greater requirement applies, quorum and Board action shall be governed by the New York Business Corporation Law.

### Section 10. Written Consent

Unless restricted by the Certificate of Incorporation or these By-Laws, Board action may be taken without a meeting by the written/electronic unanimous consent permitted by BCL §708. The resolution and consents shall be maintained with the Board minutes.

### Section 11. Remote Participation

Directors may participate by conference telephone or similar communications equipment to the extent permitted by BCL §708 and then-current law.

### Section 12. Committees

The Board may establish committees only to the extent permitted by law and may define their authority by resolution. No committee may exercise authority that applicable law reserves to the full Board or shareholders.

---

## ARTICLE IV — OFFICERS

### Section 1. Officers

The Board may elect or appoint a President, one or more Vice Presidents, a Secretary, a Treasurer, a Chief Executive Officer, and such other officers as it determines appropriate.

### Section 2. Multiple Offices

Two or more offices may be held by the same person to the extent permitted by BCL §715 and the Corporation's then-current ownership structure.

### Section 3. Appointment and Term

Officers shall be appointed/elected and serve in accordance with BCL §715, subject to removal, resignation, and successor appointment.

### Section 4. Chief Executive Officer / President

The Chief Executive Officer or President, as the Board designates, shall have the authority assigned by the Board, these By-Laws, and applicable law, including general supervision of ordinary business subject to Board oversight.

### Section 5. Secretary

The Secretary shall maintain or cause to be maintained corporate minutes, consents, notices, and other corporate records assigned by the Board; authenticate corporate records where authorized; and perform other duties assigned by the Board.

### Section 6. Treasurer / Finance Officer

The Treasurer or other finance officer shall oversee or coordinate corporate financial records, banking administration, and financial reporting as assigned by the Board, subject to approval thresholds and internal controls.

### Section 7. Authority Limits

No officer may:

- issue shares without Board authorization;
- incur debt, grant liens, guarantee obligations, or bind the Corporation to material financing outside delegated authority;
- transfer material IP outside delegated authority;
- make an ownership or tax election outside delegated authority;
- execute regulated clinical authority on behalf of the Corporation without lawful professional authority;
- override Board-reserved matters.

### Section 8. Removal

Officers may be removed in accordance with BCL §716 and any applicable contract rights.

---

## ARTICLE V — SHARES AND SHAREHOLDER RECORDS

### Section 1. Authorized Capital

The Corporation's authorized capital is determined solely by the filed Certificate of Incorporation and valid amendments. Current certificate evidence reflects **1,000,000 shares of common stock, no par value**.

### Section 2. Issuance

Shares may be issued only after valid Board/shareholder authorization as applicable and upon consideration permitted under BCL §504.

No statement in these By-Laws identifies any person or entity as a shareholder.

### Section 3. Consideration

The Board shall document the consideration for each issuance and its determination of value. Where non-cash property, IP, services, future-service obligations, vesting, repurchase rights, or related-party considerations are involved, legal/tax review is strongly recommended before issuance.

### Section 4. Certificates / Uncertificated Shares

Shares may be certificated or uncertificated as permitted by BCL §508 and Board resolution. If uncertificated, the Corporation shall provide the required written notice to the registered owner within a reasonable time.

### Section 5. Stock Ledger

The Corporation shall maintain the shareholder record required by BCL §624, including names and addresses of shareholders of record, number/class of shares held, and dates ownership of record began.

### Section 6. Transfers

Transfers shall comply with law, these By-Laws, valid shareholder agreements, legends/notices, and Board-approved restrictions where applicable.

---

## ARTICLE VI — CORPORATE RECORDS

The Corporation shall keep correct and complete books and records of account; minutes of shareholder and Board proceedings; written consents; and the shareholder record required by BCL §624.

The corporate book should separately preserve:

- filed certificate and amendments;
- bylaws and amendments;
- incorporator actions;
- Board/shareholder minutes and consents;
- officer/director records;
- capitalization and stock ledger;
- material agreements;
- IP assignments;
- debt/guaranty resolutions;
- tax-election evidence;
- annual/biennial filing evidence;
- material governance decisions.

---

## ARTICLE VII — CONTRACTS, BANKING, AND FINANCE

### Section 1. Contracts

The Board may authorize officers or agents to enter contracts on behalf of the Corporation and may establish dollar, risk, or subject-matter approval thresholds.

### Section 2. Banking

The Corporation may maintain bank, treasury, processor, and investment accounts in its own legal name. Signatories and transaction authority shall be established by Board resolution or delegated policy.

### Section 3. Debt and Guarantees

Material loans, lines of credit, guarantees, liens, security interests, or borrowing arrangements require Board approval unless the Board has expressly delegated a defined financing authority.

### Section 4. Corporate Separation

Corporate funds shall be maintained separately from personal funds and from affiliated-company funds except for documented lawful transactions such as capital contributions, loans, reimbursements, intercompany agreements, or payments for actual goods/services.

---

Directors and officers shall disclose material interests in transactions involving the Corporation. Related-party transactions should be documented and reviewed for corporate benefit, fairness, legal/tax consequences, and appropriate approval.

## ARTICLE IX — INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE

The Corporation may indemnify directors and officers to the fullest extent permitted by New York law and the Certificate of Incorporation, subject to statutory procedures and limitations. The Corporation may purchase appropriate D&O, cyber, E&O/professional, general liability, or other insurance as approved by the Board and commercially appropriate.

---

## ARTICLE X — ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS AND SIGNATURES

Notices, waivers, consents, minutes, and other corporate communications may be delivered or maintained electronically to the extent permitted by applicable law and the governing documents. Electronic execution must preserve attribution, integrity, date, and retention evidence appropriate to the action.

---

## ARTICLE XI — AMENDMENTS

These By-Laws may be amended, repealed, or replaced by the shareholders and, only to the extent authorized by the Certificate of Incorporation or shareholder-adopted bylaw and applicable law, by the Board.
