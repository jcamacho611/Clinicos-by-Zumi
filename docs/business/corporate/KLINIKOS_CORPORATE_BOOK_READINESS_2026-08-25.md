### Safe organizational order

1. Verify corporate existence / filed certificate evidence.
2. Incorporator signs organizational action.
3. Incorporator adopts initial bylaws.
4. Incorporator elects initial director(s).
5. Initial board signs written consent or holds organizational meeting.
6. Board appoints officers.
7. Board establishes banking/contracting authority.
8. Board approves any founder/IP assignments and related-party transactions after appropriate review.
9. Board approves any share issuance **only after the purchaser, number of shares, consideration, vesting/restrictions, and tax treatment are actually decided**.
10. Corporation updates the stock ledger/shareholder record only after issuance becomes effective.
11. Signed documents move to the controlled corporate data room; repository templates remain clearly marked as drafts.
