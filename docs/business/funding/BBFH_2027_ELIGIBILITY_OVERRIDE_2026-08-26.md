# BBFH 2027 — Eligibility Override

Status: AUTHORITATIVE APPLICATION ELIGIBILITY CORRECTION

Date: 2026-08-26

This file overrides any earlier internal wording that treated Australian development/commercialisation intent as relevant only to MedTech Compass eligibility.

## Current official public requirement

IDE Group's current BBFH 2027 page states:

> BBFH Challenge 2027 is open to individuals and companies with an intent to develop and/or commercialise their MedTech innovation in Australia, such as through R&D, clinical trials or manufacturing activities.

Source:
https://ide.group/bbfh-challenge/

## Application rule

Until IDE Group supplies an explicit written exception that clearly supersedes the public eligibility language for Klinikos:

- treat genuine Australian development and/or commercialisation intent as an **entry eligibility requirement**;
- do not submit a false Australian-intent answer merely to access the prize;
- do not convert general willingness to explore Australia into a firm claim about R&D, clinical trials, manufacturing, regulatory approval, customers, revenue, or partnerships;
- an authorized representative must make the final truthful eligibility decision before submission;
- if the authorized representative cannot truthfully attest that Klinikos intends to develop and/or commercialise the innovation in Australia, do not submit the direct BBFH application under the current published rules.
