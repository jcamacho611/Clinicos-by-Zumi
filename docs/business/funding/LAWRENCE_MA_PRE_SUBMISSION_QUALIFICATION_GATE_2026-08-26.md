The Massachusetts foreign-corporation requirement is **not merely a post-award operating requirement in this procurement**.

## Massachusetts foreign registration — verified official requirements

Klinikos, Inc. is a New York corporation and therefore uses the Massachusetts Foreign Corporation Certificate of Registration path.

Current Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth guidance states:

- registration fee: **$400**, or **$375 if filed by fax**;
- the filing must be accompanied by a certificate of legal existence or good standing from the corporation's jurisdiction of organization;
- the certificate of registration requires the corporation's exact legal name, Massachusetts transaction name if different, jurisdiction and date of incorporation, principal-office address, Massachusetts registered-office address, Massachusetts registered agent and written consent, fiscal year end, and a brief description of activities to be conducted in Massachusetts;
- foreign corporations authorized in Massachusetts must file annual reports; current on-time annual-report fee is $100 electronically / $125 otherwise.

Official authorities:
- Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth — Foreign Corporation Forms
- Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth — Corporations Division Filing Fees
- 950 CMR 113.48 / G.L. c.156D §15.03

## Separate Massachusetts DOR Certificate of Good Standing gate

Attachment P requires the bidder to obtain a current Certificate of Good Standing from the **Massachusetts Department of Revenue**, not merely a New York status certificate or Massachusetts Secretary of State registration certificate.

Current Massachusetts DOR guidance states:

- there is **no fee** to request a DOR Certificate of Good Standing / Tax Compliance;
- it may be requested through MassTaxConnect;
- applicants not already registered with MassTaxConnect can still use the public request flow;
- approval requires the taxpayer to have filed and fully paid applicable Massachusetts tax obligations;
- if approved, online requests are generally processed rapidly, while paper requests may take 4–6 weeks.

This certificate is therefore a separate submission dependency from foreign registration.

Do not certify tax compliance or create Massachusetts tax-return history that does not exist. Use the actual FEIN/TIN and current tax-account facts when the authorized person completes the request.
