# Klinikos Product Vision Canon

This document preserves the founder's broader product vision as a directional product canon. It does not claim that every feature is currently live. Delivery status must be determined from the current repository and production environment, not from this vision document.

## Core identity

Klinikos is intended to become the operating system for independent clinics, medical spas, and small healthcare organizations.

Core thesis:

**Small clinics need big-system control without big-system cost.**

Klinikos should help owners and teams know:
- who needs care
- who needs follow-up
- who needs paperwork
- who is ready to bill
- which staff member owns each task
- which appointment is at risk
- which lead is valuable
- which provider is available
- which contractor provider can take a service
- which room/chair/mobile location is available
- which service is making money
- where the clinic is leaking revenue

## Product domains

### Clinic command center
Owner, front desk, provider, admin and multi-location operational views.

### Patient/client management
Registry, search, profile, timeline, operational status, tags, assignments, documents and risk/priority signals.

### Intake/forms
Intake, medical history, consents, IDs, insurance cards, missing-document tracking, review queues, e-signing and service-specific forms.

### Scheduling
Calendar, appointment types, provider availability, room/resource availability, confirmations, no-shows, cancellations, waitlist/rescheduling, deposits and reminders.

### Follow-up
Missed calls, unbooked leads, consult follow-up, post-treatment, no-show recovery, reactivation, GLP-1/IV check-ins, maintenance reminders and overdue follow-up.

### Staff tasks
Assignment, due dates, patient/appointment/document/referral/result/billing linkage, escalation and daily checklists.

### Med Spa CRM
Lead tracking, service interest, consult pipeline, Botox/filler/weight-loss/IV/body-contouring/whitening workflows, packages, memberships, reactivation, before/after workflow, and lead-value prioritization.

### Revenue recovery
Missed-call leakage, unbooked leads, no-show loss, unpaid balances, incomplete plans, rebooking opportunities, recovery campaigns, and revenue by service/provider/location.

### Referrals/network
Referral status, partner directories, handoffs, documents, results and leakage tracking.

### Labs/results
Order tracking, pending/received/review/release queues, abnormal-result signaling, document storage and future external interfaces.

### Billing/insurance
Billing readiness, eligibility, CMS-1500 preparation, clearinghouse workflows, no-fault/workers' comp, claim status, denials, payment posting, balances, superbills and billing-document completeness.

### Payments
Online payments, links, deposits, packages, memberships, invoices, receipts, balances, processor integration and refunds.

### Patient portal
Appointments, forms, documents, messages, instructions, released results, balances, appointment requests and telemedicine access.

### Provider portal
Schedule, assigned patients/cases, notes, result/referral/task/document review, treatment plans and signatures.

### Clinical notes/charting
Visit/SOAP/procedure/weight-loss/no-fault/workers' comp notes, AI drafting, signatures, locking and audit history.

### Communications
SMS/email reminders, secure messaging, staff messages, templates, follow-up campaigns, no-show/review requests and pre/post-care instructions.

### Klinikos Intelligence
Workflow assistant, summaries, task suggestions, lead scoring, revenue-recovery suggestions, document summaries, reports and operational recommendations. AI is not the final clinical decision-maker.

### GRID contractor/provider network
Provider profiles, availability, on-call state, experience/pricing tiers, licenses, certifications, malpractice, resumes, travel radius, location preferences, approval state, agreements, ratings/internal notes, room/chair/mobile/partner-location models, booking requests, payouts, platform/location fees, inventory/product cost and revenue splits.

### Inventory
Injectables/IV/supplies, lots, expiration, assignment, usage, low-stock, value and waste/loss tracking.

### Admin/settings
Users, permissions, locations, services, providers, facilities, forms, templates, billing, integrations and branding.

### Compliance/security
RBAC, audit logs, MFA, encryption, backups, BAA tracking, readiness checklists, retention, incident response, access review and demo-data policy.

### Integrations
EHR/PMS, calendars, payments, SMS/email/voice, labs/radiology, clearinghouse/eligibility, telemedicine, website forms and other approved services.

### Reporting/analytics
Daily operations, follow-up, leads, revenue leakage, appointments, staff, providers, service revenue, marketing source and clinic health.

### Case rooms
Patient, no-fault, workers' comp, referral, med-spa treatment, document, billing and partner-handoff workspaces.

### Demo/sales
Synthetic demo clinic, demo patients/leads/reports, reset workflows, sales collateral, pricing, pilot proposal, NDA, SOW and invoicing.

## Truthfulness rule

Product vision and delivery status are separate.

Every route, feature and sales claim must identify whether it is:
- Live
- Demo
- Foundation
- Manual fallback
- Pending connection
- Vendor blocked
- Requires production review
- Human review required

No sales copy should convert roadmap intent into a live claim.
