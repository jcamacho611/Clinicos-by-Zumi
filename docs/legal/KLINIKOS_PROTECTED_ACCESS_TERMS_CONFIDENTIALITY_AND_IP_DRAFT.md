# KLINIKOS PROTECTED ACCESS TERMS, CONFIDENTIALITY, IP, TRADE SECRET & RESTRICTED USE AGREEMENT

Version: `2026-08-27.1`
Status: `HIGH-PROTECTION DRAFT FOR LICENSED-COUNSEL REVIEW - NOT PRODUCTION-APPROVED`
Canonical product requirement: `KLINIKOS-UX-001` and Section 26 of `docs/KLINIKOS_MASTER_CANON.md`

This draft is the intended **combined first protected-access execution package** for Klinikos protected interactive access. It is designed to put baseline use restrictions, confidentiality, intellectual property, trade-secret protection, restricted-use obligations, security rules, breach consequences, remedies, and electronic execution evidence into one affirmative agreement instead of relying on a weak footer Terms link or passive browsewrap.

This is a product/legal drafting package, not legal advice and not a claim of universal enforceability. Final production language must be reviewed by licensed counsel for the contracting entity, governing law, audience, jurisdiction, and relationship.

---

## 1. Parties, scope, and affirmative acceptance

This Protected Access Terms, Confidentiality, Intellectual Property, Trade Secret & Restricted Use Agreement (the "Agreement") is between the Klinikos entity identified in the execution record ("Klinikos") and the individual or organization identified by the signer ("Recipient" or "User").

The Agreement applies to access to protected interactive Klinikos experiences, non-public demos, evaluations, partner/investor materials, data rooms, unreleased features, technical materials, restricted product functionality, and other protected resources identified by the execution context.

Access is conditioned on affirmative electronic assent through the server-controlled acceptance flow. Viewing a public page alone does not constitute protected-access acceptance.

A signer purporting to bind an organization represents that the signer has actual authority to do so. A title, email domain, user role, or self-description alone does not establish that authority.

## 2. Permitted purpose and limited access license

Klinikos grants only a limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable access right for the specific authorized purpose, such as evaluation, use of the product, procurement, partnership discussion, implementation, pilot, investment diligence, education, or other identified purpose.

No disclosure, payment, access, possession, account creation, invitation, or participation grants ownership or any broader license by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.

All rights not expressly granted are reserved.

## 3. Ownership and protected subject matter

Klinikos and its licensors retain all right, title, and interest in and to Klinikos, including applicable software, workflows, interfaces, designs, data models, schemas, APIs, documentation, inventions, trademarks, copyrights, patentable subject matter, trade secrets, know-how, algorithms, methods, business systems, and other proprietary materials.

Protected Information may include, without limitation:

- source/object code, repositories, schemas, migrations, APIs, infrastructure, and internal tooling;
- security architecture, controls, heuristics, credentials, access patterns, and incident methods;
- data models, ontologies, taxonomies, event models, policy systems, rules, and scoring systems;
- Zumi prompts, hidden instructions, orchestration, routing, memory, evaluation, model-selection, safety, and tool logic;
- Grid eligibility, ranking, matching, trust, verification, transaction, pricing, anti-fraud, and anti-gaming logic;
- clinical, operational, configuration, revenue, education, workforce, implementation, and integration methods;
- private interfaces, prototypes, screenshots, product designs, unreleased features, and interaction patterns;
- roadmaps, research, specifications, tests, inventions, discoveries, and know-how;
- pricing, discounting, margins, costs, forecasts, fundraising materials, and internal financial analysis;
- customer, prospect, investor, provider, institution, partner, vendor, referral, and network information not intended for public disclosure;
- non-public negotiations, opportunities, introductions, contracts, strategies, distribution plans, and commercialization information.

## 4. Confidentiality duty

Recipient shall protect Protected Information using at least reasonable care and no less care than Recipient uses for comparably sensitive information of its own.

Recipient may use Protected Information only for the Permitted Purpose and may disclose it only to personnel or professional advisers who genuinely need it for that purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties reasonably sufficient to protect it.

Recipient remains responsible to the extent permitted by law and contract for prohibited disclosure Recipient knowingly enables or directs.

## 5. Standard exclusions

Protected Information does not include information Recipient can demonstrate by contemporaneous evidence:

1. was lawfully known without confidentiality restriction before disclosure by Klinikos;
2. was independently developed without use of or reference to Protected Information;
3. was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty;
4. became public through no breach of duty; or
5. must be disclosed by valid legal process, subject to the compelled-disclosure provisions below where legally permitted.

## 6. No copying, scraping, harvesting, mirroring, or unauthorized recording

Except to the extent expressly authorized in writing or protected by non-waivable law, Recipient shall not:

- copy, reproduce, mirror, frame, archive, republish, redistribute, or commercially reproduce Protected Information;
- scrape, crawl, harvest, bulk extract, enumerate, or systematically record restricted Klinikos content;
- create a substitute database from restricted content;
- record protected demos or sessions without authorization;
- export or retain protected material beyond the Permitted Purpose or authorized retention period;
- use automated agents, bots, proxies, distributed systems, synthetic identities, or alternate accounts to accomplish conduct prohibited if done manually.

## 7. Reverse engineering, reconstruction, prompt extraction, and competitive replication

Except where non-waivable law expressly permits otherwise, Recipient shall not:

- decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or attempt to derive protected source code;
- reconstruct private protocols, non-public APIs, internal architecture, rules, or data models from protected access;
- enumerate protected endpoints or defeat feature, tenant, rate, legal, security, or access controls;
- extract or attempt to extract Zumi system prompts, hidden instructions, model routing, memory architecture, orchestration, evaluation, safety, or tool-selection logic;
- reconstruct Grid ranking, matching, eligibility, trust, risk, verification, anti-fraud, or anti-gaming systems;
- use measurements, recordings, screenshots, exports, or protected product behavior to create a substitutive commercial reproduction.

## 8. No unauthorized AI ingestion, training, fine-tuning, or replication benchmarking

Recipient shall not submit Protected Information to third-party AI, machine-learning, model-training, embedding, indexing, retrieval, or data-mining services unless Klinikos expressly authorizes that use for the applicable data class.

Recipient shall not use Protected Information to train, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark for replication, validate, improve, or accelerate a competing or substitutive model, software product, workflow, marketplace, network, or healthcare operating platform.

## 9. Competitive use restriction tied to Protected Information

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits lawful independent competition.

Recipient may not use Protected Information to build, finance, advise, validate, market, procure, accelerate, position, or materially benefit a competing or substitutive product or service.

Genuinely independent development without use of Protected Information remains permitted.

## 10. Intellectual-property claims and misappropriation

Recipient shall not knowingly file, prosecute, acquire, or assist another person in obtaining intellectual-property rights that misappropriate Protected Information or falsely claim ownership of protected Klinikos subject matter.

This does not restrict Recipient from protecting independently developed intellectual property.

## 11. Protected introductions and bounded anti-circumvention

Where Klinikos introduces Recipient through protected access to a specifically identifiable non-public customer, investor, provider, institution, vendor, partner, procurement path, referral source, or commercial opportunity, Recipient shall not intentionally bypass Klinikos to appropriate that protected opportunity in violation of the applicable signed transaction/participation agreement.

This does not apply to relationships demonstrably pre-existing before the Klinikos introduction, opportunities independently sourced without Protected Information, or public-market relationships not introduced through Klinikos.

Any fixed duration, economic consequence, non-solicitation, or broader anti-circumvention provision must be stated in the applicable counsel-approved execution version or specific transaction agreement.

## 12. No indirect-action loophole

To the extent permitted by applicable law and subject to required intent standards, prohibited conduct remains prohibited when Recipient directly or indirectly attempts, requests, directs, induces, finances, facilitates, assists, enables, conspires in, or knowingly benefits from that conduct.

Recipient may not evade this Agreement by acting through an employee, contractor, consultant, affiliate, controlled entity, automated system, alternate account, or other intermediary.

## 13. Security and account restrictions

Recipient shall not:

- share credentials, tokens, private links, or invitations with unauthorized persons;
- bypass authentication, MFA, authorization, legal gates, rate limits, tenant restrictions, or technical controls;
- exploit vulnerabilities outside an authorized security-research program;
- introduce malicious code or destructive payloads;
- access another user's or organization's protected data without authorization;
- retain or reuse access after revocation;
- knowingly traffic in compromised credentials or protected access mechanisms.

## 14. Acceptable use and prohibited harm

Recipient shall not use Klinikos to:

- violate applicable law;
- impersonate another person or organization;
- interfere with service availability or security;
- upload malicious code;
- abuse patients, users, workers, providers, organizations, or counterparties;
- submit unlawful or unauthorized sensitive data;
- attempt to cause Klinikos or another party to violate privacy, healthcare, employment, financial, intellectual-property, or security obligations;
- use product access to facilitate fraud, theft, misappropriation, unauthorized surveillance, or unlawful discrimination.

Product-specific acceptable-use rules may impose additional restrictions.

## 15. Data, privacy, and healthcare boundaries

Protected access does not grant permission to upload PHI, patient information, regulated data, third-party confidential data, or other sensitive information unless the applicable product environment, contract, authorization, BAA/DPA where required, and operational controls permit that data class.

Recipient is responsible for ensuring Recipient has authority to provide data to Klinikos.

Klinikos may apply minimum-necessary access, tenant isolation, role/purpose controls, retention, audit, redaction, and external egress rules.

This Agreement does not itself constitute a BAA, DPA, patient consent, clinical authorization, employment authorization, payer authorization, or professional credential.

## 16. AI / Zumi boundary

Zumi may assist with interpretation, explanation, preparation, and orchestration. Zumi is not independent authority for clinical decisions, credentials, patient release, tenant access, payment, settlement, legal acceptance, or other regulated effects.

Recipient shall not represent model output as verified Klinikos domain truth when the relevant domain has not established it.

## 17. Access suspension, termination, and protective measures

Where Klinikos reasonably suspects material breach, credential compromise, security incident, fraud, unlawful access, abuse, or misuse of Protected Information, Klinikos may take proportionate protective measures, including:

- session suspension,
- credential/token/invitation revocation,
- access restriction,
- protected-download disablement,
- data-room closure,
- temporary blocking,
- preservation of relevant evidence,
- investigation and incident-response action.

Serious security, fraud, confidentiality, intellectual-property, or trade-secret risk may justify immediate protective action without a cure period where law and contract permit.

## 18. Breach classifications

### Class I - Material Breach

May include unauthorized recording/export, credential sharing, prohibited automation, misuse outside the Permitted Purpose, or failure to return/delete protected materials under a valid requirement.

### Class II - Serious Confidentiality / IP Breach

May include unauthorized disclosure of non-public architecture, systematic extraction, unauthorized AI ingestion/training, competitive use of Protected Information, protected-opportunity circumvention, derivative commercial exploitation, or facilitation of another party's prohibited use.

### Class III - Severe Protected-Asset Breach

May include intentional or reckless:

- trade-secret theft or misappropriation;
- unauthorized source/repository acquisition or exploitation;
- deliberate authentication, authorization, tenant, security, or legal-gate bypass;
- mass extraction, exfiltration, scraping, mirroring, or harvesting;
- extraction/exploitation of hidden prompts, orchestration, routing, ranking, matching, eligibility, security, or anti-abuse logic;
- credential/token/key/session compromise or trafficking;
- malicious concealment, alteration, falsification, or destruction of evidence;
- organized, financed, directed, or coordinated breach;
- deliberate use of Protected Information to materially accelerate a competitor;
- material compromise of protected systems, data, confidentiality, or trade-secret secrecy.

Classification does not eliminate any element, burden of proof, causation, intent standard, defense, or remedy limitation required by law.

## 19. Evidence preservation and non-spoliation

Once Recipient knows or reasonably should know of a suspected material breach, valid preservation demand, legal hold, or security incident, Recipient shall not knowingly destroy, conceal, falsify, or materially alter relevant evidence in violation of applicable law or a valid preservation obligation.

Nothing here requires unlawful retention or overrides mandatory privacy, employment, or records law.

## 20. Return, deletion, destruction, and certification

Upon termination of protected access or a valid written demand, Recipient shall cease unauthorized use and, subject to law, legal holds, ordinary backup systems, and required retention, return, delete, or destroy Protected Information as required by the applicable agreement.

For high-risk confidential relationships, Klinikos may request reasonable written certification of required destruction.

## 21. Remedies, damages, enforcement costs, and no-double-recovery

Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure, theft, extraction, misuse, security compromise, or commercial exploitation may cause harm difficult to fully compensate with money alone.

Subject to applicable law and the final counsel-approved execution version, Klinikos may seek or preserve rights to:

- temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive/equitable relief;
- specific performance where available;
- proven actual damages;
- restitution, unjust enrichment, and disgorgement where available;
- applicable statutory, copyright, patent, trademark, computer-access, and trade-secret remedies;
- reasonable forensic investigation, containment, credential rotation, restoration, remediation, required notification, and incident-response costs caused by breach where legally recoverable;
- attorneys' fees, expert fees, court costs, and enforcement expenses where the Agreement and applicable law permit;
- termination of evaluation, access, license, Grid, partner, investor, data-room, or other protected rights;
- referral to appropriate authorities where legally appropriate or required.

Remedies are cumulative only to the extent legally permitted. No party may obtain double recovery for the same injury.

## 22. Liquidated damages - counsel gate only

This draft intentionally does **not** activate an arbitrary punitive $25,000, $50,000, $75,000, or other preset fine.

A liquidated-damages clause may be considered only for a specifically defined breach where damages are difficult to estimate at contracting and the amount is a reasonable estimate of anticipated harm rather than punishment. The trigger, amount, no-double-recovery treatment, and governing-law fit require licensed-counsel approval before production use.

## 23. Indemnity and liability allocation - relationship-specific counsel gate

The final execution version may include indemnification for defined third-party claims, misuse, unlawful data submission, IP misuse, security incidents, or breach where appropriate to the audience and governing law.

The final version may also define exclusions of damages, liability caps, carve-outs from caps, and uncapped categories for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality, IP misappropriation, security breach, or other high-risk conduct where legally appropriate.

No generic cap or indemnity formula is activated by this draft. These are counsel-reviewed relationship-specific terms because consumer, enterprise, healthcare, employment, marketplace, and investor contexts differ materially.

## 24. Attorneys' fees and enforcement expenses

Where legally permitted and expressly included in the final execution version, the prevailing or enforcing party may recover reasonable attorneys' fees, experts, and enforcement expenses in the circumstances defined by that version.

Fee shifting is not assumed universally and requires governing-law review.

## 25. Compelled disclosure and protected reporting

If Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Protected Information, Recipient may make the disclosure required by law. Where legally permitted, Recipient should provide reasonable advance notice and cooperation so Klinikos may seek sealing, confidential treatment, or protective relief.

Nothing prohibits lawful confidential reporting of suspected violations of law to government officials, regulators, law enforcement, or attorneys, or other disclosure protected by non-waivable law.

## 26. Defend Trade Secrets Act notice

For covered employment, contractor, or consultant agreements governing confidential information or trade secrets, the final agreement must include or lawfully cross-reference the immunity notice required by 18 U.S.C. §1833(b).

The required protection includes qualifying confidential disclosures to government officials or an attorney for reporting/investigating suspected law violations, and qualifying sealed court filings. The exact statutory notice or an approved compliant cross-reference should be used in covered worker agreements.

Material revisions require a new version and renewed assent where applicable.

Electronic execution must be implemented consistently with applicable E-SIGN law, including applicable consumer-consent and statutory exceptions where relevant.

## 28. No transfer, sublicensing, or credential delegation

Protected access may not be assigned, sublicensed, resold, transferred, or delegated to an unauthorized person except as expressly permitted by the applicable agreement or mandatory law.

Klinikos may assign the Agreement in connection with a lawful corporate reorganization, financing, merger, acquisition, or transfer of the relevant business/assets, subject to applicable law and the final agreement.

## 29. Term, survival, and supersession

The Agreement begins on valid execution and continues for the protected-access relationship unless superseded or terminated.

Trade-secret duties survive for as long as information legally remains a trade secret where applicable. Other confidentiality, IP ownership, misuse, payment, evidence-preservation, dispute, anti-circumvention, and remedy terms survive according to their nature and the final counsel-approved duration.

Historical executed versions are retained as evidence and are not silently overwritten.

## 30. Governing law, venue, arbitration, jury/class waivers

The production execution version must identify the governing law and dispute forum appropriate to the contracting entity and audience.

Arbitration, jury waiver, class waiver, forum-selection, and related dispute mechanics are not activated generically in this draft. They require counsel review for enforceability, consumer/business status, healthcare context, state law, and applicable federal law.

## 31. Severability, waiver, and precedence

If a provision is unenforceable, it should be enforced to the maximum lawful extent or severed as appropriate without invalidating the remainder.

Failure to enforce one breach does not automatically waive future enforcement.

A later signed MSA, SOW, DPA, BAA, Grid agreement, institution agreement, investor/partner agreement, employment/contractor agreement, or negotiated amendment may control an expressly conflicting subject according to its stated order of precedence.

## 32. No obligation to proceed

Protected access does not obligate either party to enter an investment, procurement, pilot, partnership, employment relationship, transaction, or other business arrangement.

## 33. No universal enforceability representation

Enforceability depends on jurisdiction, audience, bargaining context, relationship, subject matter, statutes, public policy, and facts.

Strong wording, repository inclusion, clickwrap implementation, or electronic signature does not itself guarantee enforcement.
