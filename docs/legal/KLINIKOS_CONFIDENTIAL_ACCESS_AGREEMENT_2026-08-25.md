# KLINIKOS CONFIDENTIAL ACCESS, EVALUATION, NON-USE, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND NON-CIRCUMVENTION AGREEMENT

**Version:** 2026-08-25.1  
**Status:** Counsel-review draft. Not production-approved.  
**Disclosing Party:** Klinikos, Inc., a New York corporation ("Klinikos")  
**Recipient:** The person or entity identified in the signature block or electronic acceptance record ("Recipient")  
**Purpose:** Evaluation of a possible investment, financing, commercial, strategic, employment, advisory, vendor, customer, licensing, acquisition, or other relationship with Klinikos (the "Purpose").

This Agreement is effective on the earlier of Recipient's signature, electronic acceptance, or first access to Protected Materials after Recipient has received conspicuous notice and affirmatively agreed to this Agreement (the "Effective Date"). In consideration of access to Protected Materials, Recipient agrees as follows.

## 1. Protected Materials and Confidential Information

"Protected Materials" means every non-public item made available by or for Klinikos, including pitch decks, data-room materials, demonstrations, recordings, screen shares, prototypes, product access, analyses, reports, roadmaps, pricing, financial materials, business plans, customer or prospect information, institutional and partner strategy, source code, object code, interfaces, schemas, models, prompts, agent instructions, orchestration, workflows, algorithms, scoring, ranking, matching, trust, safety, risk, fraud, authorization, credentialing, verification, security, infrastructure, architecture, designs, inventions, research, know-how, trade secrets, and any notes, extracts, summaries, screenshots, recordings, benchmarks, inferences, reconstructions, derivatives, or analyses reflecting them.

"Confidential Information" means Protected Materials and all other non-public information disclosed in any form, whether or not marked confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances. Confidential Information includes the existence and substance of discussions, access credentials and links, identities of non-public relationships and opportunities, and combinations of information whose components may individually be public.

Confidential Information excludes information Recipient proves by contemporaneous written records: (a) was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure; (b) becomes public without breach; (c) is received lawfully from a third party without confidentiality duty; or (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to Confidential Information. A general idea, residual memory, or unsubstantiated assertion is not proof of independent development.

## 2. Limited Evaluation Right

Klinikos grants Recipient a personal, limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose. No license or other right is granted by implication, estoppel, disclosure, access, feedback, or otherwise. Klinikos may withhold, watermark, personalize, monitor, suspend, or revoke access at any time.

## 3. Non-Disclosure and Need-to-Know Controls

Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care and no less care than it uses for its own similarly sensitive information. Recipient may disclose it only to employees, officers, directors, attorneys, accountants, and financing sources who strictly need it for the Purpose, are informed of its confidential nature, and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement ("Representatives"). Recipient is responsible for every act and omission of its Representatives.

Recipient shall not forward, publish, post, quote, summarize for outsiders, distribute, sell, sublicense, publicly discuss, or otherwise disclose Confidential Information without Klinikos's prior written consent. Recipient shall not share protected links, credentials, copies, screenshots, exports, recordings, or access tokens.

## 4. Strict Non-Use and Competitive Misappropriation Protection

Recipient shall not use Confidential Information, directly or indirectly, to: (a) design, build, improve, finance, advise, validate, market, procure, or accelerate a product or service competitive with or substitutive for any disclosed Klinikos capability; (b) recreate Klinikos workflows, combinations, methods, product strategy, business model implementation, data relationships, network strategy, or commercial sequencing; (c) file, support, or obtain intellectual-property rights derived from it; (d) solicit another person to perform a prohibited act; or (e) obtain any commercial, technical, strategic, or negotiating advantage outside the Purpose.

Nothing prohibits lawful independent competition based solely on information developed without use of Confidential Information. The restriction is a confidentiality and non-use covenant, not a general restraint on lawful trade.

## 5. Prohibited Technical and AI Uses

Without prior written consent, Recipient shall not: reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, decode, probe, scan, scrape, crawl, mirror, systematically benchmark, conduct model or prompt extraction, bypass access controls, enumerate endpoints, capture network traffic beyond ordinary browser operation, create unauthorized copies or derivatives, or test for vulnerabilities.

Recipient shall not submit Confidential Information to any public, shared, consumer, third-party, or externally trained artificial-intelligence system; use it to train, fine-tune, evaluate, ground, retrieve for, benchmark, distill, or improve any model or automated system; or permit a provider to retain it for training. Authorized enterprise tools may be used only with Klinikos's written approval and binding no-training, access-control, retention, and deletion protections.

## 6. Non-Circumvention of Protected Relationships

For twenty-four (24) months after each introduction or disclosure, Recipient shall not knowingly use Confidential Information to bypass Klinikos and directly exploit a non-public customer, prospect, investor, funder, workforce body, school, clinic, provider, vendor, channel, strategic partner, acquisition target, or opportunity first introduced or specifically identified through Protected Materials. This section does not restrict a relationship Recipient proves existed independently before the disclosure, ordinary market outreach not informed by Confidential Information, or activity Klinikos authorizes in writing.

## 7. Ownership, Feedback, and No Commitment

Klinikos and its licensors retain all rights in Confidential Information and all Klinikos intellectual property. No disclosure transfers ownership. Voluntary feedback may be used by Klinikos without restriction or compensation, provided Klinikos does not thereby acquire Recipient's pre-existing confidential information or intellectual property. Neither party is obligated to proceed with any transaction. Any transaction requires a separate definitive written agreement.

## 8. Accuracy; No Reliance; Securities and Forward-Looking Information

Protected Materials may contain estimates, assumptions, illustrative economics, forward-looking statements, prototypes, plans, or information subject to change. Except in a definitive agreement signed by Klinikos, materials are provided for evaluation without representation or warranty as to completeness or future results. Recipient must conduct independent diligence and may not treat a deck, demo, conversation, or access event as a securities offer, binding commitment, customer contract, production certification, regulatory approval, or guarantee.

## 9. Security, Incidents, and Minimum Retention

Recipient shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards; restrict access to authorized Representatives; and promptly notify Klinikos at legal@klinikos.io of any suspected loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, credential compromise, legal demand, or security incident. Recipient shall cooperate in containment, investigation, evidence preservation, remediation, and legally required notices. Recipient shall not make public statements about an incident without Klinikos's written approval except where law requires.

## 10. Compelled Disclosure and Protected Reporting

If legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall, to the extent lawful, provide prompt written notice, disclose only the minimum legally required, seek confidential treatment, and reasonably assist Klinikos in seeking protection. Nothing prohibits communications with government agencies, reporting possible legal violations, or conduct protected by whistleblower law. Under 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), an individual may disclose a trade secret confidentially to a government official or attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected legal violation, or in a sealed court filing, without trade-secret liability under applicable federal or state trade-secret law.

## 11. Return, Destruction, and Certification

Upon request or termination of access, Recipient shall immediately stop using Confidential Information and, within ten (10) business days, return or permanently destroy all copies within its custody or control and certify completion in writing. One archival copy may be retained only by counsel if required by law or bona fide compliance policy, remains inaccessible for business use, and stays protected under this Agreement. Routine backups need not be selectively altered if inaccessible in ordinary operations and deleted through normal cycles.

## 12. Term and Survival

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date. Confidentiality and non-use obligations continue for five (5) years after the last disclosure; trade-secret obligations continue for as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret; Sections concerning ownership, remedies, electronic evidence, dispute terms, and retained copies survive as their nature requires.

## 13. Remedies

Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which money damages are inadequate. Klinikos may seek temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief, specific performance, preservation orders, access termination, and all other available legal or equitable remedies, without limiting proof requirements imposed by law. The prevailing party in an action to enforce this Agreement may recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the extent permitted by law. No preset penalty or liquidated-damages amount applies unless separately negotiated and counsel-approved.

## 14. Electronic Assent and Evidence

Recipient consents to electronic records and signatures. A click labeled to communicate agreement, typed signature, electronic signature, or signed counterpart constitutes assent. Klinikos may retain and reproduce the exact agreement version, content hash, acceptance text, date and time, identity and organization supplied by Recipient, authenticated account or verified email, IP address where lawful, user agent, resource identifier, access event, and immutable acceptance ID. Recipient must be able to view, download, print, and retain the accepted version. Passive browsing alone is not assent.

## 15. Governing Law; Forum; Jury Waiver

New York law governs without regard to conflicts principles. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction, and each party consents to personal jurisdiction and venue there. TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY AND VOLUNTARILY WAIVES TRIAL BY JURY for disputes arising from this Agreement. Either party may seek emergency equitable relief in any court with jurisdiction to prevent imminent misuse or disclosure.

## 16. General Terms

This Agreement is the complete agreement concerning its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions on that subject, but does not reduce stricter obligations in another signed agreement. Amendments and waivers must be in a writing signed by Klinikos and Recipient, except Klinikos may issue a new version prospectively and require fresh assent before further access. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without Klinikos's written consent; Klinikos may assign it in connection with reorganization, financing, sale of equity or assets, or transfer of the protected business or intellectual property. If any provision is unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum lawful extent and the remainder remains effective. Waiver once is not waiver later. Notices may be delivered electronically. Headings are for convenience. Counterparts are effective.
