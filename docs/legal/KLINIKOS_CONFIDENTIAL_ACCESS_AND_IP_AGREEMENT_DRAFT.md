# KLINIKOS Confidential Access, Intellectual Property, Trade Secret & Restricted Use Agreement

Status: DRAFT FOR LICENSED-COUNSEL REVIEW. NOT PRODUCTION-APPROVED.
Version: 2026-08-27.1

This draft is intended for protected demos, investor or partner reviews, data rooms, private product access, technical evaluations, pre-release access, and other circumstances in which Klinikos, Inc. or another properly identified Klinikos contracting entity discloses non-public proprietary information.

## 1. Parties and acceptance

This Confidential Access, Intellectual Property, Trade Secret & Restricted Use Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between the Klinikos entity identified in the execution record ("Klinikos") and the individual or organization identified by the signer ("Recipient"). The Agreement becomes effective only through the applicable affirmative execution process or other signed counterpart.

If an individual signs solely in an individual capacity, only that individual is bound. A person purporting to sign for an organization represents that the person has actual authority to bind that organization. An email domain, title or application role does not alone establish authority.

## 2. Permitted purpose

Recipient may use Protected Information solely for the specific legitimate evaluation, negotiation, pilot, partnership, investment, procurement, implementation, diligence or other purpose for which Klinikos granted access (the "Permitted Purpose"). No other use is authorized by disclosure, viewing, payment, access, possession or participation.

## 3. Protected Information

"Protected Information" means Confidential Information, proprietary information, trade secrets, pre-release information and commercially sensitive information disclosed by or through Klinikos that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be non-public or confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

Protected Information may include, without limitation:

- source code, object code, repositories, schemas, migrations, APIs and infrastructure;
- credentials, access patterns, security architecture, controls, heuristics and internal tooling;
- algorithms, scoring systems, rules, ontologies, taxonomies, event models and data models;
- Zumi prompts, hidden instructions, orchestration, routing, memory, evaluation, model-selection and safety systems;
- Grid eligibility, ranking, matching, trust, verification, transaction, pricing, anti-fraud and anti-gaming logic;
- internal clinical, operational, configuration, revenue, education, workforce and implementation methods;
- private interfaces, prototypes, screenshots, product designs, unreleased features and interaction patterns;
- roadmaps, research, specifications, tests, inventions, discoveries and know-how;
- pricing, discounting, margins, costs, unit economics, forecasts, fundraising materials and internal financial analysis;
- customer, prospect, investor, provider, institution, partner, vendor, referral and network information not publicly available;
- non-public contracts, negotiations, opportunities, introductions and commercial relationships;
- confidential strategy, commercialization, distribution, sales and implementation information;
- other information qualifying for trade-secret, copyright, patent, trademark, contract or other legal protection.

## 4. Exclusions

Protected Information does not include information Recipient proves by contemporaneous written evidence:

1. was lawfully known to Recipient without confidentiality restriction before Klinikos disclosed it;
2. was independently developed without use of or reference to Protected Information;
3. was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty;
4. became public through no breach of this Agreement or another duty; or
5. must be disclosed by valid legal process, subject to Section 19 where legally permitted.

The burden of documenting an asserted exclusion remains with the party relying on that exclusion to the extent applicable law permits.

## 5. Confidentiality and safeguards

Recipient shall protect Protected Information using at least reasonable care and no less care than Recipient uses for comparably sensitive information of its own. Recipient may disclose Protected Information only to personnel or professional advisers who genuinely need it for the Permitted Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality duties reasonably sufficient to protect it.

Recipient is responsible, to the extent permitted by law and the applicable relationship, for breaches committed by persons to whom Recipient knowingly discloses Protected Information in violation of this Agreement.

## 6. No license; no transfer of ownership

Except for the narrow, revocable evaluation or access right expressly granted for the Permitted Purpose, no license, assignment, ownership interest, franchise, partnership, agency, distribution right, copyright license, patent license, trademark license or other intellectual-property right is granted by implication, estoppel, disclosure, payment, access or possession.

All rights not expressly granted are reserved.

## 7. Prohibited copying, extraction and reconstruction

Except to the extent expressly authorized in writing or protected by non-waivable law, Recipient shall not:

- copy, reproduce, mirror, frame, archive, republish or redistribute Protected Information;
- scrape, crawl, harvest, bulk-extract, systematically record or create a database from restricted Klinikos content;
- decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer or attempt to derive protected source code;
- reconstruct private protocols, internal APIs or system architecture from protected access;
- enumerate protected endpoints or defeat access, rate, feature or tenant controls;
- extract or attempt to extract system prompts, hidden instructions, models, routing, memory, evaluation or orchestration logic;
- extract or reconstruct Grid ranking, matching, eligibility, trust, verification or anti-abuse systems;
- use screenshots, recordings, exports or measurements to create a substitutive commercial reproduction;
- use automated agents, bots, proxies, distributed systems or synthetic identities to accomplish conduct that would be prohibited if done manually.

## 8. No unauthorized AI ingestion or training

Recipient shall not upload, submit or expose Protected Information to a third-party AI, machine-learning, model-training, embedding, indexing, retrieval or data-mining service unless Klinikos expressly authorizes that use in writing and the service is approved for the applicable data class.

Recipient shall not use Protected Information to train, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark for replication, improve or validate a competing or substitutive model, product or service.

## 9. Competitive-use restriction tied to Protected Information

Recipient may lawfully compete in the marketplace. This Agreement does not prohibit independent competition.

Recipient may not, however, use Protected Information to build, finance, advise, validate, market, procure, accelerate, position or otherwise materially benefit a competing or substitutive product, service, workflow, network, marketplace, AI system or healthcare operating platform.

Independent development is permitted only when it is genuinely independent and does not use Protected Information.

## 10. No unauthorized intellectual-property claims

Recipient shall not knowingly file, prosecute, acquire or assist another person in obtaining patent, copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property rights that misappropriate Protected Information or falsely claim ownership of protected Klinikos subject matter.

Nothing prevents Recipient from protecting Recipient's own independently developed intellectual property.

## 11. Anti-circumvention of protected introductions

Where Klinikos introduces Recipient to a specifically identifiable non-public customer, investor, provider, institution, vendor, partner, transaction, procurement path or other commercial opportunity through protected access, Recipient shall not intentionally bypass Klinikos for the purpose of appropriating that protected opportunity in violation of the applicable transaction or participation agreement.

This restriction does not apply to a relationship Recipient proves existed before the Klinikos introduction, an opportunity independently sourced without use of Protected Information, or a public-market relationship not introduced through Klinikos.

Any fixed duration, economic consequence or broader non-circumvention restriction must be stated in the applicable signed transaction agreement and reviewed for the governing law.

## 12. No indirect-action loophole

To the extent applicable law permits and subject to required intent standards, conduct prohibited by this Agreement remains prohibited when Recipient directly or indirectly attempts, requests, directs, induces, finances, facilitates, assists, enables, conspires in or knowingly benefits from that conduct.

Recipient may not avoid this Agreement by acting through an employee, contractor, consultant, affiliate, controlled entity, automated system, alternate account or other intermediary.

## 13. Security and access restrictions

Recipient shall not:

- share credentials, access tokens, private links or invitations with unauthorized persons;
- bypass authentication, MFA, authorization, legal gates, rate limits or tenant restrictions;
- exploit vulnerabilities outside a written authorized security-research program;
- intentionally introduce malware, destructive payloads or denial-of-service activity;
- access another organization's data or another person's protected account without authorization;
- retain access after revocation or knowingly use credentials known to be unauthorized.

## 14. Breach classifications

### 14.1 Material Breach

Material Breach may include unauthorized recording or export, credential sharing, prohibited automation, violation of restricted-use obligations, or failure to return or destroy protected material when validly required.

### 14.2 Serious Confidentiality / IP Breach

Serious Confidentiality / IP Breach may include unauthorized disclosure of non-public architecture, systematic extraction, unauthorized AI ingestion or training, competitive use of Protected Information, protected-opportunity circumvention, derivative commercial exploitation, or facilitation of another party's prohibited use.

### 14.3 Severe Protected-Asset Breach

A Severe Protected-Asset Breach includes intentional or reckless conduct such as:

- theft, misappropriation or commercial exploitation of a trade secret;
- unauthorized acquisition, possession, copying or exploitation of source code, repositories, credentials or protected system materials;
- deliberate authentication, authorization, legal-gate, security-control or tenant-boundary circumvention;
- mass extraction, exfiltration, scraping, mirroring or harvesting of protected material;
- extraction or commercial exploitation of hidden prompts, system instructions, orchestration, routing, ranking, matching, eligibility, security or anti-abuse logic;
- deliberate credential, token, invitation, key or session compromise or trafficking;
- malicious concealment, destruction, alteration or falsification of evidence;
- organized, financed, directed or coordinated breach involving multiple actors;
- deliberate use of Protected Information to materially accelerate a competing or substitutive product;
- conduct causing a material compromise of protected Klinikos systems, user data, Confidential Information or trade-secret secrecy.

Classification does not eliminate any element, burden of proof, causation requirement, intent requirement or defense required by applicable law.

## 15. Immediate protective measures

When Klinikos reasonably suspects a material breach, credential compromise, security incident, fraud, unlawful access or misuse of Protected Information, Klinikos may take proportionate protective measures including session suspension, credential or token revocation, access restriction, data-room closure, preservation of relevant evidence and temporary blocking while facts are investigated.

Serious security, fraud, confidentiality, intellectual-property or trade-secret risks may justify immediate protective action without a cure period where permitted by law and contract.

## 16. Return, deletion and destruction

Upon termination of protected access or a valid written demand, Recipient shall cease unauthorized use and, subject to applicable law, legal holds, ordinary disaster-recovery backups and other required retention, return, delete or destroy Protected Information as required by the applicable agreement.

For a high-risk confidential relationship, Klinikos may request a reasonable written certification that required destruction has been completed.

## 17. Evidence preservation

Once Recipient knows or reasonably should know of a suspected material breach, legal hold, security incident or valid preservation demand, Recipient shall not knowingly destroy, conceal, falsify or materially alter relevant evidence in violation of applicable law or a valid preservation obligation.

Nothing in this section requires unlawful retention or overrides mandatory privacy, employment or records law.

## 18. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure, theft, extraction, misuse or commercial exploitation of Protected Information may cause harm difficult to fully compensate with money alone.

Subject to applicable law, the affected party may seek temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief, specific performance where available, actual damages, restitution, unjust enrichment, disgorgement, and applicable statutory or intellectual-property remedies.

To the extent legally recoverable and caused by breach, recoverable losses may include reasonable forensic investigation, containment, credential rotation, restoration, remediation, notification, incident response, expert expenses, court costs, attorneys' fees and enforcement expenses where the Agreement and applicable law permit recovery.

Klinikos may terminate licenses, evaluation rights, data-room access, Grid privileges, partner access or other protected-access rights where permitted.

Remedies are cumulative only to the extent legally permitted. Klinikos may not obtain double recovery for the same injury.

## 19. Compelled disclosure and protected reporting

If Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Protected Information, Recipient may make the disclosure required by law. Where legally permitted, Recipient shall provide reasonable advance notice to Klinikos and reasonable cooperation so Klinikos may seek confidential treatment, sealing or other protective relief.

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits lawful confidential reporting of suspected violations of law to government officials, regulators, law enforcement or attorneys, or another disclosure protected by non-waivable whistleblower law.

### Defend Trade Secrets Act notice

NOTICE UNDER 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b): An individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under Federal or State trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a Federal, State or local government official, directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or for disclosing a trade secret in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding if the filing is made under seal. An individual filing a retaliation lawsuit for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to that individual's attorney and use it in the proceeding if documents containing the trade secret are filed under seal and the trade secret is not otherwise disclosed except pursuant to court order.

This notice is included because federal law applies the notice rule to covered employee agreements and defines employee for this purpose to include contractors and consultants. Counsel must confirm use for the actual relationship.

## 20. Liquidated damages

This draft does not impose an arbitrary punitive fine or automatic $25,000, $50,000, $75,000 or other preset amount.

Any liquidated-damages provision must be placed in the applicable signed high-risk agreement only after counsel determines that the defined breach presents loss difficult to estimate at contracting, the amount is a reasonable estimate of anticipated harm rather than punishment, the trigger and amount fit the governing law, and the provision prevents duplicative recovery.

## 21. No waiver of mandatory law

Nothing in this Agreement waives rights or restrictions that governing law makes non-waivable. If a provision conflicts with mandatory law, mandatory law controls to the extent of the conflict and the remainder should continue to the maximum lawful extent.

## 22. No representation of universal enforceability

The parties acknowledge that enforceability may depend on jurisdiction, user status, bargaining context, relationship, subject matter and applicable statutes. Repository inclusion, clickwrap implementation or use of strong language does not itself guarantee enforcement.

## 23. Term and survival

This Agreement begins on execution and remains effective for the protected-access relationship unless superseded or terminated.

Confidentiality obligations for information that qualifies as a trade secret survive for as long as the information legally remains a trade secret. Other confidentiality duties survive for the period established in the applicable final counsel-approved agreement.

IP ownership, accrued payment obligations, anti-circumvention duties for their applicable period, misuse restrictions, evidence-preservation obligations, dispute provisions and remedies survive to the extent their nature or express terms require and applicable law permits.

## 24. No obligation to proceed

Disclosure does not obligate Klinikos or Recipient to enter a transaction, investment, partnership, pilot, employment relationship, procurement or other commercial arrangement.

## 25. Governing law, forum and dispute mechanics

The final governing law, forum, arbitration, jury waiver, class waiver and dispute process must be supplied by the applicable counsel-approved execution version or negotiated agreement. This draft intentionally does not pretend one dispute mechanism is appropriate for every user or jurisdiction.

## 26. Entire agreement and precedence

This Agreement governs the protected confidentiality and restricted-use relationship it addresses. A signed MSA, NDA, order form, SOW, DPA, BAA, Grid agreement, investor data-room agreement, negotiated amendment or other specific agreement may control for an expressly conflicting subject according to its stated order of precedence.

## 27. Severability and waiver

If a provision is held unenforceable, it should be enforced to the maximum lawful extent or severed as applicable without invalidating the remainder. Failure to enforce a provision on one occasion does not automatically waive future enforcement.

## 28. Assignment

Recipient may not transfer protected access or assign confidentiality rights to an unauthorized party without permission except where law provides otherwise. Klinikos may assign the Agreement in connection with a lawful corporate reorganization, merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of the relevant business or assets, subject to applicable law and the applicable agreement.
