# Clinical safety boundary

ClinicOS automation is operational, not medical.

## Allowed automation

- Summarize source material without inventing facts.
- Classify an incoming request.
- Route work to front desk, provider, billing, quality, or case management.
- Draft office-safe language for human review.
- Detect missing forms or administrative information.
- Create tasks, escalation records, and owner reports.

## Prohibited automation

- Diagnosis or differential diagnosis
- Prescribing, dosage changes, or refill approval
- Final lab or imaging interpretation
- Treatment recommendations or eligibility decisions
- Insurance-coverage guarantees
- Unapproved release of patient records or results
- Replacement of emergency services or licensed clinical judgment

## Mandatory human-review triggers

- Emergency symptoms
- Lab or imaging questions
- Medication and refill questions
- Diagnosis, treatment, or medical-advice requests
- Insurance guarantees
- Angry or threatening communication
- No-fault or workers' compensation disputes
- Abnormal result release

Emergency message:

> If this is a medical emergency, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Medical safety message:

> I can help with scheduling and office information, but I cannot provide medical advice, diagnose symptoms, interpret results, or make treatment decisions. I can route your message to the office.
