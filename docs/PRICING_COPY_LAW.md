# Pricing copy law

## Say
- from $X when scope can increase
- included allowance
- customer-funded usage
- pending connection
- requires verification
- custom for enterprise/complex scope
- 100% credited toward qualifying next step when the stated terms apply

## Do not say unless technically and contractually true
- unlimited AI
- unlimited messaging
- free trial for live production
- all integrations included
- instant activation
- automated payouts
- no additional fees
- guaranteed savings/revenue
- HIPAA compliant as a blanket substitute for actual controls/agreements
- 10% on every Grid transaction

## Price hierarchy
1. Clinic Operating Analysis: $500.
2. Implementation Blueprint: $1,500.
3. Founding Clinic Implementation: from $8,000.
4. Core: $995/month.
5. Growth: $1,995/month.
6. Scale: $3,995/month.
7. Enterprise: custom.
8. Grid Basic: $0 entry where eligible.
9. Grid Pro tiers: Professional $39/month, Facility $99/month, Seller $49/month.
10. Grid transaction fee: describe 10% only as the launch/testing midpoint or standard configured fee where applicable; resource-class policy and legal review remain authoritative.

## Sales framing
Lead with cost of fragmentation, missed work, leakage, coordination burden, and consolidation. Price is the exchange for an operating outcome, not a comparison of feature counts.
