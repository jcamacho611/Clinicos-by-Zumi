# Klinikos Product Paths and Paid Access

## Purpose

Every feature, idea, screen, payment, and future partnership must belong to a defined Klinikos path.

No new idea should be added without answering:

1. Who is this for?
2. Which Klinikos path owns it?
3. Is it included, paid, or an add-on?
4. What does the user receive immediately after access is approved or paid?
5. What information can that user see?
6. What actions can that user take?
7. Where do they land after sign-up?

This document is the master product catalog for organizing growth.

---

## Path 1 — Klinikos Clinic

### For
Clinic owners, medical practices, medical spas, office managers, front desk staff, billing staff, and clinical teams.

### Entry
`/clinic`

### Initial paid products

#### Clinic Essentials
Includes:
- clinic command center
- patient/client registry
- scheduling
- daily staff work
- follow-up tracking
- intake and paperwork tracking
- basic reporting

#### Clinic Growth
Includes Clinic Essentials plus:
- lead tracking
- med-spa customer relationship management
- rebooking and reactivation
- revenue opportunity tracking
- campaigns and patient/client retention tools

#### Clinic Revenue
Includes Clinic Growth plus:
- Revenue Desk
- billing-readiness tracking
- insurance workflow tools as connected
- referral leakage tracking
- no-show and cancellation recovery
- unpaid balance worklists

#### Clinic Complete
Includes all active clinic modules purchased for the organization, subject to regulatory and integration availability.

### User lands on
Owner/clinic command center.

### Immediate outcome after paid access
The organization is created, the first owner account is activated, the selected clinic package is unlocked, onboarding begins, and the buyer can start entering or connecting clinic information immediately.

# Paid Access Law

Payment alone must never accidentally grant permissions that require professional verification, clinic authorization, or legal approval.

Paid access has two layers:

## 1. Purchased Access
What the user paid for.

Examples:
- Clinic Growth
- GRID Professional
- Student Access
- Multi-Location

## 2. Approved Access
What the user is allowed to do after required verification.

Examples:
- a nurse can pay for GRID Professional immediately but cannot accept a clinical opportunity until required credentials are approved;
- a clinic can buy Clinic Revenue immediately but insurance transactions remain unavailable until required payer/clearinghouse enrollment is connected;
- a student can purchase EDU immediately but an educator-controlled course may still require enrollment approval.

The interface must explain this in plain language.

# Instant Sign-Up Experience

The intended public experience at klinikos.io is:

1. User selects who they are.
2. Klinikos shows the appropriate products only.
3. User chooses a product or enters an invitation.
4. User creates/verifies an account.
5. User pays when the selected product is paid.
6. Klinikos records exactly what was purchased.
7. The account is created or linked to the correct organization/profile.
8. The purchased features become available immediately.
9. The user lands directly inside the purchased product.
10. Any feature requiring external verification clearly shows what remains before it can be used.

No buyer should pay and then arrive at a generic homepage that does not know what they purchased.

# Access Records

Every active account must resolve these questions before showing a protected feature:

- Who is this person?
- Which organization or personal profile are they acting under?
- What role do they have?
- What product(s) are active?
- What add-ons are active?
- What professional/organization approvals are complete?
- What location(s) can they access?
- Is the access active, trial, pending, suspended, expired, or canceled?

---

# Product Catalog Rule

Every sellable product must have one internal catalog record containing:

- product key
- public name
- audience/path
- description
- price or contract-required status
- billing period
- included capabilities
- usage limits
- optional add-ons
- landing destination
- required approvals
- status: available / invitation-only / coming soon

Prices must not be scattered across page components.

---

# Feature Tally Rule

Every new Klinikos idea must be recorded before it is built.

Required fields:

- idea / capability
- owning path
- intended user
- problem solved
- revenue model
- dependencies
- current status
- paid tier or add-on
- required external partner/integration
- legal/compliance review requirement
- launch priority

This becomes the master tally so Klinikos can grow without losing its structure.

# Cross-Path Shared Features

These can serve multiple paths but must not become separate products by accident:

- Klinikos Intelligence
- identity and sign-in
- payments
- messaging
- documents
- scheduling
- credential verification
- facilities
- analytics
- access control
- notifications
- search
- audit history

The user sees the feature through their own Klinikos path; the shared machinery remains behind the scenes.
