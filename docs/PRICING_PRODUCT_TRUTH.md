# Commercial product truth

Klinikos sells an operating relationship, not access to a pile of unfinished modules.

The public commercial promise should remain narrower than the internal capability graph:
- Core daily clinic operations.
- Growth into revenue, referrals/network, and automation.
- Scale into multi-location coordination, reporting, and integration complexity.
- Enterprise for institution/network-specific requirements.
- Grid as a separate network marketplace business line.
- Zumi as embedded operating intelligence with funded usage economics.

Roadmap capabilities must not be used to justify a current price as if already production-ready. Pricing can reserve the right to expand scope through add-ons or contract amendments as capabilities become real.
