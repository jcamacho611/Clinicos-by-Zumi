# Klinikos Grants & Incentives Live Control Register

**As of:** 2026-08-26

This register is the operating source of truth for non-dilutive capital, incentives, tax benefits, startup credits, workforce procurements, prizes, and no-equity accelerators. It separates cash from tax credits, reimbursements, and in-kind credits and prevents pipeline from being misreported as awarded capital.

## Operating rules

- **Completion outranks discovery.** Existing viable applications with nearer deadlines and confirmed fit must be advanced before adding speculative opportunities.
- Never fabricate eligibility, scientific results, revenue, customers, partnerships, matching funds, employee counts, addresses, certifications, or location presence.
- Never create a sham office or nominal state presence solely to qualify for an incentive.
- Do not submit anything requiring a binding certification, legal attestation, equity commitment, hard credit pull, fee, or materially unverified representation without authorized human action.
- Treat tax credits and reimbursements as contingent future value, not cash on hand.
- Treat cloud credits as in-kind operating-cost reduction, not revenue.
- Every opportunity must have a dated source, eligibility state, amount type, next action, and blocking fact.

## 50-state expansion-arbitrage method

For each state, score only programs that are currently open/rolling or have a concrete upcoming cycle. Each state entry must answer:

1. Is the benefit cash, refundable credit, nonrefundable credit, reimbursement, loan, guarantee, or in-kind credit?
2. What exact operating-presence rule applies: incorporation, employee, payroll, work-hour share, principal place of performance, incubator tenancy, customer contract, facility, or tax nexus?
3. What is the minimum credible cost of establishing that real presence?
4. What capital becomes available only after establishing it?
5. Does the incremental expected value exceed the operating cost and compliance burden?
6. Is the timing compatible with Klinikos's current cash position and federal-award stage?
7. What evidence proves compliance?

**Expansion rule:** no relocation or state presence is recommended unless expected incremental value materially exceeds the real cost and the required presence has independent operational value.

## Truth labels

Use exactly these states:

- `DISCOVERED` — source found, not yet eligibility-checked.
- `ELIGIBILITY CHECKING` — rules being verified or program-officer inquiry pending.
- `ELIGIBLE BY VERIFIED FACTS` — current known company facts satisfy published requirements.
- `DECISION READY` — all non-binding preparation complete; only an authorized signature/certification/payment/attestation remains.
- `SUBMITTED` — application actually transmitted.
- `AWARDED` — written award evidence received.
- `NOT PURSUING` — expected value is inferior to burden, cash requirement, or strategic fit.

Never use `SUBMITTED` or `AWARDED` without direct evidence.
