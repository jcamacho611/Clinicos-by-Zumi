## 1. Purpose

Project Klinikos into a complete Stripe/Billing/Connect commercial architecture without shrinking the company into a clinic SaaS pricing page and without turning every capability into a separate SKU.

The monetization system must map the approved Living Universe and exactly-five-plane architecture into a small number of coherent economic layers:

1. free network entry;
2. individual paid value;
3. organization software subscriptions;
4. module / premium add-ons;
5. implementation and optimization services;
6. customer-funded usage / infrastructure;
7. Grid transaction economics;
8. institutional / workforce contracts;
9. API / strategic partner economics;
10. enterprise / government quote-and-invoice contracts.

The objective is maximum legitimate lifetime value while preserving trust, liquidity, legal defensibility, and one-product coherence.

## 2. Non-negotiable laws

### 2.1 Never sell authority
The following are never directly buyable:

- identity truth;
- licensure;
- credentials;
- privileges;
- clinical authority;
- organization authority;
- patient disclosure rights;
- consent;
- PHI access;
- Grid eligibility;
- clinical signature;
- legal acceptance;
- payment truth itself;
- settlement truth.

Payment may fund a product, service, usage allowance, reservation, subscription, or commercial obligation. Payment never overrides eligibility, RBAC, tenant isolation, consent, clinical/safety, credential, privacy, jurisdiction, or evidence rules.

### 2.2 Do not charge merely to exist
Person identity, patient participation, basic professional presence, basic Grid listing/search/decline, and basic ecosystem participation should remain low-friction/free where safe. Network liquidity and trust are more valuable than toll-booth pricing at entry.

### 2.3 Do not create one SKU per feature
Care Circle, Handoff Receipts, explainable action provenance, universal unfinished-work projections, continuity cues, relationship memory, Outcome/Success Graph projections, and similar cross-product capabilities should normally increase the value of a plan or add-on rather than become arbitrary standalone charges.

### 2.4 Specialty = configuration, not product fork
Med spa, pain, behavioral health, PT, orthopedics, and other specialty experiences should be configured on one Klinikos platform. Commercially, specialty implementation may be sold as a configuration pack; it must not become separate permanent code forks.

## 13. Stripe rail architecture

### Rail A — Klinikos sells its own products/services
Use Stripe Checkout, Billing, Payment Links, Quotes, and Invoicing.

Examples:
- analysis;
- blueprint;
- implementation deposit/invoice;
- Core/Growth/Scale;
- paid professional tools;
- EDU;
- add-ons;
- usage packs;
- optimization services.

### Rail B — Clinic uses Klinikos to accept its own customer/patient payments
Use Stripe Connect as a **SaaS Platform / direct-charge** model where appropriate.

Principles:
- clinic/merchant remains merchant of record for its customer sale;
- connected-account readiness must be verified before payments;
- Stripe-managed loss/pricing is preferred for the SaaS direct-charge path where supported;
- Klinikos may only charge application/platform fees when the commercial/legal policy permits it;
- regulated professional-fee restrictions must not be bypassed by calling a charge a platform fee.

### Rail C — Grid multi-party marketplace transaction
For lawful marketplace/resource classes requiring one buyer payment and multiple recipients, prefer **Separate Charges and Transfers** rather than pretending a subscription/payment link can model the transaction.

Potential resource classes:
- room/space;
- equipment;
- nonclinical services;
- professional services;
- staffing;
- education;
- facility capacity;
- other approved resources.

Permanent law:
- no universal Grid percentage;
- hard eligibility before ranking;
- resource-class + jurisdiction policy decides fees;
- regulated clinical services require healthcare-law/payment-structure review before percentage economics;
- platform transaction risk and negative-balance exposure must be explicitly designed before launch.

### Rail D — Enterprise / government / procurement
Use Stripe Quotes + Invoicing where Stripe is an allowed payment rail, with contract/net terms as required.

Do not force government/enterprise buyers through a consumer payment link.

### Rail E — Customer-funded usage
Use prepaid allowance products plus server-side metering and evidence.

## 14. Grid fee strategy — attack value without killing liquidity

### 14.1 Professionals
- basic listing/search/decline: free;
- premium professional tooling: subscription;
- regulated work: do not assume a percentage of professional clinical fees;
- where lawful, fixed platform/service economics may be preferable to percentage professional-fee participation.

### 14.2 Employers / organizations
Potential monetization:
- Network subscription;
- posting/workforce tools;
- contracted recruiting/implementation services;
- resource-specific success/transaction fees where lawful.

### 14.3 Space / ordinary equipment / nonclinical capacity
This is the strongest early candidate for percentage transaction economics because the platform is monetizing capacity rather than a professional clinical fee.

A future policy experiment may test a one-sided or split platform fee, but no percentage becomes active until the resource-class policy is approved.

### 14.4 Clinical opportunity assembly
A patient/customer may experience one coherent checkout, while the economic ledger separates:
- professional service;
- authorized clinical entity/practice;
- product/resource obligation;
- room/facility;
- technology/platform;
- payment processing;
- other legitimate obligations.

Stripe orchestration must not collapse these legal/economic roles into one fictional seller.

## 15. Automatic payment vs automatic authority

Klinikos should automate payment collection aggressively where the offer is approved:

`CHECKOUT → PAYMENT → STRIPE EVENT → KLINIKOS EVIDENCE → RECONCILIATION → ENTITLEMENT / NEXT STEP`

But:

`PAYMENT ≠ PHI ACCESS`
`PAYMENT ≠ CLINICAL AUTHORITY`
`PAYMENT ≠ CREDENTIAL`
`PAYMENT ≠ GRID ELIGIBILITY`
`PAYMENT ≠ IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE`

Software entitlements may activate automatically when deterministic server policy allows it. Regulated authority remains separately governed.

## 16. Static Payment Links vs governed app Checkout

Public/shareable Payment Links are valuable for:
- direct sales follow-up;
- email/SMS offers;
- simple approved self-serve products;
- payment collection without a custom checkout page.

However, current recurring activation code expects a server-owned checkout intent/state. Static Payment Links therefore require a dedicated reconciliation bridge before they should be treated as equivalent to the governed app Checkout rail for automatic software entitlement activation.

Engineering requirement:
- map Payment Link / Checkout Session metadata to a server-owned commercial intent;
- idempotently create/recover the intent where safe;
- verify product, amount, currency, mode, customer/org binding, event signature, and payment state;
- activate only approved product entitlement;
- keep PHI/integration/regulated gates independent;
- preserve audit/evidence.

Until that bridge is verified, static subscription links may collect sandbox payments but must not be advertised as production entitlement automation.

## 17. Price/version governance

Every Stripe Price should have:
- stable `lookup_key` where appropriate;
- Klinikos product key;
- billing model;
- pricing version;
- lifecycle classification;
- visibility;
- effective-date/version in the governing server policy.

When price changes:
1. create a new Stripe Price;
2. move the stable lookup key deliberately if used;
3. retire/deactivate old price for new purchases;
4. preserve historical subscription/payment evidence;
5. update canonical server catalog;
6. test webhook/reconciliation behavior;
7. only then update public links/surfaces.

Do not mutate history into a new price generation.

## 18. Discount strategy

Do not spray public coupons.

Approved current discount logic:
- clinic annual commitment: 15% savings;
- Clinic Operating Analysis / Blueprint credit-forward rules where current server/business policy allows them.

Future discounts should be policy-based:
- annual commitment;
- contracted cohort volume;
- founding/design-partner agreement;
- multi-location enterprise negotiation;
- institutional procurement terms.

Avoid fake countdowns, fake scarcity, or uncontrolled promotion codes.

## 19. Tax strategy

Do not enable automatic tax collection merely because Stripe can calculate it.

Initial strategy:
1. classify products/services correctly;
2. monitor nexus/registration thresholds;
3. confirm actual tax obligations;
4. register where required;
5. enable Stripe Tax collection only for supported/registered jurisdictions;
6. preserve contract-specific tax treatment for government/enterprise buyers.

## 21. Unit-economics discipline

Targets are planning targets, not current facts.

- recurring software should trend toward software-like gross margins as scale improves;
- implementation/services should be scoped to avoid becoming unlimited custom consulting;
- variable vendor usage should be customer-funded or bounded before execution;
- Grid transaction fees must account for processing, refunds, disputes, payouts, fraud, support, and legal structure;
- no offer should be priced below attributable cost merely to create vanity revenue;
- no offer should capture all customer value merely because a model says it can.

## 22. What not to monetize yet

Do not create a direct Stripe SKU for:
- patient identity;
- caregiver authority;
- professional verification badge;
- license verification outcome;
- Grid eligibility;
- referral completion itself where fee rules are unclear;
- clinical order/prescription authority;
- raw PHI access;
- basic Handoff Receipt;
- basic explainability/provenance;
- internal operator access;
- investor/lender diligence.

## 25. Success criteria

This architecture succeeds when:
- every legitimate economic event in the Living Universe has a clear commercial owner;
- free participation remains liquid;
- customers can pay automatically for approved offers;
- recurring software expands naturally into add-ons/usage/enterprise;
- professionals can progress from free participant to paid power user to owner without duplicate identity;
- schools/workforce/government can buy institutionally without consumer checkout theater;
- Grid can monetize completed value without using one unsafe universal transaction percentage;
- Stripe Connect can eventually support clinic SaaS payments and marketplace flows without collapsing merchant/legal roles;
- payment evidence remains separate from authority and settlement truth;
- future products can be priced/tested without being falsely represented as implemented.
