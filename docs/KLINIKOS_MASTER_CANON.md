## 1. One-sentence definition

**Klinikos is a living healthcare operating network in which one persistent identity can learn, work, receive care, provide care, operate organizations, discover and exchange healthcare capacity, transact, build evidence, progress professionally, and expand into larger networks through role-aware spatial experiences orchestrated by Zumi.**

Klinikos is not reducible to an EHR, staffing marketplace, LMS, billing application, telemedicine product, patient portal, CRM, directory, or AI chatbot. Those are capabilities and operating surfaces inside one governed ecosystem.

## 5. Trust, entitlement, and authority are different axes

Trust progression may include:

`PUBLIC → ACCEPTED → ACCOUNT-BOUND → CLAIMED → RELATIONSHIP-LINKED → VERIFIED → AUTHORIZED FOR THIS ACTION`

These are not subscription levels.

Commercial entitlements may include:

- free participant;
- professional premium;
- Grid paid capability;
- EDU entitlement;
- advanced Zumi usage;
- organization subscription;
- Clinic OS;
- paid implementation;
- Network;
- Enterprise;
- transaction capability.

Money changes entitlement. It never manufactures identity, credential truth, clinical authority, organization ownership, or patient access.

A verified RN may still be unauthorized to open a specific patient.
A verified clinic owner does not automatically receive unrestricted chart access.
A paying organization is not automatically clinically authorized.

## 6. Claim → evidence → verification → entitlement → authority

Permanent law:

`CLAIM != VERIFIED FACT != ELIGIBILITY != ENTITLEMENT != AUTHORITY`

A statement like `I am an RN` becomes a claim.
A statement like `I own ABC Medical` becomes a relationship claim.

Consequential authority may require evidence such as licensure, credential status, organization verification, assignment, location privilege, supervision, consent, effective dates, and policy.

Zumi and external models may interpret claims but may never manufacture authority.

## 11. Zumi must understand real human language

Users may type badly, dictate, abbreviate, use slang, or omit formal terminology.

Starting experience:

`ENTRY SIGNAL + INTENT + MINIMUM IDENTITY + CLAIM + NECESSARY VERIFICATION + RELATIONSHIP + GOAL = STARTING EXPERIENCE`

Then:

`STARTING EXPERIENCE + NEW RELATIONSHIPS + NEW INTENTS + NEW EVIDENCE + NEW WORK + NEW ENTITLEMENTS + NEW AUTHORITY + CURRENT CONTEXT = CONTINUOUSLY EVOLVING KLINIKOS EXPERIENCE`

## 13. Login restores work, not a generic dashboard

Returning users follow:

`AUTHENTICATION → RESTORE SAFE CONTEXT → RECOMPUTE EXPERIENCE ENVELOPE → RESUME THE MOST RELEVANT WORKSPACE`

A user may redirect the environment through Zumi at any time, subject to authority.

## 16. Supreme screen law

Every screen must have:

## 45. Errors, empty states, blocked states

### Errors

Communicate:

- what happened;
- what was preserved;
- whether action completed;
- what can happen next.

### Empty

Explain what is absent and the best next step.

### Blocked

State the exact governed boundary in plain language.

Example:

`Your RN claim exists, but this opportunity requires an active verified NY license.`

Then provide the legitimate next action.

Never blur claim, verification, eligibility, entitlement, and authority.

## 48. Universal production wiring

`VISIBLE EXPERIENCE → USER ACTION/LANGUAGE → INPUT NORMALIZATION → IDENTITY → ACTIVE EXPERIENCE ENVELOPE → INTENT → CLAIMS/RELATIONSHIPS → AUTHORIZATION → ELIGIBILITY → ENTITLEMENT → DOMAIN ENGINE → REAL DATA → WORKFLOW/TRANSACTION → PERSISTENCE → EVENT → EVIDENCE → AUDIT → FINANCIAL STATE IF APPLICABLE → MEMORY/CONTINUITY → TRUTHFUL RESULT → NEXT BEST ACTION`

- Clinics opens as a sales brochure instead of operating diagnosis/value;
- long prose appears where an operating action belongs;
- content exists primarily to explain our architecture to the user;
- text disappears under or behind imagery on scroll;
- artwork overlays later sections;
- sticky visual layers collide with downstream content;
