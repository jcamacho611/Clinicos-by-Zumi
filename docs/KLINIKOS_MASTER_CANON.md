## 6.3 Patient identity, documents, relationships, and reconciliation

**CURRENT RECOGNITION PHOTO ≠ GOVERNMENT PHOTO IDENTIFICATION.** A current patient-recognition image may be captured or uploaded as a clinical/operational projection used to help the care team recognize the right person. A government photo-identification document is a separately governed identity-evidence artifact with its own capture/upload, source, verification, access, retention, expiration, replacement, and disclosure rules. Neither may silently overwrite the other, and raw identity documents must not be projected wherever a recognition image is sufficient.

**QUICK REGISTRATION ≠ COMPLETE REGISTRATION.** Quick registration captures the minimum safe identity, contact, encounter, consent/readiness, and duplicate-check inputs required for the immediate purpose, then creates explicit unfinished work. Complete registration resolves the governed demographic, relationship, document, consent, coverage/FinancialCase, directive, communication-preference, and other policy-required evidence without forcing duplicate entry or falsely blocking lawful urgent care.

The relationship model explicitly supports **NEXT OF KIN / SPOUSE / PARENT OR GUARDIAN / CAREGIVER / EMERGENCY CONTACT / HEALTHCARE PROXY / AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE** and other lawful relationship classes. Each class remains independently effective-dated, attributable, revocable where applicable, and scoped to its actual legal/operational meaning.

**RELATIONSHIP EXISTS ≠ CONTACT PERSON ≠ EMERGENCY CONTACT ≠ AUTHORIZED DISCLOSURE RECIPIENT ≠ HEALTHCARE PROXY ≠ GUARDIAN ≠ CHART ACCESS AUTHORITY.** Kinship and contact data are first-class effective-dated relationships, but relationship alone grants no chart access, consent authority, disclosure authority, or ability to act for a patient. Scope, purpose, legal basis, patient capacity, consent/directive state, dates, revocation, and audit remain explicit.

Patient identity also preserves versioned consent, advance-care/advance-directive state, immunization/vaccination evidence, identity assurance, and document provenance without converting a document upload into verified truth.

**PATIENT DUPLICATE MATCH / RECONCILIATION** is a governed human-review workflow. Candidate similarity is not an automatic merge. Klinikos must preserve both source identities, explain matching evidence, prevent cross-tenant leakage, require authorized resolution, maintain reversible linkage/merge provenance where practicable, and retain a durable audit of what changed.

## 6.4 Provider-context readiness and chart authority

Clinical readiness is contextual:

`PROVIDER × ORGANIZATION × LOCATION × SPECIALTY / PROFESSION × SERVICE / APPOINTMENT TYPE × PAYER / FINANCIAL CASE × CREDENTIAL / PRIVILEGE / SUPERVISION × SCHEDULE POLICY × JURISDICTION × TIME`.

A professional may be licensed but not privileged at a location, trained for one service but not another, insured for one activity but not another, or unenrolled with the payer/case required for billable work. License, credential, malpractice, supervision/collaboration, facility privilege, service scope, payer enrollment, organization relationship, availability, and location readiness are separate effective-dated truths.

**ADMIN AUTHORITY ≠ CHART AUTHORITY.** Organization administration, scheduling, billing, configuration, or ownership never automatically grants clinical-record access. Chart access requires the relevant care/operations relationship, purpose, assignment, minimum-necessary scope, and policy. Break-glass access, where legally and operationally permitted, requires explicit reason, heightened audit, notification/review, time limit, and post-event reconciliation.
