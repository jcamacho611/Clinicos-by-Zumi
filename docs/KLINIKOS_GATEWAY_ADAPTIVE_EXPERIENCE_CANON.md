Klinikos must become broader underneath while becoming simpler at the surface.

`INTENT → AUTHORIZED CONTEXT → RELEVANT TRUTH → NEXT USEFUL ACTION → OWNED COMPLETION`

## 2. Product category

Klinikos is not adequately described as an EHR, practice-management application, staffing marketplace, LMS, CRM, billing product, patient portal, directory, commerce marketplace, or AI assistant.

Klinikos is a unified healthcare operating, clinical, economic, professional, educational, capacity, transaction, and coordination network.

It should progressively absorb the useful outcomes now fragmented across EHRs, practice-management products, referral systems, revenue-cycle tools, directories, professional networks, staffing/work platforms, education systems, commerce systems, CRMs, scheduling systems, ambient AI, and enterprise workflow software without forcing the user to operate all of those categories separately.

The goal is not the largest menu. The goal is to make entire incumbent workflows unnecessary.

## 3. Layer Zero — public discovery to governed ecosystem

Klinikos has two fundamentally different surfaces:

### Public Klinikos

Public discovery remains visible and indexable where appropriate for:

- search/SEO;
- education about Klinikos;
- public trust/security information;
- public pricing information when intentionally published;
- public thought leadership/research;
- public Grid/EDU discovery where privacy and product policy allow;
- press, investors, prospects, referrals, and link sharing;
- bounded public Zumi guidance with no private clinic authority.

Public information is public. An agreement does not retroactively convert intentionally public information into a trade secret.

### Protected interactive Klinikos

`ENTER KLINIKOS` is the canonical governed door into non-public interactive product experiences.

Target sequence:

`PUBLIC DISCOVERY → ENTER KLINIKOS → PROTECTED ENTRY AGREEMENT → SERVER EVIDENCE → SIGNUP / LOGIN → IDENTITY BINDING → ZUMI → PERSONALIZED KLINIKOS`

Protected/private demo, authenticated Grid participation, professional workflows, Clinic OS, organization workspaces, private EDU workspaces, private product intelligence, and other restricted experiences must not gain alternate public bypass routes.

The patient portal remains a separate governed patient-access path and is not forced through a proprietary professional/evaluation confidentiality agreement merely to reach the patient's own authorized records.

## 4. Agreement and trust architecture

The entry agreement is a trust/control layer, not a magical secrecy mechanism.

Required protections are layered:

`PUBLIC DISCLOSURE CONTROL + CONTRACTUAL RESTRICTIONS + SERVER-SIDE SECRECY + AUTHORIZATION + MINIMUM-NECESSARY DTOs + AUDIT + ABUSE CONTROLS`

Anonymous entry acceptance may be recorded before authentication only when:

- the exact agreement key/version/hash is bound to the evidence;
- required acknowledgments are affirmative and not pre-selected;
- the review/presentation interaction is server-verifiable;
- browser local/session storage is never legal authority;
- the acceptance is short-lived for entry purposes;
- it is bound to an authenticated identity only after valid credential authentication;
- the system records the binding event;
- stale, mismatched, expired, or missing evidence fails closed when enforcement is on.

Relationship-specific agreements remain separate. Protected entry is not a BAA, DPA, MSA, patient consent, HIPAA authorization, professional verification, employment agreement, payment evidence, Grid fulfillment evidence, or organization authority.

Published legal versions are immutable. Material text changes require a new version.

## 5. One identity, many healthcare contexts

Klinikos must move toward one persistent person-level identity rather than forcing disconnected accounts as a person evolves.

Representative lifecycle:

`STUDENT → LEARNER → GRADUATE → PROFESSIONAL → EMPLOYEE → CONTRACTOR → PRECEPTOR → INDEPENDENT OPERATOR → PRACTICE OWNER → MULTI-LOCATION OWNER → EDUCATOR / EMPLOYER / NETWORK OPERATOR`

These states may overlap.

A person may simultaneously be an RN, an employee of one organization, a Grid participant, an EDU learner, a preceptor, and an owner elsewhere.

The user does not select one permanent persona. Klinikos determines the active governed context from identity, relationship, role, organization, location, profession, credential, privilege, purpose, and the task the person is trying to accomplish.

Do not create a fake organization merely to give an individual a free account.

## 6. Zumi first-run activation

After protected entry and authentication, the first product question is not a module picker.

It is:

> **What brings you to Klinikos?**

Zumi should progressively learn only the facts that unlock useful value.

Examples:

- `I own a clinic.`
- `I am an RN looking for weekend work.`
- `I am finishing nursing school.`
- `We keep losing referrals.`
- `Our billing is a mess.`
- `I have treatment rooms available Saturdays.`
- `I want to replace our current EHR.`
- `I need a specialist.`

Zumi converts ordinary language into candidate structured intent/context, but the user confirms consequential facts before persistence or action.

Zumi never invents credentials, experience, authority, verification, eligibility, clinical truth, payment, fulfillment, or organization ownership.

## 10. Current Visit is the clinical hero experience

Klinikos must target the practical clinical and operational outcomes expected from MDLand and Epic-class systems while delivering materially lower cognitive load.

Do not copy incumbent screens button-for-button.

Provider-facing target:

`PATIENT SNAPSHOT → WHAT CHANGED → STAFF HANDOFF → TODAY → CLINICAL → ASSESSMENT & PLAN → ORDERS & RESULTS → DOCUMENTATION & CODING → CLOSE VISIT`

The doctor should be able to answer quickly:

- who is this patient in the current encounter context;
- what changed;
- what staff already completed;
- what needs provider judgment;
- what orders/results/referrals matter now;
- what remains before the visit is legitimately complete;
- what must happen after the note.

The backend may involve many domains. The physician should not experience them as a wall of separate modules.

No raw patient information is used in public demos or design artifacts. The reference environment uses synthetic data.

Show EDU, simulation, assessment, instructor-reviewed evidence, placement, and transition into the same professional identity.

### Healthcare entrepreneur / seller

Show organization/profile, services, availability, discovery, lead flow, booking, payments where lawful, marketing, analytics, and operations.

The user should see their future Klinikos, not forty-seven modules.

## 20. Incumbent replacement doctrine

Primary clinical benchmarks include MDLand and Epic, with continuous comparison against athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, Oracle Health, NextGen, Elation, Canvas, Tebra, and other relevant systems.

Adjacent mechanism references may include Zocdoc, Abridge/Suki/Nabla, Airbnb, delivery networks, Shopify, Stripe, LinkedIn, Indeed/Upwork, and other products whose interaction/economic mechanisms can inform Klinikos.

Research rule:

For every important workflow ask:

- Who currently does this best?
- Why does it work?
- What do users hate?
- What outcome is the incumbent feature actually producing?
- How can Klinikos reproduce the complete useful outcome with fewer clicks/context switches?
- What handoff or completion state can Klinikos connect that the incumbent leaves fragmented?

Do not copy protected interfaces or implementation details. Learn from observable workflows, standards, public documentation, professional feedback, and lawful competitive research.

## 21. Completion doctrine

Klinikos should not mentally declare victory when a screen action occurs.

Representative universal pattern:

`SOURCE → INTENT / EVENT → DESTINATION → OWNER → ACKNOWLEDGMENT → EXPECTED NEXT STATE → DEADLINE → OUTCOME → RECONCILIATION`

Clinical example:

`ORDERED → TRANSMITTED → ACCEPTED → PERFORMED → RESULTED → REVIEWED → COMMUNICATED → CLOSED`

Revenue example:

`PERFORMED → DOCUMENTED → CODE EVIDENCE → CHARGE EXPECTED → CHARGE PRESENT → CLAIM READY → SENT → ACCEPTED → ADJUDICATED → PAID → RECONCILED`

Missing transitions become visible owned work.

## 23. Final product law
