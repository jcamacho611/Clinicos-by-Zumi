# Required reviews before regulated launch

Business/legal review required for:
- Grid percentage fees involving professional/clinical services.
- Fee splitting, corporate practice, anti-kickback, referral, marketplace, tax, and payout structures.
- Any revenue-recovery success fee.
- Refund/credit terms and annual commitments.
- BAA/PHI status of paid external providers.
- Claims, eligibility, lab, imaging, telemedicine, and other regulated connector commercial terms.

Engineering review required for:
- production deployment health
- recurring billing activation
- webhook idempotency
- usage reservation/reconciliation
- entitlement source of truth
- payout processor readiness
- checkout amount/product correctness
