### Pricing structures to model

1. Fixed implementation/configuration + authorized live cohort/session delivery + participant/material component + controlled travel.
2. Per-cohort/session delivery + smaller fixed program-management/reporting layer.
3. Per-participant pricing with explicit volume bands and minimum authorized cohort economics.
4. Instructor-day / facilitation-day pricing for in-person requirements.
5. Blended platform/content license only if it reflects actual supported product capability and procurement preference.

Do **not** represent a 50% deposit as standard government payment terms unless SCWDB expressly permits it. Do **not** assume 980 participants will be referred.

## Final evaluator gate

Before submission, a skeptical reviewer must be able to answer **yes** to all of the following:

- Is the bidder legally eligible to prime?
- Is the three-year experience requirement explicitly and credibly satisfied?
- Can the team deliver both services and all five occupational pathways?
- Can the team deliver live remote and requested in-person instruction?
- Are all representative materials attached?
- Is healthcare operational/nonclinical and bounded by human authority?
- Are non-healthcare pathways described as configurable only where that is the truth?
- Is pricing economically viable at low, expected, and high utilization without assuming 980 guaranteed participants?
- Are accessibility, data, security, reporting, and continuous-improvement responsibilities addressed without unsupported certifications?
- Can the program launch within 30 days of notice to proceed?
- Are every reference, past-performance claim, partnership, credential, certification, and outcome backed by evidence?
