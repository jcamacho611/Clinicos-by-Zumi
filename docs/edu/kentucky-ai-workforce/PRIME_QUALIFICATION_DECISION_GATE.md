### Q19 — recently formed legal entity / three-year experience

SCWDB states that it **may consider clearly documented relevant experience of key personnel, affiliated entities, or predecessor organizations** when evaluating the three-year experience requirement, especially where meaningful continuity of leadership, personnel, operations, or service delivery is demonstrated.

The proposal must clearly distinguish:

- work actually performed by Klinikos, Inc.;
- work performed by an individual key person;
- work performed by an affiliate; and
- work performed by a predecessor organization.

Prior work must not be attributed to Klinikos, Inc. if Klinikos did not actually perform it.

SCWDB will evaluate the totality of demonstrated experience **and** the current prime's organizational capacity. Prior individual/affiliate/predecessor experience does not relieve Klinikos of the need to prove current staffing, systems, financial capacity, management structure, and ability to perform the contract.

### Q20 — Appendix C references

SCWDB states that Appendix C references **may include relevant comparable engagements performed by key personnel, affiliated organizations, or predecessor entities** when those engagements are relied upon to demonstrate qualifications and experience of the proposed project team.

Every such reference must clearly identify:

1. the organization or individual that actually performed the work;
2. that party's relationship to Klinikos;
3. the role of proposed key personnel in the referenced engagement; and
4. the nature and scope of the comparable services provided.

Appendix C still requires three references for comparable work completed within the last five years. The buyer's response says the RFP does not expressly require all three referenced contracts to have been held directly by the current prime legal entity.

SCWDB reserves the right to weigh how attributable the experience is to the prime when evaluating organizational experience and capacity.

### Q21 — experienced subcontractor / teaming structure

SCWDB states that a respondent may use a teaming or subcontracting structure to demonstrate the experience and capacity of the proposed delivery team.

The experienced organization is **not necessarily required to serve as prime** solely because it supplies qualifying technical or instructional experience.

However, Klinikos as prime must still demonstrate sufficient:

- organizational capacity;
- administrative capacity;
- financial capacity;
- management capacity; and
- contract-management capacity.

Every subcontractor/teaming partner must be clearly identified with services, qualifications, and responsibility allocation. All subcontractors remain subject to SCWDB approval, and the prime remains responsible for performance, compliance, records, and payment.
