# SCWDB Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network — Prime Qualification Decision Gate

Status: **capture control / bid-no-bid gate.** This document does not establish SCWDB eligibility, award, past performance, or legal sufficiency. It exists to prevent Klinikos from signing a proposal that relies on an unsupported interpretation of the solicitation.

## Why this gate exists

SCWDB's August 21 written response states that an out-of-state U.S. for-profit corporation may serve as prime if it satisfies the RFP, including delivery, instructor availability, administrative and financial capacity, reporting, accessibility, data security, and applicable requirements. SCWDB also permits instructors, subcontractors, and subject-matter experts, while keeping the prime fully responsible for performance, compliance, records, quality, and payment.

The same response states that **the organization must have at least three years of relevant experience** in adult education, workforce training, career-readiness instruction, AI/digital-skills training, or closely related services.

Klinikos has asked SCWDB in writing whether that threshold may be satisfied through the documented combined experience of proposed key personnel and approved subcontractors, or whether the prime legal entity itself must independently possess three years of operating history. Until SCWDB answers or an addendum resolves the issue, the proposal must treat this as a qualification gate rather than a copywriting problem.

## Decision tree

### Outcome A — SCWDB confirms key-personnel / approved-subcontractor experience may satisfy the threshold

Klinikos may continue developing a prime response only if the final proposal contains a complete, verifiable evidence chain showing that the proposed delivery organization actually satisfies the requirement.

Required evidence before signature:

- named key personnel and any qualifying subcontractor(s);
- actual dates demonstrating at least the required duration of relevant work;
- description of adult-learning, workforce, career-readiness, AI/digital-skills, or closely related services actually performed;
- live-instruction/facilitation evidence where applicable;
- role on this contract and occupational pathway responsibility;
- resume or bio supported by source records;
- availability for live remote and requested in-person delivery;
- real reference contacts who can verify comparable work;
- written subcontractor/teaming scope if a third party is used;
- pricing that includes the true subcontractor and cash-flow cost;
- confirmation that Klinikos can administer records, QA, reporting, accessibility, security, invoicing, and subcontractor payment as prime.

**Do not convert an individual's years in a profession into training experience unless the underlying work actually included qualifying instruction, facilitation, implementation enablement, workforce services, or closely related delivery.**

### Outcome B — SCWDB confirms the prime legal entity itself must independently possess three years

Do not assert that Klinikos, Inc. satisfies the threshold merely because its founder or proposed personnel have older experience.

Before any prime submission, determine whether the procurement legally permits a documented predecessor/continuity theory and whether real evidence supports it. Do not invent predecessor status, corporate succession, contracts, customers, or operating history.

If no lawful documented path exists, the compliant options are:

1. participate as a disclosed subcontractor to a genuinely qualified prime;
2. use another expressly permitted legal/team structure only if SCWDB confirms it satisfies the RFP and the structure is real before submission; or
3. no-bid as prime.

**Do not sign a prime proposal that knowingly fails a stated minimum qualification.** Product strength cannot cure bidder ineligibility.

### Outcome C — SCWDB does not answer before the question deadline / remains ambiguous

Do not silently choose the interpretation most favorable to Klinikos.

Before final submission:

- review any September 4 addenda and posted Q&A;
- obtain procurement/legal review of the exact minimum-qualification wording;
- compare the proposed structure to the RFP's respondent, subcontractor, and reference requirements;
- choose the most defensible structure based on written procurement evidence.

If qualification remains materially uncertain, the proposal must not contain an unqualified statement that Klinikos meets the three-year requirement.

## Three-reference gate

The capture matrix currently treats at least three relevant comparable-work references within the required period as mandatory. Before proposal signature, every reference used must be real, current enough for the solicitation, and permissioned.

For each reference preserve:

- organization;
- reference name/title;
- verified email and phone where required;
- engagement dates;
- services actually delivered;
- population/users served when supportable;
- approximate value only when supportable and requested;
- direct relevance to this RFP;
- permission/availability to serve as a reference.

A personal character reference, academic contact, vendor, friend, or practitioner who cannot verify comparable delivered work does not satisfy a past-performance reference requirement merely because the person knows the founder.

## Administrative and financial-capacity gate

SCWDB expressly conditioned out-of-state prime eligibility on administrative and financial capacity. Before Klinikos represents that it can perform as prime, document at minimum:

- responsible contract administrator;
- invoicing/accounting process;
- instructor scheduling and backup coverage;
- subcontractor administration and payment responsibility;
- records/reporting ownership;
- accessibility review ownership;
- data-security ownership;
- realistic 30-day launch plan;
- ability to front implementation, instructor, travel, participant-material, and subcontractor costs under 30/45/60/90-day collection scenarios;
- actual working-capital source or other documented capacity relied upon.

Never represent expected contract revenue, an uncommitted investor, an unapproved credit line, speculative grant funding, or assumed participant volume as available working capital.

## Founder/team evidence still needed

The strongest remaining founder inputs are source documents, not a longer biography. Collect and verify:

- dated teaching, training, facilitation, mentoring, workshop, fellowship, implementation-enablement, or workforce/career-readiness engagements;
- AI or digital-skills instruction and enablement work;
- healthcare-operations training or implementation enablement;
- organizations/programs served and dates;
- duration and audience type/size where supportable;
- curricula, slides, certificates, contracts, emails, project records, platform records, recommendations, or other contemporaneous evidence;
- people who can verify the work as comparable references;
- actual instructor availability for Kentucky live remote and in-person delivery.

Use founder/team technical, AI, healthcare-operations, education, fellowship/project, business, and implementation experience aggressively **when it is relevant and documented**. Do not collapse all experience into company history, and do not understate real qualifying work merely because Klinikos, Inc. is newly formed.

## Signature gate

The proposal may state that the minimum experience/reference requirements are satisfied only when all applicable boxes below are supported by evidence:

- [ ] SCWDB/addendum interpretation of the three-year requirement is documented.
- [ ] Respondent structure matches that interpretation.
- [ ] Required experience duration is proven by dated records.
- [ ] Required comparable references are real, verified, and permissioned.
- [ ] Named instructors/SMEs have verified qualifications and availability.
- [ ] Any subcontractor/teaming relationship is real, disclosed, scoped, and priced.
- [ ] Klinikos can demonstrate administrative capacity as prime.
- [ ] Klinikos can demonstrate financial/working-capital capacity under non-guaranteed volume.
- [ ] No formation date, predecessor status, customer, contract, partnership, outcome, or award is invented or implied beyond evidence.

If any mandatory minimum remains unsupported at signature time, escalate to bid/no-bid review rather than trying to solve the gap with persuasive wording.
