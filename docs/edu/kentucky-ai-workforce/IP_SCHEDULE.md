# Klinikos EDU — Proposed Intellectual Property Schedule for SCWDB

Status: **contract-preparation working document, not legal advice and not an executed allocation of rights.** Final ownership, license, reuse, modification, statewide access, and post-contract rights are subject to the negotiated contract.

## Purpose

The SCWDB RFP states that ownership/license/reuse rights will be negotiated and the posted procurement Q&A indicates SCWDB does not intend to require transfer of pre-existing proprietary software or materials. The proposal and contract should clearly distinguish what Klinikos already owns from Kentucky-specific deliverables.

## A. Pre-existing Klinikos proprietary material

Subject to final legal review, identify as pre-existing proprietary Klinikos material to the extent actually developed before or independently of the SCWDB engagement:

- Klinikos EDU source code and application architecture;
- Virtual Clinic Lab architecture and synthetic healthcare simulation model;
- EDU authentication, authorization, institution/course/cohort access patterns;
- reusable curriculum package architecture;
- reusable workforce AI literacy framework and common instructional method;
- reusable assessment/rubric/completion architecture;
- reusable certificate-of-completion architecture and non-licensure safety model;
- reusable reporting/completion projection code;
- reusable accessibility, privacy, security, and human-AI governance methods;
- Klinikos design system, components, navigation patterns, and proprietary backend services;
- pre-existing Klinikos brand, trademarks, trade dress, internal prompts, proprietary algorithms, business logic, and confidential technical know-how;
- existing healthcare curriculum packages and role-based simulation content developed outside the Kentucky-specific engagement.

Pre-existing Klinikos IP should remain owned by Klinikos and be licensed only to the extent expressly negotiated for contract performance and authorized use.

## B. Third-party material

Any actual third-party platform, model, font, library, AI service, conferencing tool, accessibility/captioning service, credential/badge service, or other licensed material used in delivery must remain subject to its own license/terms.

The proposal should identify only third-party systems actually intended for delivery and should disclose relevant participant-access, privacy, retention, accessibility, and cost implications where required.

Do not promise assignment of third-party rights that Klinikos does not own.

## C. Kentucky / SCWDB-specific deliverables

Potential Kentucky-specific deliverables may include:

- SCWDB-approved final pathway curricula;
- Kentucky-specific lesson sequencing and examples;
- SCWDB-specific participant materials;
- SCWDB-specific instructor guides;
- SCWDB-specific assessment forms and scoring anchors;
- SCWDB-specific reporting layouts/data dictionaries;
- SCWDB-specific delivery calendar/templates;
- SCWDB-specific implementation/work-plan documents;
- SCWDB-specific branding/configuration;
- approved Kentucky workforce examples or employer-validated adaptations created specifically under the contract.

The final contract should state whether these deliverables are owned, licensed, jointly usable, or reusable, and should define modification/statewide/post-contract rights without unintentionally transferring the underlying pre-existing Klinikos platform or reusable methods.

## D. Configuration vs source-code transfer

Institution-specific configuration should not automatically imply transfer of:

- platform source code;
- underlying database architecture;
- reusable domain models;
- reusable simulation engines;
- security/authorization internals;
- proprietary AI prompts/orchestration;
- generic rubric engines;
- reusable reporting logic;
- proprietary trade secrets or internal operational methods.

If SCWDB requires continuity after contract end, negotiate a practical license to approved deliverables/data exports/documentation rather than an implied transfer of the entire Klinikos product.

## E. Proposal disclosure language to refine with counsel

> Klinikos intends to retain ownership of all pre-existing and independently developed software, platform architecture, reusable curriculum methods, simulation frameworks, assessment/reporting methods, source code, security controls, design systems, and other proprietary materials. SCWDB-specific deliverables developed under the contract will be identified separately, and appropriate license, reuse, modification, statewide access, and post-contract rights will be negotiated consistent with the RFP and final contract. Third-party materials remain subject to their applicable licenses.

## Contract review checklist

- [ ] Confirm the final agreement expressly defines pre-existing/background IP.
- [ ] Attach or incorporate a schedule of pre-existing Klinikos materials.
- [ ] Confirm Kentucky-specific deliverables are separately identified.
- [ ] Review work-made-for-hire/assignment language for accidental source-code transfer.
- [ ] Define SCWDB's operational continuity and statewide-use rights precisely.
- [ ] Define post-contract participant/data export rights and retention duties.
- [ ] Confirm rights to update/reuse generic improvements developed during performance.
- [ ] Identify all third-party licensed components and any pass-through restrictions.
- [ ] Protect confidential source code, prompts, algorithms, pricing/margin logic, credentials, and security architecture from unnecessary public disclosure.
- [ ] Reconcile confidentiality claims with Kentucky public-records requirements; do not assume a confidentiality label guarantees nondisclosure.
