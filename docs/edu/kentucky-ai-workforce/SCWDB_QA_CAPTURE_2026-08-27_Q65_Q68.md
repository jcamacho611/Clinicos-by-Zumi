## Q65 — Financial / Administrative Capacity Evidence

SCWDB states that the RFP does **not** require a specific mandatory financial-capacity attachment such as audited financial statements, a bank reference, or an operating budget.

The proposal must instead address capacity inside the **Organizational Experience and Capacity** section by describing:

- financial and administrative systems;
- staffing;
- federal-grant experience;
- relevant audit or litigation findings; and
- capacity to begin and administer the proposed services.

SCWDB specifically expects systems sufficient for federally funded contracting, documentation, invoicing, monitoring, and audit. Additional evidence may be included if useful, but is not specifically required unless another RFP provision says otherwise. SCWDB may request more information during evaluation or contracting.

### Execution consequence

Use `ORGANIZATIONAL_EXPERIENCE_AND_CAPACITY_NARRATIVE_2026-08-27.md` as the proposal base. Do **not** fabricate audited financials, bank references, revenue history, federal-award history, audit/litigation status, or working-capital facts. Optional financial exhibits should be included only if verified, useful, and disclosure-safe.

## Q66 — Insurance

SCWDB refers respondents to Question 50. Question 50 states that the selected contractor must maintain insurance acceptable to SCWDB, but the RFP does **not** establish specific coverage limits or require certificates of insurance at proposal submission.

### Execution consequence

Do not hold proposal completion for an insurance certificate or invented coverage limit. Disclose current legal/organizational status truthfully. Treat final insurance requirements as a post-selection / contracting item unless a later addendum changes this.

## Q67 — Invoicing and Payment Timing

SCWDB refers respondents to Question 36 regarding payment structure.

Question 36 confirms that SCWDB prefers clear, scalable pricing tied to verified deliverables and participant completion, and permits respondents to separately price appropriate scope components, including one-time curriculum development/customization/implementation/launch, per-completed-participant delivery, cohort/session pricing, in-person delivery, approved travel, and other appropriate components identified in the RFP.

Questions 16 and 22 confirm there is no guaranteed participant volume, class count, referral count, completion count, payment amount, contract ceiling, or target budget.

### Execution consequence

The current Appendix B architecture remains permissible, but **Q67 does not establish invoice cadence, payment terms, or a right to pre-completion milestone billing**. Do not represent Net 30, milestone prepayment, advance payment, or implementation-payment timing as buyer-approved. Keep payment timing as a contracting / negotiation risk and preserve sufficient working-capital controls.

## Q68 — Registrations

SCWDB refers respondents to Question 50.

Question 50 states:

- no separate Kentucky physical-presence requirement is identified for proposal submission;
- respondents must complete required assurances/certifications and be legally authorized to conduct business and provide the proposed services at proposal submission;
- applicable disclosures such as debarment, conflicts of interest, litigation/audit findings, and other required certifications apply;
- specific insurance requirements may be established during contract negotiation;
- subcontractors must be disclosed and approved in writing; and
- any required federal, state, or SCWDB approvals must be satisfied before notice to proceed.

### Execution consequence

Do **not** treat SAM/UEI, Kentucky vendor registration, or a Kentucky office as automatic proposal-submission blockers unless another explicit RFP/addendum provision requires them. Do verify legal authority, required assurances/certifications, and any applicable disclosures before signature. Track registrations that may be needed before contract execution or notice to proceed.
