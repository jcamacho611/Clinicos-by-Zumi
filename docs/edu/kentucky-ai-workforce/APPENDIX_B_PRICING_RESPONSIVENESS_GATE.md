# SCWDB Appendix B Pricing Responsiveness Gate

Status: **internal capture control — not proposal pricing.**

Purpose: prevent Klinikos from submitting a commercially reasonable pricing model that is nevertheless nonresponsive to SCWDB's required pricing format.

## Buyer truth locked from the posted Q&A

SCWDB's posted Q&A says Appendix B requests **per-completed-participant pricing**. SCWDB will establish and approve the final completion standard during implementation, and payment-grade completion must be supported by verified attendance and completion evidence. Registration or partial attendance alone is not enough for payment.

SCWDB has not guaranteed participant volume, completions, classes, referrals, or payments. Approximately 980 unique participants is a planning target only.

The current second-round bidder clarification asks whether implementation, curriculum configuration, accessibility/reporting setup, or similar launch work may be separately priced. Until SCWDB answers that question, a separate setup fee must not be assumed proposal-compliant.

## Submission gate

Do not finalize Appendix B until the September 4 addenda/Q&A and the pending pricing clarification are reconciled.

The final pricing schedule must use one of the following structures only after SCWDB's answer makes that structure permissible.

### Structure A — separate implementation line permitted

If SCWDB expressly permits a separately priced implementation/customization deliverable:

- price the approved one-time implementation/configuration work separately;
- provide a clear per-completed-participant rate for Career Readiness;
- provide a clear per-completed-participant rate for each Industry Accelerator pathway, or a common accelerator rate if justified;
- include ordinary required Klinikos EDU platform access and basic completion evidence in the participant rate unless SCWDB expressly permits separate pricing;
- identify travel and unusual in-person delivery costs only in the manner SCWDB allows;
- do not use a startup-style deposit unless SCWDB expressly permits one.

The existing internal `$48,000` implementation figure remains a **working scenario only** until staffing, accessibility, reporting, travel, qualification/teaming, insurance, and working-capital costs are verified.

### Structure B — Appendix B requires participant-only pricing

If SCWDB does **not** permit a separate implementation/setup line, the fixed launch burden must be recovered inside the per-completed-participant economics rather than placed on a nonresponsive extra line.

Use this internal formula for each service before setting a proposal rate:

`required unit rate = direct delivery cost per completion + participant-variable cost + allocated fixed implementation burden + reporting/QA burden + working-capital/risk margin`

Where:

`allocated fixed implementation burden = fixed launch cost allocated to that service / conservative billable-completion base`

Do not allocate fixed cost against the 980-person planning target as though 980 completions are guaranteed.

The conservative billable-completion base must be derived from low/expected/high scenarios and the likely mix between Career Readiness and Industry Accelerator completions.

## Fixed-cost sensitivity

Using the current internal `$48,000` setup scenario only to illustrate sensitivity:

| Billable completions used to absorb fixed setup | Fixed-cost burden per completion |
| --- | ---: |
| 245 | $195.92 |
| 490 | $97.96 |
| 980 | $48.98 |
| 1,960 | $24.49 |

These are **not proposed prices**. They demonstrate why the pricing model must distinguish unique participants from service completions and why a 980-participant planning target cannot be treated as guaranteed revenue.

If a participant completes both Career Readiness and one Industry Accelerator pathway, that may create two separately billable service completions only if Appendix B and the final SCWDB completion/invoicing rules treat them that way. Do not assume this until confirmed.

## Volume-tier gate

The current internal pricing recommendation uses completion-volume tiers. Do not place tiers into Appendix B unless the form/addenda permit tiered unit pricing or SCWDB confirms that approach.

If Appendix B requires one firm unit price per service, convert the internal low/expected/high economics into one defensible unit rate that survives the conservative completion scenario.

If tiered rates are allowed:

- make the thresholds objectively measurable;
- define whether tiers apply by contract-year, cumulative service completion, or pathway completion;
- avoid retroactive ambiguity;
- ensure the lowest tier remains financially performable without relying on future volume.

## In-person delivery gate

SCWDB expects live remote delivery to be routine and in-person delivery to be strategic. The proposal must state assumptions around class size, travel, minimum enrollment, cancellation, and other price conditions.

Do not automatically add `$3,500/day + travel` to Appendix B merely because it is the current internal scenario. Confirm whether SCWDB permits:

- a separate in-person instructor-day line;
- pre-approved reimbursable travel;
- a different per-completed-participant rate for in-person delivery; or
- one blended rate covering both modalities.

If none of those is expressly permitted, calculate the expected in-person burden inside the unit rate using a conservative frequency assumption.

## Completion-evidence economics

A completed participant is a payment event only after the approved evidence standard is satisfied. The pricing model therefore must include the cost of:

- attendance verification;
- required activities;
- assessments or demonstrations of learning;
- instructor review where required;
- completion determination;
- certificate evidence;
- participant-level records sufficient for invoicing, grant monitoring, and audit;
- correction/reconciliation of incomplete or disputed completion records.

Do not model completion evidence as a zero-cost administrative afterthought.

## Working-capital gate

Because SCWDB guarantees no minimum completion volume, unit pricing must remain performable under delayed payment and low utilization.

Before final rates are approved, stress-test at minimum:

- low, expected, and high service-completion volume;
- 30-, 45-, 60-, and 90-day collection timing;
- remote-first and higher-than-expected in-person mixes;
- instructor preparation and delivery cost;
- subcontractor/SME cost if qualification requires teaming;
- platform/AI/inference cost;
- accessibility/reporting/QA labor;
- insurance and contract-administration cost.

Expected contract revenue is not working capital until collected.

## Proposal decision rule

The final Appendix B rate must satisfy all four tests:

1. **Responsive** — matches the exact SCWDB pricing format and addenda.
2. **Performable** — covers verified delivery and compliance costs at a conservative utilization level.
3. **Competitive** — remains defensible against public live-instructor AI training benchmarks and evaluator expectations.
4. **Auditable** — maps payment to an approved, evidence-backed completed-participant event.

If a proposed rate fails any one of these tests, it is not ready for submission.
