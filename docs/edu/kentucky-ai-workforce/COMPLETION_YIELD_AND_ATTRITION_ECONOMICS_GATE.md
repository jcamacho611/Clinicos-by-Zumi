# SCWDB Completion Yield & Attrition Economics Gate

Status: **internal capture/CFO control — not proposal pricing and not a participant-outcome claim.**

Purpose: prevent Klinikos from setting an Appendix B per-completed-participant rate that appears profitable when modeled only on completions but becomes loss-making after accounting for participants who enroll, start, attend partially, withdraw, miss required evidence, or otherwise do not reach SCWDB-approved completion.

## Buyer truth

SCWDB's posted Q&A states that:

- approximately 980 unique participants is a planning target only and is not a guaranteed minimum;
- no minimum enrollment is guaranteed for any workshop/session;
- Appendix B uses per-completed-participant pricing;
- SCWDB will approve a uniform completion standard during implementation;
- registration or partial attendance alone does not create a payable completion;
- verified attendance and completion evidence must support invoicing, grant monitoring, and audit;
- participants may receive Career Readiness, an Industry Accelerator pathway, or both depending on need.

Therefore **enrollment, attendance starts, and billable completions are different economic events**.

## Core rule

Do not price against completions alone.

The unit rate must cover the cost of the broader delivery population required to produce those completions.

For each service/pathway model at minimum:

`billable completions = enrolled participants × start rate × completion-after-start rate`

`cost per billable completion = total delivery cost for the enrolled/start population / billable completions`

The model must not assume that costs disappear when a participant fails to complete.

## Required funnel states

Model these states separately rather than collapsing them into one "participant" count:

1. **Referred / scheduled** — participant is expected but training has not begun.
2. **Enrolled / rostered** — participant record is approved for the cohort.
3. **Started** — participant has qualifying evidence of beginning instruction.
4. **Partially attended** — participant consumed instructor/platform/admin capacity but has not met completion.
5. **Completion-ready** — required attendance, activity, assessment, and evidence requirements appear satisfied.
6. **Instructor/admin approved completion** — human authority confirms the approved completion standard.
7. **Invoiced completion** — completion is included in an invoice package with required evidence.
8. **Accepted/paid completion** — SCWDB accepts the completion for payment under the final contract.

Revenue must not be recognized merely because a participant is referred, enrolled, started, or partially attended.

## Attrition scenarios

Before final Appendix B rates are approved, model at least these internal completion-yield scenarios for each service:

| Scenario | Start rate from enrolled | Completion rate after start | Effective completions per 100 enrolled |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stress | 75% | 70% | 52.5 |
| Conservative | 85% | 80% | 68.0 |
| Expected planning case | 90% | 85% | 76.5 |
| Strong | 95% | 90% | 85.5 |

These percentages are **internal stress assumptions only**, not SCWDB forecasts, historical Klinikos outcomes, or promised performance.

Replace them with better evidence only when authoritative program data becomes available.

## Cost allocation rule

For each cohort/session, include costs incurred even when some participants do not complete:

- instructor preparation;
- live instructor delivery time;
- backup instructor/facilitator coverage where planned;
- platform access provisioned for rostered/started participants;
- participant materials;
- attendance verification;
- assessment and evidence review;
- accessibility/accommodation delivery costs;
- support/rescheduling labor;
- reporting, invoice reconciliation, and audit documentation;
- travel and onsite costs for approved in-person delivery;
- subcontractor/SME costs where applicable;
- program administration and QA;
- variable AI/inference or external-service costs only when actually approved/funded.

Do not allocate all cohort cost only to people who complete while ignoring costs caused by non-completers.

## Cohort economics

For each proposed class-size operating point, calculate:

`cohort contribution = accepted billable completions × proposed unit rate - total cohort delivery cost`

Test at minimum:

- minimum cohort size;
- recommended cohort size;
- one-instructor maximum cohort size;
- the stress, conservative, expected, and strong completion-yield assumptions above;
- remote-first delivery;
- higher-than-expected in-person delivery;
- 30/45/60/90-day collection timing.

A rate that is profitable only at the maximum class size and strong completion yield is **not performable**.

## Service-mix gate

Do not convert 980 unique participants into 980 Career Readiness completions + 980 Accelerator completions.

Model service mix separately:

- Career Readiness only;
- Industry Accelerator only;
- both services;
- uneven pathway demand across Manufacturing, Construction, Logistics, Healthcare, and Business Operations.

If a participant completes both services, treat that as two billable completion events **only if the final Appendix B and SCWDB-approved invoicing rules explicitly permit separate payment for both**.

Do not assume a participant will complete more than one Industry Accelerator pathway unless the contract explicitly permits and funds that pattern.

## No-show and cancellation gate

SCWDB guarantees no minimum enrollment for a scheduled session. Before signature, define the economic response to:

- low enrollment before a scheduled session;
- same-day participant no-shows;
- SCWDB-requested cancellation/rescheduling;
- contractor-caused cancellation;
- weather/technology disruption;
- an accessibility accommodation that requires rescheduling rather than an inferior substitute.

No minimum-session fee, cancellation charge, travel reimbursement, or reschedule payment may be assumed unless the final solicitation/addenda/contract expressly permit it.

If such protection is not permitted, the unit rate must absorb a conservative frequency of these costs.

## Completion-quality rule

Klinikos must not increase completion yield by weakening the approved completion standard.

The following remain separate:

- verified attendance;
- required participant activities;
- scored knowledge evidence where configured;
- applied demonstration/rubric evidence;
- instructor/admin completion authority;
- certificate issuance;
- invoicing evidence.

AI/Zumi cannot convert a partial participant into a completion, waive required evidence, or override instructor/admin authority.

## Cash-flow interaction

Attrition and reimbursement timing compound each other.

The working-capital model must calculate the cash needed to deliver the **enrolled/start population**, while revenue arrives only from **accepted payable completions**.

At minimum stress:

- conservative/expected completion yield;
- low participant volume;
- uneven pathway utilization;
- 60- and 90-day collection timing;
- two overlapping live cohorts;
- one approved in-person event with travel exposure.

Expected future completions are not working capital.

## Final Appendix B decision rule

No per-completed-participant rate is ready for signature until it passes all of these tests:

1. **Completion-yield resilient** — economically performable under conservative attrition, not just ideal completion.
2. **Low-volume resilient** — does not require 980 unique participants or full classrooms to break even.
3. **Evidence-cost complete** — funds attendance/completion verification and invoice/audit support.
4. **Delivery-complete** — funds realistic instructors, administration, accessibility, platform, travel assumptions, and subcontractors where applicable.
5. **Cash-flow survivable** — Klinikos can finance delivery until payment without relying on expected contract revenue as if already collected.
6. **Procurement responsive** — pricing format and any cancellation/travel/minimum-enrollment assumptions match the final SCWDB addenda and contract.
7. **Quality-preserving** — no economic pressure is allowed to weaken human-reviewed completion standards.

If any test fails, the rate or delivery structure must change before proposal signature.
