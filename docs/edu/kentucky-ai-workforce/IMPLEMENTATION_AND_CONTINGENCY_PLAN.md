# SCWDB Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network — Implementation and Contingency Plan

Status: **proposed implementation framework, subject to award, SCWDB decisions, final staffing, approved curriculum, and contract terms.**

## Objective

Be capable of launching approved live instructor-led services within 30 calendar days after written notice to proceed while preserving quality, accessibility, data-security, reporting, and human-review requirements.

The RFP also requires an implementation plan within 15 business days of notice to proceed. This document is the pre-award operating blueprint for that deliverable.

## Day 0 prerequisites

Work begins only after contract execution and written authorization. No contract cost is incurred before authorization.

Required inputs from SCWDB at kickoff:

- designated program/contract contacts;
- final participating regions/LWAs and expected demand assumptions;
- approved participant registration and data-transfer process;
- approved pricing/invoice terms;
- final reporting/data dictionary;
- branding/funding acknowledgment requirements;
- accessibility/accommodation coordination process;
- employer/industry-validation contacts or process;
- approved AI/technology rules and any prohibited tools;
- final completion criteria approval process;
- travel authorization/reimbursement rules;
- any required state/federal contract registrations or training.

## Days 1–5: mobilize

- confirm governance, decision rights, escalation contacts, and meeting cadence;
- lock delivery team roles and backup coverage;
- confirm remote-first technology stack and in-person classroom requirements;
- map SCWDB participant fields to minimum-necessary Klinikos EDU program records;
- confirm privacy, retention, approved data-transfer, and incident-reporting procedures;
- open curriculum/employer validation workstream;
- configure program, service, pathway, and cohort structure;
- open implementation risk register.

## Days 6–10: validate curricula and delivery operations

- review all five Industry Accelerator pathways and Career Readiness with designated reviewers;
- update examples based on employer/industry feedback;
- finalize learning objectives and practical competencies;
- finalize instructor guides, participant activities, assessment forms, and take-away resources;
- conduct accessibility review of distributed materials and software surfaces;
- finalize completion criteria and evidence requirements;
- finalize participant survey;
- finalize workshop calendar operating process;
- calibrate instructors against sample rubric responses.

## Days 11–15: reporting, QA, and instructor readiness

- validate approved registration/attendance/completion workflow;
- validate participant-level invoice evidence requirements;
- finalize monthly report and survey-report format;
- complete live-remote technology rehearsal;
- complete in-person classroom checklist;
- validate captioning/accessibility support process;
- run instructor absence and technology-failure drills;
- review privacy/security/incident process with delivery team;
- finalize delivery calendar capacity and instructor assignment rules.

**15-business-day deliverable:** final work plan, staffing, curriculum-development timeline, technology plan, quality-control process, and launch schedule.

## Days 16–23: pilot/rehearsal and final approval

- conduct at least one internal full-duration rehearsal for each service family;
- conduct focused pathway rehearsals for all five pathways;
- validate all final materials against approved versions;
- test alternate accessible response formats;
- test attendance/completion evidence capture;
- test participant survey and report generation;
- test rescheduling/low-enrollment workflow;
- resolve critical defects;
- obtain required SCWDB approvals before participant delivery.

## Days 24–30: launch

- publish approved routine workshop calendar;
- open approved registration flow;
- deliver first authorized live sessions;
- verify attendance/completion records within required deadline;
- issue completion records/certificates only after approved completion criteria are met;
- send participant survey within required deadline;
- log delivery issues and corrective action;
- monitor instructor capacity and upcoming schedule.

## Delivery operating model

### Live remote

Preferred routine mode unless SCWDB directs otherwise.

Required controls:

- approved conferencing/delivery platform;
- instructor and backup instructor access;
- participant joining instructions that do not require unapproved purchases;
- fallback dial-in/connection method where available;
- accessible captions/transcripts where required and supported;
- approved mechanism for activities/assessment evidence;
- privacy-safe screen-sharing practices;
- no use of real PHI/employer secrets in exercises.

### In person

SCWDB/LWA generally coordinates an appropriate location. Before each event confirm:

- room capacity/layout;
- internet availability;
- presentation equipment;
- participant device assumptions;
- whether shared devices/offline materials are needed;
- accessibility/accommodation needs;
- instructor travel authorization;
- printed/accessible fallback materials;
- sign-in/attendance evidence method.

The training cannot assume every participant owns a laptop, has an email address, maintains an AI subscription, or can create a new technology account.

## Contingency plans

### Instructor absence

- maintain approved backup instructor coverage by service/pathway;
- preserve common instructor guide and calibration anchors;
- transfer only minimum-necessary cohort/session context;
- notify SCWDB promptly if reassignment could affect delivery;
- reschedule only when qualified coverage is unavailable.

### Technology interruption

- preserve downloadable/printable activity and source materials;
- instructor may switch to guided group demonstration or approved offline exercise without changing the assessed objective;
- do not mark an inaccessible/unfinished activity complete merely because technology failed;
- reschedule assessment time where required.

### Low enrollment

- follow the final contract's minimum cohort/cancellation terms;
- where permitted, consolidate cohorts or convert to live remote rather than cancelling unnecessarily;
- do not assume SCWDB guarantees minimum enrollment.

### Severe weather / travel disruption

- use live-remote fallback when SCWDB approves;
- otherwise reschedule through the centralized calendar process;
- communicate promptly to participants through approved channels;
- preserve audit trail of cancellation/reschedule.

### Accessibility issue

- provide an approved alternate accessible format that preserves the learning objective;
- log the operational issue without unnecessary disability detail;
- remediate material/platform barriers before reuse;
- escalate systematic accessibility defects to program management.

### Suspected data/privacy/security incident

- stop the affected activity/data transfer where appropriate;
- preserve evidence and limit further disclosure;
- notify authorized Klinikos/SCWDB contacts under the final incident process;
- do not attempt to conceal, minimize, or independently adjudicate the incident;
- complete corrective action before resuming the affected workflow when required.

## Quality assurance

After each session:

- verify attendance/completion evidence;
- review instructor notes and participant questions;
- review accessibility/technology issues;
- identify recurring misconceptions;
- verify material/version used;
- trigger corrective action when required.

Monthly:

- delivery reliability;
- enrollment/attendance/completion;
- assessment and pre/post trends where available;
- participant satisfaction;
- instructor effectiveness;
- accessibility issues;
- reporting timeliness;
- corrective action status;
- upcoming capacity.

Quarterly:

- employer/participant feedback;
- changes in AI tools/practices;
- privacy/security/regulatory expectations;
- curriculum improvements;
- approved material/version changes.

## Staffing truth boundary

This plan defines roles and operating processes, not named personnel. The final proposal must identify real key personnel, availability, qualifications, backup coverage, and any subcontractors. Never invent instructor identities, pathway SMEs, Kentucky presence, travel capacity, or prior contract experience.
