# SCWDB Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness — Final Submission Assembly and Transmission Gate

Status: **mandatory internal submission-control checklist**.

## Incident captured 2026-08-23

An email was transmitted to SCWDB stating that a complete proposal was attached, but the Outlook message record showed no attachment. This gate exists to prevent recurrence.

No email may be described as the controlling complete proposal unless the final attachment is visibly present in the draft/message record before send.

## Final package rule

Submit **one searchable PDF** organized in RFP order unless the final RFP/addendum expressly requires another structure.

Recommended filename:

`KLINIKOS_INC_SCWDB_Kentucky_AI_Workforce_Readiness_Proposal_2026-09-18.pdf`

## Qualifications gate

Before final export:

- verify exact legal respondent name;
- verify signer authority;
- attach final key-personnel resumes/bios;
- verify three-year requirement treatment against SCWDB clarification/addenda;
- verify three Appendix C comparable-work references;
- verify names, titles, dates, scope, email, phone, and required value fields;
- never substitute an academic recommendation for comparable-work past performance unless the engagement itself satisfies Appendix C.

## Pricing gate

Before final export:

- reconcile rates to the latest Appendix B;
- reconcile all September 4 addenda/Q&A;
- verify one-time implementation/customization amount;
- verify Career Readiness per-completion tiers;
- verify each of five Accelerator per-completion tiers;
- verify in-person day/session pricing;
- verify travel basis;
- verify credential/basic certificate treatment;
- verify optional services;
- verify assumptions and exclusions;
- verify senior instructor/SME labor economics;
- verify working-capital exposure.

## Product-truth gate

Before final export, ensure the proposal distinguishes:

- implemented and demonstrated capability;
- configurable implementation work;
- roadmap/future extensibility.

Never claim:
- a specialized industry simulator that does not exist;
- an external integration that is not verified live;
- automatic credential/licensure verification without authority evidence;
- production deployment of branch-only functionality;
- autonomous completion/credential decisions by AI;
- guaranteed participant volume or outcomes.

## PDF preflight gate

Before attaching:

1. Searchable text verified.
2. Table of contents/page numbering correct.
3. All required signatures/certifications present.
4. No tracked changes/comments/placeholders.
5. No internal-only pricing notes.
6. No unsupported claims.
7. All tables fit page width.
8. Hyperlinks readable but not required for evaluator understanding.
9. Accessibility-friendly heading structure preserved where possible.
10. File opens cleanly in at least two viewers/renderers.
11. Filename is final and unambiguous.
12. File size is below the email attachment limit or transmission method is otherwise permitted by the RFP.

## Email transmission gate

Draft email first.

Before send verify all of the following from the actual Outlook draft/message record:

- To: `contact@southcentralworkforce.com`
- subject clearly identifies KLINIKOS INC and the Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network RFP;
- body says the attached PDF is the controlling complete proposal;
- final PDF is actually attached;
- attachment filename is correct;
- attachment size is nonzero;
- no older/draft proposal is attached;
- no internal working documents are attached;
- sender identity is correct;
- sent-item retention enabled.

After send:

1. Fetch the sent message record.
2. Verify `has_attachments = true`.
3. Verify recipient and subject.
4. Preserve message ID/timestamp as submission evidence.
5. Request receipt confirmation.
6. If any attachment/transmission defect exists, immediately send a corrected complete revision before the deadline and clearly identify it as controlling.

## Revision control

If a complete proposal is submitted early and later improved before the deadline:

- send the entire replacement package, not partial pages unless SCWDB expressly allows amendments;
- state clearly that the later complete revision supersedes the earlier submission;
- use a revision date/version in the filename and cover sheet;
- retain each sent-message record for audit evidence.

## Final no-go conditions

Do not call the submission complete if any of these remain unresolved:

- required attachment missing;
- required Appendix B fields incomplete;
- Appendix C references missing/inadequate;
- required certifications unsigned;
- qualification treatment unresolved without an accepted proposal strategy;
- final addenda not incorporated;
- PDF corrupted/unsearchable;
- unsupported product/deployment claims;
- internal-only material leaked into the proposal.
