# Klinikos EDU + Zumi - Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Master Proposal & Demo Directive

## Mission

Produce the strongest truthful proposal and evaluator demo Klinikos, Inc. can submit for the South Central Workforce Development Board Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network RFP while building reusable Klinikos EDU revenue infrastructure rather than a Kentucky-only product.

The proposal must meet every stated requirement and deliberately exceed the evaluation criteria wherever Klinikos has a defensible advantage.

The public product must never be renamed or hard-coded as a Kentucky product. Kentucky/SCWDB-specific names, pricing, compliance language, and configurations belong only in private proposal/configuration material. The reusable product remains Klinikos EDU powered by Zumi.

## Core positioning

Klinikos EDU is not a generic LMS, slide deck, or AI lecture series. It is a live-instructor-led applied workforce learning environment in which participants learn to use AI by using Zumi inside realistic work contexts, then prove they can evaluate, verify, correct, safely apply, and explain AI-assisted work.

Zumi must be presented as all of the following, with human authority preserved:

1. AI literacy tutor - explains concepts in plain language.
2. Applied workflow coach - helps learners use AI inside realistic tasks.
3. Prompt coach - teaches context, constraints, audience, privacy, and verification.
4. Simulation partner - participates in synthetic work scenarios.
5. Deliberate challenge generator - can present controlled flawed/unsafe outputs that learners must catch.
6. Verification coach - requires learners to distinguish AI assertions from verified evidence.
7. Reflection coach - asks learners to explain why an output is acceptable or unsafe.
8. Progressive-assistance engine - demonstrate, coach, hint, observe, challenge.
9. Instructor-assist layer - summarizes patterns and flags work for review but never decides final competence/completion.
10. Post-training work copilot - where permitted, the same Zumi experience can remain available after training so participants can continue applying responsible AI skills in real work contexts.

Human instructors remain authoritative for instruction, pacing, grading, competency, completion, credentials, escalation, and any consequential judgment.

## The differentiated learning loop

Every pathway should use the reusable Klinikos learning loop:

UNDERSTAND -> ASK -> DO -> INSPECT -> CATCH -> VERIFY -> CORRECT -> APPLY -> EXPLAIN -> PROVE

Participants do not merely hear about hallucinations, privacy, prompting, bias, or human oversight. They encounter those issues in applied exercises and demonstrate how to respond.

## Proposal scoring strategy - target evaluator confidence, not minimum compliance

### 20 points - Understanding / Technical Approach

Aim for near-perfect scoring by demonstrating rapid reskilling, adult-learning design, live instruction, multi-region scalability, low-barrier access, compatibility with WIOA/LWA workflows, and an operational model that reduces SCWDB administrative burden.

### 25 points - Curriculum / Pathway Design

This is the largest scoring category. Make hands-on Zumi/Klinikos applied learning the centerpiece. Provide all five pathway outlines and strong Career Readiness content. Every pathway must include an exercise where the participant evaluates and corrects AI output.

### 15 points - Experience / Capacity

Use verified organizational/team history and credentials aggressively and truthfully. Do not volunteer weakness language. Provide credible management, instructional, technical, healthcare/workforce, AI, business, and operational evidence. References must be real. Any qualification gap must be addressed through an allowed structure, disclosed staffing, or clarification rather than fabrication.

### 15 points - Implementation / Delivery / Accessibility / QA

Provide a day-by-day/weekly 30-day launch plan, staffing coverage, calendar model, class-size assumptions, backup instructors, technology contingencies, accessibility process, QA reviews, participant-support model, and quarterly improvement loop.

### 10 points - Data / Reporting / Privacy / Governance

Use Klinikos' existing institutional data model as a differentiator. Show participant-level completion provenance, reporting readiness, synthetic-data-first training, human authority, secure data boundaries, and auditability.

### 15 points - Budget / Pricing / Cost Effectiveness

Do not race to the bottom. Make pricing transparent, scalable, completion-oriented, and defensible. Include implementation/customization, per-completed-participant prices, cohort/session alternatives, volume tiers, in-person/travel, platform/software, badge/certificate, and optional services. Explain how the reusable platform reduces duplicate administrative costs and total cost of ownership.
