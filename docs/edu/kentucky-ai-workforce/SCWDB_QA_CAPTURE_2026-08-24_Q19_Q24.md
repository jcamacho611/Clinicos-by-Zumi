## Q19 — recently formed legal entity / three-year experience

SCWDB says it **may consider clearly documented relevant experience of key personnel, affiliated entities, or predecessor organizations** when evaluating the three-year experience requirement, particularly where the respondent demonstrates meaningful continuity of leadership, personnel, operations, or service delivery.

Required attribution rule:

- distinguish experience performed by the responding legal entity from experience performed by an individual, affiliate, or predecessor;
- do not attribute prior work to a newly formed entity if that entity did not perform it.

Current-prime capacity remains independently required. SCWDB says prior individual/affiliate/predecessor experience does not relieve the prime from demonstrating staffing, systems, financial capacity, management structure, and ability to perform the contract.

### Klinikos consequence

The age of Klinikos, Inc. is not by itself an automatic bar if the qualification case is built from properly attributed evidence. Klinikos must not rewrite founder, affiliate, predecessor, or team work as Klinikos corporate history.

## Q20 — Appendix C references

SCWDB says Appendix C references **may include relevant comparable engagements performed by key personnel, affiliated organizations, or predecessor entities** when relied upon to demonstrate qualifications and experience of the proposed project team.

Each reference must identify:

1. the organization or individual that actually performed the work;
2. that party's relationship to the current respondent;
3. the role of proposed key personnel in the referenced engagement; and
4. the nature and scope of the comparable services provided.

SCWDB encourages references that most directly demonstrate the experience and capacity of the individuals/organizations that will actually perform the proposed services.

SCWDB reserves the right to weigh how attributable referenced experience is to the prime when evaluating organizational experience and capacity.

The buyer's answer states that Appendix C requires three references for comparable work completed within the last five years, but does not expressly state that all three contracts must have been held directly by the current prime legal entity.

### Klinikos consequence

Do not use academic/professional contacts as Appendix C references merely because they know the founder. A reference must verify a relevant comparable engagement and must carry the required attribution.

## Q21 — experienced subcontractor / prime structure

SCWDB says a respondent may use a teaming or subcontracting structure to demonstrate experience and capacity of the proposed delivery team.

SCWDB will consider relevant experience of substantive subcontractors and team members when evaluating whether the proposed team possesses the qualifications necessary to perform the scope.

The experienced organization is **not necessarily required to serve as the prime respondent solely because it provides qualifying technical or instructional experience**.

Klinikos as prime must still demonstrate sufficient overall:

- organizational capacity;
- administrative capacity;
- financial capacity;
- management capacity; and
- contract-management capacity.

The proposal should identify each subcontractor/teaming partner, services, qualifications, and management of responsibilities. Subcontractors remain subject to SCWDB approval. Prime remains fully responsible for performance, compliance, records, and payment.

### Klinikos consequence

A qualified partner can strengthen the team without automatically displacing Klinikos as prime, but no partner may be presented as committed until a real teaming/subcontractor relationship exists.

## Q22 — contract budget / pricing ceiling

SCWDB refers respondents to the prior Question 16 answer and reiterates:

- no final contract ceiling or target budget has been established;
- final funding and service volume depend on Reskill DWG award, SCWDB allocation/subaward, participating LWA commitments, negotiated pricing, and actual participant referrals;
- approximately 980 unique participants is a planning figure;
- scalable pricing is requested, including per-completed-participant pricing, cohort/session pricing where applicable, volume discounts, in-person delivery costs, and assumptions affecting total cost.

### Klinikos consequence

Do not treat 980 participants as guaranteed volume, revenue, completions, classes, or payments.

## Q23 — annual cohorts / delivery-mode split

SCWDB reiterates:

- approximately 980 unique participants over the anticipated contract period is a planning target;
- no annual participant target or guaranteed cohort count is established;
- the 980 figure is not a guaranteed minimum of referrals, completions, classes, or payments;
- no predetermined in-person/live-remote split exists;
- live remote is expected to be the most common/routine delivery method;
- in-person is expected strategically for significant Rapid Response events, large layoffs, plant closures, or other concentrated groups;
- respondents should propose a scalable model accommodating volume, cohort size, geography, and modality variation.

### Klinikos consequence

Remote should remain the operating default; in-person pricing/travel must remain separately controlled and capacity-backed.

## Q24 — blended live instruction + AI-enabled practice

SCWDB says **yes**, a blended model may supplement required live instructor-led training with:

- AI-enabled practice;
- feedback;
- digital resources;
- workflow tools; and
- other learning activities before, between, or after live sessions.

Supplemental technology/self-directed activity may support but **not replace** required live instruction unless expressly approved by SCWDB.

Any technology should be clearly described, including:

- instructional purpose;
- accessibility;
- licensing/subscription requirements;
- privacy and data-security practices;
- duration of participant access; and
- associated costs.

Participants may not be required to independently purchase software, subscriptions, or equipment unless expressly approved and funded by SCWDB.

### Klinikos consequence

This directly supports the intended Klinikos EDU + Zumi model:

`LIVE HUMAN INSTRUCTION + GOVERNED AI PRACTICE + INSTRUCTOR REVIEW`

Zumi must not be represented as replacing the instructor or as creating official completion/competence independently.
