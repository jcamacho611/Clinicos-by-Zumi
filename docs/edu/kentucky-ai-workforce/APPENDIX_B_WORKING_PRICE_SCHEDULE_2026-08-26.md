### 7. Cohort-size, low-volume, and cancellation assumptions

SCWDB's published Q&A expressly leaves class-size, minimum-enrollment, minimum-session, and cancellation assumptions to the respondent and does not guarantee minimum enrollment for any individual workshop. The working proposal therefore uses the following flexible operating assumptions.

#### Live remote — Career Readiness

- Recommended cohort: **12–24 participants**.
- Working minimum for normal per-completion economics: **5 participants**.
- Maximum without a separately approved instructional redesign: **30 participants**.
- At SCWDB's request, Klinikos may run a smaller cohort. If fewer than 5 participants are scheduled, the working minimum session charge is **$1,325**, equivalent to five completions at the 1–99 tier, with actual verified completions credited against that minimum rather than charged twice.

#### Live remote — AI Industry Accelerator

- Recommended cohort: **10–18 participants**.
- Working minimum for normal per-completion economics: **6 participants**.
- Maximum without a separately approved instructional redesign: **24 participants** because applied exercises, instructor review, and pathway-specific feedback require more instructional attention than a lecture-only format.
- At SCWDB's request, Klinikos may run a smaller cohort. If fewer than 6 participants are scheduled, the working minimum session charge is **$3,150**, equivalent to six completions at the 1–99 tier, with actual verified completions credited against that minimum rather than charged twice.

#### In-person delivery

- Recommended cohort: **12–24 participants**.
- Working minimum: **8 participants**, unless SCWDB specifically requests a smaller Rapid Response or concentrated-employer session.
- Maximum without a separately approved staffing/configuration change: **24 participants**.
- The separate **$3,500 instructor delivery-day rate plus approved travel** remains the minimum in-person delivery economics; participant completion charges are assessed only for participants who satisfy the final SCWDB-approved completion standard.

#### Cancellation / rescheduling

The intent is to remain flexible for a referral-driven workforce program while protecting committed instructor capacity and nonrefundable travel.

- **5 or more business days before delivery:** no cancellation charge, other than nonrefundable third-party travel or venue costs already approved and incurred.
- **2–4 business days before delivery:** up to **50% of the applicable minimum session charge or instructor-day charge**, plus approved nonrefundable third-party costs.
- **Less than 2 business days / same-day cancellation / no-show:** up to **100% of the applicable minimum session charge or instructor-day charge**, plus approved nonrefundable third-party costs.
- If SCWDB reschedules the same session within **30 calendar days** and the originally assigned instructor can reasonably be reused, Klinikos should apply any avoidable cancellation amount as a credit against the rescheduled session rather than treating it as duplicate revenue.
- Weather, declared emergencies, widespread infrastructure outages, or other mutually recognized force-majeure conditions should be handled through good-faith rescheduling rather than punitive cancellation charges where reasonably possible.

These are proposal assumptions, not executed contract terms. Final cancellation, low-volume, and cohort rules remain subject to SCWDB negotiation and written contract approval.

### 8. Volume and invoicing guardrails

- The approximately 980 participants stated in procurement materials are a planning estimate only, not guaranteed referrals, completions, classes, revenue, or payment.
- Volume tier is determined by actual completed-participant volume under the final contract measurement rules.

- Ordinary participant software access required to perform the contracted training is included in the participant rate.
- No participant is assumed to independently buy software or equipment unless SCWDB expressly approves/funds that requirement.
- Final invoice-acceptance and payment timing remain subject to the written contract. The published Q&A states final payment terms will be determined during contract negotiation and must comply with applicable federal funding requirements.
- Approved travel may be separately identified rather than embedded in the per-completion rate, consistent with SCWDB's published Q&A.
