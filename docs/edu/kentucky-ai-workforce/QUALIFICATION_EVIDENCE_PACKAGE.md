# Klinikos EDU — Qualification, Key Personnel & Reference Evidence Package

Status: **proposal-development evidence register.** Use only evidence that has been verified before final submission. This document is intentionally confident about established strengths while refusing unsupported claims.

## Proposal posture

Klinikos should answer experience and qualifications with evidence, not disclaimers.

Lead with:

- founder-led technical execution;
- applied AI/software work;
- security-oriented engineering discipline;
- direct healthcare-operations exposure;
- healthcare-professional/practitioner input into workflow realism;
- functioning Klinikos EDU product evidence;
- repository, architecture, assessment, reporting, and governance artifacts;
- adult-learning/instructor-delivery capability supported by named, verified personnel.

Do not lead with company age, startup status, or language implying that Klinikos is asking evaluators to overlook capability gaps.

## Practitioner-informed healthcare evidence register

Klinikos EDU's healthcare pathway should be positioned as **practitioner-informed and operationally grounded**.

Before naming any professional, verify and document:

1. full name;
2. preferred title;
3. current credential/license only when relevant and verified;
4. organization/practice affiliation only with permission and current evidence;
5. exact contribution to Klinikos or EDU;
6. dates or period of contribution;
7. permission to identify the professional in the proposal or evaluator interview.

Acceptable contribution descriptions include:

- workflow review;
- clinical/administrative terminology review;
- documentation-workflow feedback;
- front-desk/scheduling feedback;
- privacy/compliance operational feedback;
- referral/care-coordination workflow feedback;
- lab/diagnostic workflow feedback;
- usability review;
- simulation/scenario realism review;
- instructor/content review.

Do **not** convert professional input into claims of accreditation, clinical authority, licensure, curriculum approval, or SCWDB endorsement.

## Organizational experience requirement

The RFP/Q&A must be read exactly as posted when the final response is assembled. If the requirement is stated as organizational experience, build the strongest lawful evidence chain available for the responding entity and its relevant predecessor/operating history where the procurement rules permit that treatment.

Do not fabricate formation dates, contracts, customers, or delivery history.

If final legal review determines that the literal requirement cannot be met by the respondent's documented record alone, resolve it through an allowed structural approach before submission rather than weakening the proposal narrative. Any prime/subcontract/teaming structure must be real, documented, priced, and consistent with the RFP.

## Confidence language

Preferred:

- “Klinikos combines founder-led technical execution with practitioner-informed healthcare workflow design.”
- “Our team brings applied AI, software, security, healthcare-operations, and product-delivery experience directly into the training environment.”
- “Klinikos EDU is an operating product foundation, not a slide-only concept assembled for this solicitation.”
- “Participants practice responsible AI use inside governed workflows and produce instructor-reviewable evidence.”
- “Klinikos is structured to translate evaluator and customer feedback directly into product, curriculum, reporting, and delivery improvements.”

Avoid:

- “We are just a startup.”
- “We may not have enough experience.”
- “Although we are new...”
- “We hope you will give us a chance.”
- “We are looking for a partner” unless a real procurement strategy requires and documents one.
