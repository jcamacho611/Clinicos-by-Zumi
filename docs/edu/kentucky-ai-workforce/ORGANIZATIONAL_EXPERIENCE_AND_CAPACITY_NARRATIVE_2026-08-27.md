## Ready-to-use narrative

Klinikos, Inc. is a newly formed New York healthcare technology company building software and training infrastructure for healthcare operations, responsible AI use, workflow execution, and workforce readiness. Because the company is newly formed, Klinikos does not rely on an invented corporate operating history. Where relevant experience of proposed key personnel, affiliates, or approved teaming participants is used, the proposal will identify the person or organization that actually performed the work, the dates, scope, role, and relationship to Klinikos, consistent with SCWDB's written Questions & Responses.

Klinikos is organized to administer the proposed engagement through defined operational controls for scope, delivery, evidence, invoicing, participant records, accessibility, security, subcontractor oversight, and issue escalation. The proposed program structure separates organization-level administration from pathway-level instructional delivery so responsibility for participant enrollment evidence, attendance, completion, assessment, instructor review, certificates, reporting, and invoice support can be traced to a defined owner and source record.

For financial administration, Klinikos will maintain distinct records for one-time implementation/customization work, verified participant completions, approved in-person delivery, approved travel, technology or licensing charges, credential costs, and any separately authorized optional services. Registration, partial attendance, projected participant volume, proposal value, or unapproved pipeline will not be treated as earned revenue or billable completion. Invoice support will be tied to the applicable contract deliverable and retained evidence rather than model-generated or manually assumed completion state.

For documentation and monitoring, the delivery model is designed around auditable participant- and cohort-level evidence. Records may include enrollment/import records, session attendance, module or pathway status, assessment evidence, instructor review, rubric outcomes, completion criteria, certificate issuance, participant feedback, program revision history, and other contractually required reporting fields. Synthetic or non-PHI training data is the default for instructional simulation. Real patient information is not required for the proposed workforce-training program and will not be used as training content.

For contract administration, Klinikos will designate a responsible contract administrator and maintain clear ownership for scheduling, instructor coverage, subcontractor coordination, participant support, accessibility accommodations, security/data handling, reporting, invoicing, and corrective action. Any proposed subcontractor or outside instructor will be disclosed, scoped, and used only after required permission and SCWDB approval. Klinikos remains responsible as prime for performance, records, compliance, and payment of approved subcontractors.

For launch readiness, the implementation plan is structured around a controlled startup period covering final curriculum configuration, pathway validation, instructor assignment, roster and cohort setup, reporting-field configuration, accessibility checks, security review, delivery-mode readiness, assessment and completion controls, and buyer acceptance of the operating plan. Live remote delivery is the routine delivery model, with in-person capacity provided where requested and contractually authorized.

Klinikos's current administrative approach is intentionally evidence-based and fail-closed. Proposed prices are not revenue; projected enrollment is not cash; an application is not an award; and a participant is not a completion until the agreed completion evidence exists. These controls are designed to support federally funded documentation, invoicing, monitoring, and audit requirements while preserving a clear distinction between verified fact, proposed plan, and executed performance.
