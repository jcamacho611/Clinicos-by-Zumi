# SCWDB Q&A Execution Lock — 2026-08-26

Status: authoritative proposal packaging control for the Kentucky AI Workforce Readiness Network response.

Source: SCWDB published Questions & Answers dated 2026-08-25. Final proposal remains due September 18, 2026 at 4:00 PM Central. Written-question window closes August 28, 2026 at 4:00 PM Central; responses/addenda are scheduled for September 4.

## Requirements now locked

1. Klinikos must propose the full coordinated scope: Service A plus Service B and all five Industry Accelerator pathways. Do not submit a healthcare-only or career-readiness-only response.
2. The approximately 980 participants are a planning target only. Do not model them as guaranteed referrals, completions, classes, revenue, or payments.
3. Delivery must support variable demand. Live remote is expected to be the routine modality; in-person capacity must remain available for concentrated Rapid Response events and other needs across participating Kentucky regions.
4. A proprietary Klinikos platform may support delivery and continued participant access. The proposal must state instructional purpose, licensing/access duration, accessibility, privacy/data retention/security practices, and costs. Participants cannot be required to buy software, subscriptions, or equipment unless SCWDB expressly approves and funds it.
5. Pre-existing Klinikos software, curriculum, methodologies, tools, and other independently developed IP are not to be represented as transferred to SCWDB. The proposal must distinguish background IP, third-party licensed material, and Kentucky-specific newly developed/materially customized deliverables. Final rights for Kentucky-specific deliverables remain a contract-negotiation item.
6. Completion must be evidence based. Registration or partial attendance alone cannot trigger a completed-participant invoice. Recommend attendance + required activity + assessment/demonstration criteria and maintain participant-level evidence sufficient for invoicing, grant monitoring, and audit. SCWDB will approve the uniform final standard.
7. No Kentucky office is required. Klinikos may serve as an out-of-state for-profit prime if it proves statewide/multi-region remote and in-person capacity, instructor availability, administration, financial capacity, reporting, accessibility, data security, and compliance.
8. Subcontractors, teaming partners, specialist instructors, and SMEs are permitted, but the proposal must identify proposed parties, roles, responsibilities, qualifications, and relationship to Klinikos. SCWDB approval is required before use; Klinikos remains accountable.
9. All five pathways must be addressed at proposal submission. A common responsible-AI foundation plus distinct pathway-specific applied modules is acceptable. Each pathway still needs meaningful occupation-specific scenarios, exercises, and examples.
10. No standard cohort size or guaranteed minimum enrollment exists. Final Appendix B must disclose recommended/minimum/maximum class sizes, travel assumptions, cancellation terms, minimum-enrollment economics, and other price conditions without treating volume as guaranteed.
11. Performance design should emphasize participant satisfaction, demonstrated/perceived skill gain, instructional quality/relevance, instructor effectiveness/reliability, accessibility/engagement, accurate reporting, continuous improvement, privacy/data/grant compliance. Do not promise employment outcomes that depend on other actors.
12. Healthcare pathway remains nonclinical/responsible-AI oriented: administrative and operational workflows, communication, scheduling, documentation support, information organization, privacy, verification, human oversight, safe prompting, cybersecurity/confidentiality, employer policy, and appropriate AI boundaries. Do not present regulated clinical decision-making as the training objective.
13. A fully developed LMS is not required at proposal submission. Representative slides, participant activities, assessment items, certificate/badge example, and instructor-guide example are required. Final curricula/materials/accessibility review/completion requirements can be finalized during implementation before delivery.
14. Klinikos may rely on clearly documented relevant experience of key personnel, affiliates, predecessor organizations, and substantive team members for the three-year experience evaluation. Never attribute that work to Klinikos if Klinikos did not perform it.
15. Appendix C may use comparable work performed by key personnel, affiliates, or predecessor organizations, but each reference must identify the actual performer, relationship to Klinikos, proposed person's role, and comparable scope. Three references for comparable work within the last five years remain required.
16. An experienced subcontractor/team member can contribute qualifying experience without becoming prime, but Klinikos must independently demonstrate present organizational, administrative, financial, management, and contract-management capacity.
17. There is no additional minimum prior-contract value or organizational-age threshold beyond the RFP/clarifications. Do not invent one.
18. Pricing must remain scalable and may include per-completed-participant, cohort/session, volume discount, in-person, approved travel, technology/licensing, credential, implementation/customization, and other clearly disclosed components as allowed by the RFP/Q&A. Do not convert the 980 planning target into guaranteed contract value.
