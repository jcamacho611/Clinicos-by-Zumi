# Klinikos Paid Audit Sales Funnel

## Objective
Make a zero-experience sales associate capable of consistently qualifying independent clinics, selling a paid Klinikos Operational Audit, collecting payment, and handing a qualified account into the audit and Founding Clinic process without inventing claims, ROI, compliance status, or product capabilities.

## Funnel
Prospect → Qualify → Find pain → Quantify → Sell paid audit → Collect payment → AI + specialist audit → Prove value → Founding Clinic qualification → $8,000 implementation → recurring platform relationship.

## Founder positioning
Klinikos exists because clinic operators can spend thousands every month across EHR, billing, insurance workflows, phones, scheduling, coding, staffing and other systems and still lose time and money through fragmented operations. The product goal is not another dashboard to babysit. It is an operating assistant that reduces strain and tediousness while improving organization, visibility, follow-through and assistance.

## Associate rules
1. Get to the owner, administrator, practice manager, or other authorized decision maker.
2. Ask permission to qualify the clinic.
3. Collect clinic scale: locations, providers, staff, encounters.
4. Collect economics as clinic-reported values/bands: revenue, insurance mix, billing arrangement, known leakage.
5. Collect technology fragmentation: EHR/PM, scheduling, phone, CRM, billing, fax, communications, AI, payments, other subscriptions and approximate monthly spend.
6. Identify the largest operating frustration and after-hours administrative burden.
7. Ask about claims/denials, no-shows, referrals, labs, missed calls, patient follow-through and other applicable workflows.
8. Use the system-generated qualification result. Do not manipulate the answers to force qualification.
9. If qualified, sell the paid audit and collect payment while on the call when the buyer is ready.
10. Do not promise Founding Clinic acceptance until the audit is complete.

## Audit pricing
- Solo/micro: $750
- 2–5 providers: $1,250
- 6–15 providers: $2,500
- 16–30 providers: $4,000
- 30+: starting at $5,000/custom

Pricing is intended to cover AI/API processing, specialist labor, analysis and report preparation. It is a real standalone service, not a free sales consultation.

## Audit output framework
### CUT
Potential unnecessary/redundant cost.
### CAPTURE
Revenue already earned/generated but not successfully followed through or collected.
### GROW
Supported operational opportunities such as recall/reactivation, capacity or cash-pay opportunities where applicable.
### ORGANIZE
Workflow, task, referral, lab, claims, patient coordination and accountability opportunities.
### ASSIST
Appropriate automation and governed AI assistance with human review for sensitive actions.

Every financial datum/conclusion must be tagged as VERIFIED, CLINIC-REPORTED, ESTIMATED, or UNKNOWN.

## Audit disposition
- NOT CURRENTLY QUALIFIED
- QUALIFIED WITH CONDITIONS
- FOUNDING CLINIC QUALIFIED

## Founding Clinic commercial offer
Current Founding Clinic implementation: $8,000 one-time, only after qualification. Recurring package pricing, integrations, usage and vendor/pass-through costs must be shown separately and must reflect the actual enabled scope.

## Required compliance/product guardrails
- Do not describe Klinikos as HIPAA compliant.
- Approved wording: “Built toward HIPAA-regulated deployment.”
- Do not describe Klinikos as a fully certified replacement EHR unless that status is actually achieved and verified.
- Do not claim planned capabilities are production-ready.
- Do not invent savings, revenue, losses, EHR vendor, patient count, owner, authority, or other facts.
- Clinical and financially sensitive actions require appropriate human review.
- Never guarantee ROI.
