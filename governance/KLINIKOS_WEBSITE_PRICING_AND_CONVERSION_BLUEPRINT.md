# KLINIKOS Website, Pricing & Conversion Blueprint

Status: GOVERNING PUBLIC WEBSITE / COMMERCIAL EXPERIENCE
Date: 2026-08-25

## 1. Core public-site law

> **Broadly discoverable outside. Deeply orchestrated inside.**

Klinikos should expose the breadth of the ecosystem through search-friendly, human-friendly capability, solution, specialty, persona, Grid and EDU pages while keeping internal complexity hidden behind one coherent experience.

The website is not a brochure. It is a product-led discovery, qualification, conversion and onboarding system.

Target:

`DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → RECEIVE USEFUL VALUE → ACCOUNT → QUALIFY → APPROVED OFFER → CONTRACT/PAYMENT → ONBOARD → FIRST VALUE → EXPAND → GRID/NETWORK`

## 2. Navigation

Recommended top navigation:

- Platform
- Solutions
- Grid
- EDU
- Resources
- Trust
- Pricing
- Sign in
- **Map my clinic**

Use mega menus and public hubs to expose major capabilities. Do not crowd the top bar with every category.

## 3. Homepage

The homepage must make Klinikos understandable within seconds.

Recommended headline:

> **Know what needs to happen next.**

Recommended category line:

> **Klinikos is the operating network for healthcare.**

Recommended supporting explanation:

> Klinikos connects care, operations, revenue, people, capacity and intelligence so healthcare organizations can see what is unfinished, who owns it, what needs attention and what should happen next.

### Homepage sequence

1. Hero + Public Zumi / Operating Map entry
2. Fragmented-before → connected-after story
3. Core platform map
4. Care / EHR / Current Visit
5. Telemedicine / patient experience
6. Insurance / coding / Revenue Integrity
7. Zumi operations autopilot
8. Grid / jobs / shifts / space / services / capacity
9. EDU / workforce
10. Enterprise / payer / interoperability
11. Vendor/stack consolidation economics
12. Trust
13. Pricing entry paths
14. final CTA

No fake customer logos, testimonials, ROI, integrations, certifications, compliance state, awards or usage numbers.

## 4. Operating Map

Operating Map is the preferred no-PHI public product-led acquisition experience.

Collect business/operational information such as:

- organization type
- specialty
- locations
- provider/staff count
- current software/vendor categories
- major workflows
- bottlenecks
- manual work
- communication channels
- billing arrangement
- desired improvements
- timeline

Do not collect patient names, DOBs, diagnoses, medical records or other PHI in the public Operating Map.

Output:

- workflow map
- fragmentation map
- likely operational continuity gaps based on user answers
- systems Klinikos could potentially replace
- systems Klinikos would likely connect
- possible cost/efficiency opportunities only from supplied/verified data
- what cannot yet be determined
- recommended next action

## 5. Public Zumi

Public Zumi should understand broad buyer/professional/student/patient intents and route to useful public experiences.

Examples:

- `I need telemedicine.`
- `I need a nurse Friday.`
- `We lose patients after missed calls.`
- `I want to replace our EHR.`
- `I need prior authorization automation.`
- `I need a clinical placement.`
- `I want to rent a treatment room.`
- `We need better billing.`

Public Zumi must answer only from approved product/public knowledge and current research where permitted. It must not expose proprietary orchestration logic or private account information.

## 6. Capability pages

Every major capability receives a public page when discovery value is material and claims can be truthful.

The canonical category universe lives in:

`governance/KLINIKOS_PUBLIC_DISCOVERY_SEO_AND_CATEGORY_TAXONOMY.md`

The machine-readable coverage contract lives in:

`src/lib/public-capability-registry.ts`

Every capability page should contain:

- clear H1
- plain-English explanation
- who needs it
- problem it solves
- complete connected workflow
- related Klinikos capabilities
- what remains external if applicable
- truthful current product/integration state
- security/privacy note when relevant
- FAQ
- related specialty/persona pages
- meaningful CTA

Public capability page != separate SKU.

## 7. Telemedicine public contract

Telemedicine is a required dedicated capability page and the canonical example of cross-cutting product exposure.

Public story:

> A complete virtual-care workflow inside Klinikos, not a separate video portal.

Show:

`BOOK → VERIFY → INTAKE → CONSENT → INSURANCE/AUTH → JOIN → CURRENT VISIT → AI-ASSISTED DOCUMENTATION → ORDERS/REFERRALS → CODING → BILLING/PAYMENT → INSTRUCTIONS → FOLLOW-UP`

Explain that media transport may be provided by an external compliant vendor while the workflow remains inside Klinikos.

## 10. Grid as distribution engine

Grid should generate public-safe discovery across legitimate resource categories while requiring accounts for meaningful participation.

Potential discovery:

- healthcare jobs
- nursing jobs
- shifts/per-diem
- professional services
- clinic rooms/office space
- provider capacity
- diagnostic capacity
- healthcare business services
- clinical placements
- preceptors
- education/training

Never create fake listings or programmatic empty location pages merely for SEO.

Meaningful Grid actions require account + applicable agreements + risk-based verification.

## 11. Mandatory account / signup

Public reading/discovery stays open.

Require account for:

- posting
- messaging
- applying/responding
- saving private opportunities
- professional profile management
- organization management
- transactions/reservations
- EDU participation
- patient portal
- clinic/staff product use
- authenticated Zumi

Signup should be conversational and progressively collect only what is needed.

## 12. Agreement path

At appropriate registration/activation require affirmative versioned acceptance of applicable agreements.

Store acceptance evidence.

High-risk professional/transaction activity receives additional verification.

Do not collect SSNs as general signup data.

## 14. Offer Registry

All commercial offers should converge on one server-owned Offer Registry containing at minimum:

- offer ID
- seller entity
- buyer class
- product/scope
- price
- currency
- billing interval
- implementation fee
- included usage
- usage/overage class
- effective date
- expiration/supersession
- experiment/cohort where used
- entitlement(s)
- approval source
- status

Do not scatter hard-coded prices through marketing pages and checkout handlers.

## 15. Free strategy

Free should be used for distribution, usefulness and network growth, not uncontrolled expensive service.

Potential free surfaces:

- public site/content
- Operating Map
- Public Zumi within bounded cost policy
- account
- public-safe Grid browsing
- selected professional identity entry
- selected EDU discovery
- patient access for participating organizations

Free does not imply unlimited AI, unlimited communications, production clinical use, or unlimited marketplace services.

## 16. Paid conversion paths

### Clinic Operating Analysis

`OPERATING MAP → APPROVED OFFER → CHECKOUT INTENT → PAYMENT PROVIDER → VERIFIED PAYMENT → RECEIPT/CRM → DELIVERY WORK`

### Implementation

`QUALIFICATION → BLUEPRINT/PROPOSAL → SOW → APPROVAL → PAYMENT/INVOICE → IMPLEMENTATION → ACTIVATION`

### Subscription

`APPROVED PLAN → PAYMENT/CONTRACT EVIDENCE → ENTITLEMENTS → USAGE → RENEWAL/CHANGE`

### Enterprise

`QUALIFICATION → SECURITY/PROCUREMENT → PROPOSAL → MSA/SOW/BAA/DPA AS APPLICABLE → SIGNATURE → PO/INVOICE/ACH/PAYMENT → ENTITLEMENTS → IMPLEMENTATION`

Do not route complex enterprise agreements through a consumer checkout.

## 17. Grid monetization

Do not use a single marketplace fee model across all categories.

Potential economics, subject to category-specific legal review:

- organization subscription
- recruiting campaigns
- premium professional tools
- verification/onboarding products
- business-services marketplace economics
- space/capacity booking economics
- promotions/listings
- customer-funded transaction costs

Sensitive clinical/referral economics require legal review before activation.

## 18. EDU monetization

Potential:

- institutional contracts
- workforce-board/government contracts
- employer-sponsored training
- cohort pricing
- simulation access
- enterprise learning
- custom curriculum/implementation

Prefer institutions/employers to pay when they receive the primary economic benefit.

## 19. SEO technical requirements

Public architecture must include:

- server-rendered/indexable substantive content
- unique title/description
- canonical URL
- sitemap
- robots controls
- breadcrumbs
- appropriate structured data only when facts support it
- internal links
- social metadata
- performance/Core Web Vitals
- accessible headings/landmarks
- no PHI/private data in URLs/metadata
- no query-param indexing explosion
- redirects for renamed/synonym routes
- no duplicate keyword cannibalization

Grid JobPosting or EDU Course structured data may only describe real active items that meet schema requirements.

## 22. Visual / design quality

Public website must feel like one premium enterprise company.

Use governed Marble/Obsidian/Black Label design system.

Every key public route must work in:

- mobile
- tablet
- desktop
- light/dark/system mode
- keyboard
- reduced motion
- 200% zoom
- loading/empty/error states where interactive

Avoid generic SaaS template appearance, inconsistent visual systems, fake social proof and decorative complexity that hides the actual product.

## 23. Final conversion promise

The desired public journey is:

> **I searched for the healthcare problem I have. Klinikos had a clear page for it. I understood the complete workflow. I discovered how it connects to the rest of my organization. I received useful value before buying. I knew what the next step cost or how to qualify. I signed up, paid or entered the enterprise process, and Klinikos carried the context into onboarding instead of making me start over.**
