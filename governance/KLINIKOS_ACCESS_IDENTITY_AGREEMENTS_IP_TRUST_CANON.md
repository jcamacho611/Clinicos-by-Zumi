# KLINIKOS Access, Identity, Agreements, IP & Trust Canon

Status: GOVERNING ACCESS / CONTRACT / TRUST ARCHITECTURE
Date: 2026-08-25

## 1. Objective

Klinikos should protect the company, users, patients, professionals, organizations and marketplace without destroying growth.

The rule is:

> **Public discovery stays low-friction. Meaningful participation requires identity, account, agreements and increasing verification proportional to risk.**

Do not require invasive identity data merely because it feels protective. Collect the minimum data needed for the actual risk, legal obligation or transaction.

## 2. Public access without account

Keep appropriate public surfaces open for discovery/SEO:

- homepage
- product/solution pages
- pricing
- public resources
- Trust Center
- EDU discovery/previews
- selected public Grid discovery where privacy permits
- public Zumi product education
- no-PHI Operating Map entry

Public access must never expose:

- patient identity
- private professional data
- protected organization data
- confidential listings
- proprietary algorithms/ranking logic
- internal prompts
- trade-secret implementation details

## 3. Mandatory account boundary

Require authenticated account for meaningful participation, including:

- posting Grid supply/demand
- messaging
- applying/responding
- saving/private opportunity actions
- professional profile management
- organization creation/management
- EDU enrollment/progress
- reservations/transactions
- patient portal
- clinic/staff product use
- authenticated Zumi
- private operational data

## 4. Mandatory clickwrap / agreement evidence

At appropriate registration/activation points require affirmative versioned acceptance of applicable agreements.

Potential agreements:

- Terms of Use
- Privacy Notice acknowledgment where required
- Acceptable Use Policy
- E-sign/electronic communications consent
- platform IP/permitted-use terms
- confidentiality obligations for nonpublic platform information where appropriate
- anti-scraping/anti-abuse restrictions
- unauthorized automated extraction restrictions
- reverse-engineering restrictions to the extent enforceable
- Grid participant terms
- professional/provider terms
- organization terms
- EDU learner/institution terms
- payment/transaction terms
- telehealth/AI/audio/photo consents where applicable

Store evidence:

- agreement ID/version
- effective date
- user identity
- organization context
- signatory authority where applicable
- timestamp
- acceptance action
- IP/device/session evidence where legally appropriate

Do not pretend a checkbox automatically creates ownership over unrelated independent ideas or lawful competing products. Protect Klinikos through code/IP ownership, access controls, contracts, confidentiality, trade-secret practice, trademarks, copyright, patents where strategically justified, and evidence.

## 5. Identity assurance ladder

Verification should increase with risk.

### Level 0: Public
No account.

### Level 1: Basic account
Email/phone verification as appropriate.

### Level 2: Profile assurance
Additional identity confirmation for people publishing professional/business information.

### Level 3: Professional assurance
License/credential evidence and authoritative-source checks when the person claims regulated professional capacity.

### Level 4: Transaction assurance
Additional verification before receiving payouts, entering regulated/high-value transactions, or representing an organization.

### Level 5: Enterprise / privileged assurance
Strong MFA, SSO where available, delegated admin controls, privileged-access review and enhanced audit.

## 6. Government ID

Government ID may be appropriate for specific trust/risk workflows, such as high-risk professional verification, marketplace payout/onboarding or anti-fraud review.

Rules:

- only collect where a documented purpose exists;
- prefer specialized identity-verification providers instead of storing raw IDs when practical;
- store minimal verification result/provenance rather than copies when possible;
- raw IDs require strict encryption/access/retention/audit;
- never make government ID a blanket requirement for ordinary public browsing.

## 7. Social Security numbers / tax identifiers

Do **not** collect Social Security numbers as a blanket signup requirement.

If SSN/TIN data is required for tax reporting, employment, payout/KYC, regulated financial or authoritative identity workflows:

- use the appropriate specialized provider/regulated workflow where practical;
- tokenize/minimize storage;
- never expose to ordinary staff, logs, analytics or AI prompts;
- strictly scope access;
- apply explicit retention/deletion/legal requirements;
- never use SSN collection as a substitute for proper IP protection.

## 8. Professional credentialing

Professional profile is not credential truth.

Track:

- profession
- claimed license
- jurisdiction
- license number where appropriate
- authoritative verification source
- verification date
- expiration
- sanctions/exclusions where applicable
- organization privileges
- supervision/delegation
- malpractice/insurance evidence where applicable

Potential authoritative sources may include NPPES/NPI, state boards, OIG exclusion sources, SAM where relevant, payer/organization records and reviewed documents.

Payment never creates eligibility.

## 9. Organization authority

A user creating an organization profile does not automatically gain unlimited authority.

Support:

- organization-domain/email verification where useful
- invitation/approval
- delegated administrators
- signatory authority
- location scope
- role scope
- audit
- enterprise SSO/domain verification when appropriate

## 10. IP / trade-secret protection

Protect confidential Klinikos assets through layered controls:

- code repository access control
- confidentiality agreements
- employee/contractor IP assignment
- contributor agreements where appropriate
- server-side proprietary logic
- secret management
- browser disclosure review
- no client-side ranking/scoring/trade-secret algorithms unless exposure is acceptable
- no unreleased strategy in public bundles
- trademark/copyright hygiene
- invention/patent review where commercially justified
- provenance and decision records

Do not rely on aggressive user identity collection as the primary IP strategy.

## 11. Grid trust and safety

Grid requires category-specific policy packs.

Potential controls:

- identity verification
- credential verification
- organization verification
- listing review
- report/block
- spam/abuse controls
- transaction evidence
- dispute process
- fraud signals
- suspension/appeal
- payout holds through compliant providers where justified
- trust history

Different resource classes may require different verification and agreements.

## 12. Patient identity / proxy

Patient identity is not public Grid identity.

Support appropriate:

- patient account verification
- proxy/caregiver relationships
- minors/guardian workflows
- release/consent
- emergency access/break-glass only under policy

## 13. Security requirements

Authentication and agreement evidence must be protected with:

- MFA where appropriate
- secure sessions
- rate limiting
- account recovery controls
- audit
- encryption
- least privilege
- anti-enumeration
- bot/abuse controls
- secure uploads
- breach/incident response

## 14. Final rule

The safest growth architecture is not "collect everything from everyone."

It is:

> **Open discovery → mandatory account for participation → mandatory agreements → risk-based verification → authoritative credential/transaction checks → auditable permissions.**

This gives Klinikos strong evidence and control while preserving acquisition and minimizing unnecessary high-risk identity data.
