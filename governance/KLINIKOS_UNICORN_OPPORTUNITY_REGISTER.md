# KLINIKOS Unicorn Opportunity Register

Status: GOVERNING DISCOVERY BACKLOG
Date: 2026-08-25

This register preserves major opportunities without allowing every idea to interrupt P0.

## Scoring dimensions

Score 0–10 for:

- Customer pain
- Revenue potential
- Distribution
- Retention
- Network effect
- Defensibility
- Cross-engine compounding
- Customer money created/saved
- Gross-margin potential
- Enterprise value
- Regulatory feasibility
- Time to value

Use score as decision support, not fake precision.

## U-001 Universal Unfinished Work Graph

**Thesis:** every important healthcare event implies next work. Klinikos should project the expected next action, owner, blocker, evidence and completion state across clinical, financial, operational, Grid and EDU domains.

**Why it matters:** this becomes the connective tissue of the entire operating system and the simplest frontend question: `What needs to happen next?`

**Monetization:** raises Care/Revenue/Enterprise value; powers Zumi.

**Moat:** cross-domain workflow/evidence history.

**Priority:** P0.

## U-002 Current Visit + Clinical Change

**Thesis:** provider workflow should be one continuous encounter focused on `what changed, what matters, what do I need to decide?`

**Why it matters:** direct professional requirement; strongest clinical differentiation; creates evidence for coding/revenue.

**Priority:** P0.

## U-003 Operating Map Product-Led Acquisition

## Permanent discovery rule

Every significant product/business cycle asks:

- What user still leaves Klinikos to finish consequential work?
- What vendor still owns the workflow?
- What can become Grid supply/demand?
- What can EDU supply?
- What relationship should become Network?
- What data/evidence relationship can create value?
- What action can Zumi safely prepare or execute?
- What product feature can itself become acquisition?
- What customer cost can Klinikos remove?
- What new revenue engine emerges when existing engines connect?

New ideas are recorded, scored and dependency-mapped. They do not automatically enter P0.
