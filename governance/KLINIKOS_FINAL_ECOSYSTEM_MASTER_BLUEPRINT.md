# 1. Final company definition

Klinikos is the **governed healthcare operating network** through which healthcare work, care, money, people, capacity, education, relationships, evidence and intelligence move.

It is not merely an EHR, EMR, practice-management system, billing product, telemedicine app, AI assistant, staffing marketplace, LMS, CRM, patient portal or analytics product.

Those are capabilities inside one operating network.

Permanent product law:

> **The complexity belongs to Klinikos, not to the person using Klinikos.**

Permanent operating question:

> **What needs to happen next?**

Permanent strategic law:

> **Broadly discoverable outside. Deeply orchestrated inside.**

The final user experiences one product even when regulated/external rails remain underneath.

# 3. Zumi is the intelligence and orchestration heart

Zumi is one user-facing intelligence identity spanning the authorized Klinikos environment.

Target:

`EVENT / INTENT → AUTHORIZED CONTEXT → EVIDENCE / RESEARCH → POLICY / AUTHORITY → PLAN → PREPARE → APPROVE IF REQUIRED → DOMAIN ENGINE EXECUTES → EVIDENCE → RECONCILE → EXPLAIN NEXT ACTION`

Delegated-autonomy rule:

> **If the user has the legal, organizational and product authority to do something, Klinikos supports the action, policy permits delegation, and the user has granted Zumi permission, Zumi should be able to perform that action for the user.**

Permission cannot manufacture authority that the user does not possess.

Zumi target quality is expert professional / doctoral-level evidence synthesis, not merely conversational fluency. For changing or consequential information it should retrieve current authoritative sources, rank evidence, show provenance, identify conflicts, state uncertainty, and connect analysis to governed actions.

Clinical/regulated professional authority remains with the authorized human or external authoritative system where required.

# 8. Clinician experience law

The actual professional feedback is binding product evidence.

Provider experience:

> **The physician should experience a medical visit, not a software architecture.**

Current Visit sequence:

`PATIENT SNAPSHOT → WHAT CHANGED → STAFF HANDOFF → TODAY → CLINICAL → ASSESSMENT & PLAN → ORDERS & RESULTS → DOCUMENTATION & CODING → CLOSE VISIT`

Important history/context stays immediately accessible.

Staff get fast role-appropriate intake, not the physician workspace.

BodyMap and Clinical Change preserve longitudinal `INITIAL → PREVIOUS → TODAY` evidence.

No-Fault requires comprehensive injury/case workflows and reusable clinical components, not one generic template.

Labs, imaging, consultations, EMG, procedures, telemedicine, dictation, coding and revenue state should converge into the encounter without forcing the provider to mentally assemble separate modules.

See the dedicated experience canon for full requirements.

# 10. Account / agreements / trust architecture

Public discovery should stay low-friction.

Meaningful participation requires account and applicable agreements.

Require authenticated account for posting/messaging/transacting/organization/professional/EDU/private clinical or operational activity.

Use versioned clickwrap and preserve acceptance evidence.

Increase identity verification based on risk:

`PUBLIC → BASIC ACCOUNT → PROFILE ASSURANCE → PROFESSIONAL ASSURANCE → TRANSACTION ASSURANCE → ENTERPRISE/PRIVILEGED ASSURANCE`

Do not collect SSNs as blanket signup data. Collect sensitive identity/tax information only when a legitimate tax, payout, employment, regulated-financial or authoritative verification workflow requires it, preferably through specialized providers with minimized local storage.

> **I NEED / I HAVE**

Potential classes include people, jobs, shifts, services, rooms, chairs, offices, equipment, diagnostic capacity, provider capacity, education, placements, preceptors, business services, logistics and other lawful healthcare resources.

Meaningful actions require account.

Liquidity is built by narrow market cells:

`RESOURCE TYPE × GEOGRAPHY × TIME × ELIGIBILITY CLASS`

Grid eligibility precedes ranking and payment.

# 14. Integration and vendor-replacement law

For every vendor/system classify:

`CONNECT → ABSTRACT → CONTROL → INTERNALIZE → REPLACE`

or:

`NEVER REPLACE`

when recreating the authoritative/regulatory infrastructure is irrational.

Klinikos should own the workflow and user experience first.

Examples likely to remain external rails for significant periods include payment networks, national pharmacy/eRx rails, payers/clearinghouses, labs, imaging networks, identity sources and certain enterprise networks.

Their presence should become invisible operational plumbing where appropriate, not another portal the clinic must operate.
