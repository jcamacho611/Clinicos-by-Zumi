## 1. Company definition

Klinikos is building a governed healthcare operating network. The company itself must operate with the same discipline as the product: one truth model, clear ownership, explicit authority, measurable next actions, and no silent gaps between strategy and execution.

The company operating question is:

> **What needs to happen next to increase durable enterprise value?**

The company value loop is:

`DISCOVER → SELL → CONTRACT → COLLECT → IMPLEMENT → ACTIVATE → PROVE VALUE → RETAIN → EXPAND → NETWORK → COMPOUND`

The company must optimize simultaneously for:

- customer value
- revenue
- retention
- gross margin
- product quality
- security
- regulatory safety
- capital efficiency
- distribution
- network effects
- evidence
- enterprise readiness

## 2. Operating principles

1. Customer value precedes scale claims.
2. Revenue truth is evidence-based.
3. Cash is different from booked revenue, contracted revenue, ARR, GMV, claims volume and economic flow.
4. Strategy may be ambitious; public claims remain truthful.
5. Every recurring cost must have an owner and business rationale.
6. Every major initiative has a customer, economic outcome, owner, deadline, evidence standard and kill criterion.
7. Product scope may be enormous; execution remains dependency-ordered.
8. Zumi may operate routine business workflows under explicit policy and delegation.
9. No AI agent may silently bind the company to non-approved legal, financial, clinical or regulatory commitments.
10. Bankability and venture fundability are separate operating tracks.

## 6. Pricing governance

All pricing must converge on a server-owned Offer Registry.

Every offer has:
- offer ID
- seller entity
- buyer class
- scope
- price
- billing cadence
- included usage
- overage policy
- implementation fee
- effective date
- approval source
- entitlement mapping
- status

Current anchors remain until deliberately changed:
- Clinic Operating Analysis: $500 one-time
- Implementation Blueprint: $1,500 one-time
- Founding Clinic Implementation: from $8,000
- Core: $995/month
- Growth: $1,995/month
- Scale: $3,995/month
- Enterprise: custom

Do not interpret anchors as traction.

## 32. Failure controls

Stop or redesign initiatives that repeatedly show:
- no customer pain
- no buyer
- poor margin
- high support burden
- regulatory infeasibility
- poor retention
- no strategic reuse
- no network effect
- no proof after reasonable test period

Do not protect bad ideas because engineering time has already been spent.

## 33. Final company objective

Build a company where:

- the website creates qualified demand
- the product creates measurable value
- Zumi reduces human workload
- implementation becomes repeatable
- customers expand because value increases
- Grid converts operational shortages/capacity into network activity
- EDU creates qualified supply
- Network preserves useful relationships
- Revenue/Financial OS connect work to legitimate money
- enterprise buyers can trust and procure the system
- capital is allocated rationally
- every important company state is measurable

The final company should not merely own software.

It should own a trusted operating position inside healthcare workflows while remaining disciplined enough to survive, scale, and compound.
