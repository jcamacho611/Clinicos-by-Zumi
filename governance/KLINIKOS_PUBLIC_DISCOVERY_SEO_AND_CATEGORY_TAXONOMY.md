# KLINIKOS Public Discovery, SEO & Category Taxonomy

Status: GOVERNING PUBLIC DISCOVERY ARCHITECTURE
Date: 2026-08-25

## 1. Purpose

Klinikos must be simple to navigate without being small in public discovery.

The public architecture uses **many clear acquisition doors into one operating network**.

A buyer searching for telemedicine should find Telemedicine.
A biller searching for denial management should find Denial Management.
A patient searching for a patient portal should understand the patient experience.
A nurse searching for healthcare shifts should find Grid.
A school searching for virtual clinical simulation should find EDU.
A payer searching for interoperability should find the enterprise/payer product.

Every major capability may have a dedicated public discovery page while still sharing the same internal backend and commercial bundles.

Dedicated page != separate product SKU.

# 5. Public Grid discovery

Grid must be a major public traffic engine.

Visitors should be able to discover public-safe supply/demand categories without seeing patient/private data.

Examples of indexable category/location combinations may include, subject to real supply and quality controls:

- nursing jobs by city
- per-diem healthcare shifts
- medical assistant opportunities
- clinic rooms for rent
- medical office space
- healthcare business services
- preceptors
- clinical placements
- healthcare training
- provider availability

Do not create fake listings or empty-location spam pages.

Public Grid pages drive users toward account creation for meaningful action.

# 6. Content-quality rules

Every public capability page needs:

1. H1 matching real user intent.
2. Plain-English explanation.
3. Who it is for.
4. Problem it solves.
5. How Klinikos handles the complete workflow.
6. Connected Klinikos capabilities.
7. What remains external if relevant.
8. Current product truth.
9. Security/privacy note when relevant.
10. Useful FAQ.
11. Related solutions.
12. Related resources.
13. CTA.

Do not keyword-stuff.
Do not create near-duplicate pages solely to target variants.
Use canonical URLs and redirects for synonyms.

# 7. Commercial conversion paths

Not every page uses the same CTA.

### Clinic operational pages

Primary:
**Map my clinic**

Secondary:
**See Klinikos in action**

### High-intent capability pages

Primary may be:
**See how Klinikos handles [capability]**

Then route to Operating Map, product tour or qualified sales flow.

### Grid

Primary:
**Join Grid**

Account required for meaningful action.

### Professionals

Primary:
**Create professional profile** / **Find opportunities**

### EDU

Individual:
**Explore training**

Institution:
**Build a workforce program**

### Enterprise/payer

Primary:
**Talk to enterprise**

Do not route high-complexity enterprise contracts through ordinary checkout.

# 10. SEO technical architecture

Require:

- server-rendered/indexable content for public pages
- unique titles/descriptions
- canonical URLs
- sitemap index and segmented sitemaps when scale justifies it
- robots rules
- structured data where appropriate
- breadcrumb schema
- organization/product/service/FAQ/job/course structured data only when facts support it
- social metadata
- fast Core Web Vitals
- accessible headings
- internal links
- no authenticated/private content indexed
- no patient/PHI in URLs or metadata
- no query-param index explosion
- redirects for renamed routes
- no duplicate canonical pages

Grid job/course structured data must only describe real, active, qualifying listings/programs.

---

# 11. Discovery completeness test

A major capability is not publicly complete until:

- canonical capability record exists;
- public route exists or intentional no-index decision is documented;
- route is linked from relevant hub/solution/persona pages;
- the page describes the complete connected workflow;
- current implementation/integration truth is accurate;
- CTA maps to a real next action;
- Zumi recognizes the public intent;
- analytics can measure discovery → conversion;
- relevant Grid/EDU/Network opportunity is cross-linked where useful;
- the page does not expose confidential architecture or protected data.

---

# 12. Final public design law

> **Klinikos should appear enormous in capability, simple in navigation, precise in language, and coherent in experience. A visitor can enter through the exact healthcare problem they searched for, then discover that Klinikos connects that problem to the rest of the operating network.**
