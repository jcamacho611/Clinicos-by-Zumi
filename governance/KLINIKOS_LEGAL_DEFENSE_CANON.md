# KLINIKOS Legal Defense Canon

Status: GOVERNING LEGAL / ACCESS / IP DEFENSE ARCHITECTURE
Date: 2026-08-27
Counsel status: REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION RELIANCE

## 1. Governing rule

> **Every prohibited act must map to a defined contractual consequence, evidence path, survival rule, and remedy.**

Klinikos legal protection must be layered, specific, evidenced, technically enforced, and drafted to survive scrutiny rather than merely sound aggressive.

## 2. Defense layers

1. Public Website Terms.
2. Protected Access, Confidentiality & Intellectual Property Agreement.
3. Role/product-specific agreements, including Grid, clinic, professional, EDU, AI, payment and healthcare agreements.
4. Commercial agreements, including MSA, SOW, DPA, BAA, order forms and negotiated amendments.
5. Technical controls: authentication, authorization, legal gates, server-side proprietary logic, rate limiting, audit, evidence retention, access revocation and incident response.

No single clickwrap is expected to substitute for every contract needed by every relationship.

## 3. Clear liability architecture

The agreement must clearly distinguish:

- Material Breach;
- Serious Confidentiality / IP Breach;
- Severe Protected-Asset Breach.

Severe Protected-Asset Breach includes intentional or reckless theft or commercial exploitation of trade secrets, unauthorized source/repository acquisition, deliberate security-control circumvention, mass extraction, protected-prompt/model/orchestration extraction for exploitation, deliberate credential compromise, malicious evidence destruction, coordinated breach, competitive exploitation of protected information, and material compromise of protected systems or data.

## 4. No indirect-action loophole

A prohibited act includes, subject to applicable intent requirements, directly or indirectly attempting, requesting, directing, inducing, financing, facilitating, assisting, enabling, conspiring in, or knowingly benefiting from conduct that would be prohibited if performed directly.

Use of an employee, contractor, consultant, affiliate, automated system, alternate account or third-party intermediary does not convert prohibited conduct into permitted conduct.

## 5. No technical-circumvention loophole

Restricted conduct expressly includes unauthorized:

- copying and reproduction;
- recording and archiving;
- scraping and crawling;
- bulk extraction and mass harvesting;
- systematic benchmarking for replication;
- source-code extraction;
- reverse engineering and decompilation to the extent lawfully restrictable;
- endpoint/private-protocol reconstruction;
- hidden prompt, model, policy, routing or orchestration extraction;
- Grid ranking/matching/eligibility extraction;
- authentication, authorization, rate-limit, feature-gate or tenant-boundary bypass;
- credential/session/token sharing or trafficking;
- AI ingestion/training using protected Klinikos materials;
- commercial derivative development from Confidential Information.

Non-waivable statutory rights remain controlling.

## 6. Protected-opportunity anti-circumvention

Targeted anti-circumvention protects non-public relationships and opportunities actually introduced or materially developed through protected Klinikos access.

It must preserve documented carve-outs for:

- pre-existing relationships;
- independently sourced opportunities proven without protected information;
- public-market relationships not introduced through Klinikos.

Duration and transaction-specific scope remain counsel-review items.

## 7. Remedies

Subject to law and no-double-recovery rules, remedies may include:

- immediate suspension or termination;
- credential/session/token/invitation revocation;
- return, deletion or destruction obligations;
- certification of destruction in appropriate high-risk relationships;
- evidence preservation;
- temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief;
- specific performance where available;
- proven actual damages;
- restitution, unjust enrichment and disgorgement where available;
- reasonable forensic, containment, credential-rotation, restoration, remediation, notification and incident-response costs caused by breach where recoverable;
- attorneys' fees, expert fees, court costs and enforcement expenses where contract and law permit;
- applicable statutory, copyright, trademark, patent, computer-misuse and trade-secret remedies;
- termination of licenses, evaluation rights, Grid/partner/data-room access;
- referral to appropriate authorities where legally appropriate or required.

Klinikos may not obtain double recovery for the same injury.

## 8. Liquidated damages

No arbitrary automatic fine is production-approved.

Any liquidated-damages provision must be tied to a specifically defined breach for which anticipated loss was difficult to estimate at contracting, reflect a reasonable estimate of anticipated harm, be compensatory and not a penalty, prevent double recovery, and receive licensed-counsel approval for the actual facts and jurisdiction.

No $25,000, $50,000, $75,000 or other strategy-discussion amount becomes binding merely because it appears in a draft or repository document.

## 9. Trade-secret and whistleblower protection

Worker, contractor and consultant confidentiality agreements must include the applicable 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) trade-secret immunity notice or a legally compliant cross-reference where permitted.

The defense stack must not prohibit lawful confidential reporting of suspected violations of law to government officials or attorneys, lawful sealed filings, or other protected whistleblower activity.

These carve-outs strengthen enforceability and preserve Klinikos's ability to seek remedies available under trade-secret law.

## 10. Electronic assent and evidence

Protected access follows:

request protected resource
→ server identifies current required agreement
→ full/reasonably accessible agreement presented
→ unchecked acknowledgments
→ explicit electronic signature / Agree & Sign action
→ server verifies current document key + version + SHA-256
→ server records acceptance evidence
→ protected access becomes available.

Acceptance evidence should include, as legally appropriate:

- acceptance ID;
- document key/version/effective date;
- content hash;
- signer identity;
- organization/capacity/authority representation;
- required acknowledgments;
- presentation/review timestamps;
- signed timestamp;
- source/protected destination;
- session/request evidence;
- IP address and user agent where lawful;
- signature method;
- historical superseded-version relationship.

Historical executed records must not silently mutate.

## 11. Evidence preservation

A person subject to a valid preservation duty may not knowingly destroy, conceal, falsify or materially alter relevant evidence in violation of law or a valid preservation request.

Klinikos should preserve its own relevant legal/access/security evidence under documented retention and legal-hold controls.

## 12. Counsel-review boundary

The following remain counsel-review required before production reliance where applicable:

- governing law / forum;
- arbitration, jury or class waivers;
- liability caps and exclusions;
- indemnity;
- fee shifting;
- liquidated damages;
- restrictive covenant / anti-circumvention scope and duration;
- consumer/business-user distinctions;
- worker/contractor confidentiality terms;
- privacy and healthcare-law requirements;
- international users;
- computer-access statutes;
- trade-secret notice language and employment-law implications.

Repository presence does not equal attorney approval.

## 13. Final rule

Klinikos shall maximize **defensible protection**, not performative severity.

A clause that is impossible to prove, contradicts mandatory law, or is drafted as an obvious punishment may weaken the defense. Precision, assent evidence, technical controls and narrow-but-strong remedies are part of the moat.
