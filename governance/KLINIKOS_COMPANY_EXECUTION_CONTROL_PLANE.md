# KLINIKOS Company Execution Control Plane

Status: GOVERNING COMPANY EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE  
Date: 2026-08-25

## 1. Purpose

The Company Operating System defines how Klinikos thinks. This control plane defines how Klinikos **acts**.

The operating command is:

`READ CURRENT TRUTH → IDENTIFY HIGHEST-LEVERAGE BOTTLENECK → CHOOSE ONE PRIMARY NEXT ACTION → EXECUTE → VERIFY → UPDATE AUTHORITATIVE REGISTERS → MEASURE → CONTINUE`

The company must never confuse final-form ambition with current company stage.

## 2. Authority chain

Company execution uses five layers:

1. **Repository/runtime/provider/corporate evidence** determines current truth.
2. **Company Operating System** defines company functions and operating principles.
3. **Machine-readable company canon** defines functions, metrics, cadences, capital classes, stage gates, revenue engines, decision classes, and Zumi authority.
4. **Control registers** preserve consequential company facts and next actions.
5. **Executive Review Gauntlet** challenges material decisions before major commitment.

No chat message, pitch, or model output outranks authoritative evidence.

## 3. Current truth packet

Before a consequential company cycle, assemble the smallest reliable truth packet needed for the decision.

The packet may include:

- exact repository `main` SHA
- production/runtime state
- current payment/processor evidence
- cash actually received
- active customer/prospect evidence
- approved offers/prices
- executed contracts
- customer implementation state
- first-value evidence
- current pipeline
- current capital evidence
- security/incidents
- integration lifecycle states
- relevant corporate filings/agreements

Missing evidence is reported as missing. It is not filled with assumptions.

## 4. Executive brief contract

Every daily or material executive brief should contain:

### Current truth
Facts that are current and evidenced.

### Customer and pipeline
What active buyers/customers need and what commercial state is verified.

### Production and security
What affects reliability, release, customer trust, privacy, or safety.

### Capital and runway
What current evidence says about cash, obligations, financing, and near-term capital needs.

### Active company stage
The highest stage for which exit evidence from the prior stage has been met.

### Highest-leverage bottleneck
The single constraint currently limiting cash, customer value, activation, retention, network liquidity, enterprise proof, or platform scale.

### Next action
Exactly one primary action.

### Evidence needed
What objective result will prove the action worked.

Secondary work may continue, but there is one primary company action.

## 5. Priority formula

Prioritize work using this order:

1. patient/customer/safety-critical issue
2. security/privacy/release blocker
3. paying or signed customer blocker
4. near-term cash/revenue activation
5. first-value / retention blocker
6. reusable prerequisite for the active value loop
7. Grid/Network/EDU liquidity or repeat-value proof
8. enterprise procurement blocker
9. capital-efficiency improvement
10. strategic expansion

A new idea does not become P0 because it sounds large.

## 6. Company decision classes

### Routine reversible

Examples:

- ordinary CRM housekeeping
- approved follow-up inside policy
- metadata correction
- low-risk internal documentation

May be performed by the accountable function or authorized Zumi workflow when rollback is straightforward.

### Material reversible

Examples:

- major campaign
- substantial roadmap reprioritization
- new pricing experiment that can be ended cleanly
- new vendor trial without lock-in

Requires evidence, accountable executive review, risk recording, and review/rollback trigger.

### Material irreversible

Examples:

- signed long-term contract
- equity issuance
- debt commitment
- acquisition
- material public claim with legal exposure
- migration creating irreversible external dependence

Requires Executive Review Gauntlet, appropriate legal/finance/security review, explicit authorized human approval, and executed evidence.

### Non-delegable

AI may prepare evidence but cannot supply the missing legal/professional authority.

Includes:

- legal signature authority
- ownership authority
- banking authority
- professional clinical authority
- patient consent
- regulated credential authority
- irreversible third-party commitments outside approved policy
- unsupported public claims

## 7. Revenue-engine control

Klinikos may ultimately support many revenue engines, but each engine must have:

- real buyer class
- accountable company function
- approved monetization class
- proof requirements
- connection to the company value loop
- customer-value evidence
- margin/cost understanding before aggressive scale
- applicable legal/regulatory review

A product capability is not automatically a SKU.

A public SEO page is not automatically a product entitlement.

A transaction volume is not automatically Klinikos revenue.

## 8. Capital allocation law

Before approving meaningful spend, document:

- current company stage
- bottleneck being solved
- expected customer/business effect
- cash cost
- recurring cost
- time to value
- reversibility
- alternative build/buy/partner path
- evidence that will justify continuation
- kill criterion

Capital follows evidence, not ego.

## 9. Hiring law

No role is approved because a large company would normally have one.

A role needs:

- bottleneck
- why existing people/automation/partner cannot solve it
- cost
- 90-day outcomes
- measurable success
- ongoing leverage

One person may cover multiple company functions while the function remains explicitly represented.

## 10. Build / buy / partner / license / invest / ignore

Evaluate consequential external capability choices using:

- customer pain
- strategic importance
- workflow control
- data/evidence advantage
- current vendor cost
- internal build cost
- build time
- reliability requirement
- regulatory complexity
- integration scarcity
- support burden
- margin opportunity
- distribution opportunity
- capital requirement
- switching/exit cost

The default is not always BUILD.

## 11. Zumi as company operating intelligence

Zumi should progressively help operate the company under explicit authority.

### L0 Observe
Read authorized evidence and explain state.

### L1 Recommend
Recommend next action.

### L2 Prepare
Prepare artifacts, analysis, drafts, updates, packages, and workflows.

### L3 Execute after approval
Execute a specific approved action when tooling and policy permit.

### L4 Standing low-risk delegation
Execute bounded reversible repetitive work under standing policy, monitoring, rate limits, and audit.

### L5 Non-delegable
Research and prepare only.

Zumi does not become a parallel corporate authority.

## 12. Registers as company memory

The authoritative register definitions live in:

`governance/KLINIKOS_COMPANY_CONTROL_REGISTERS.md`

Machine-readable register identities live in:

`src/lib/company-execution-control-plane.ts`

Every consequential company fact should end in the correct register, with source and next action.

Chat memory is useful context. It is not final corporate truth.

## 13. Company metric law

The metric authority remains:

`src/lib/company-operating-canon.ts`

Metric truth classes:

- `CURRENT_FACT_ONLY`
- `DERIVED_FROM_CURRENT_FACTS`
- `SCENARIO_ONLY`

Stage advancement may not rely on `SCENARIO_ONLY` metrics.

This prevents a scenario model, TAM calculation, or aspirational revenue plan from masquerading as operating proof.

## 14. Company and economic-flow distinction

Always separate:

### Klinikos revenue
Money earned by Klinikos under current accounting/commercial evidence.

### Healthcare economic flow coordinated
Claims, payments, Grid fulfillment, professional earnings, capacity value, procurement, workforce value, or other economic activity moving through supported Klinikos workflows.

Economic flow can become strategically enormous without being identical to company revenue.

## 15. Definition of a successful company action

A company action is not successful because it was performed.

It is successful when:

`DEFINED PROBLEM → AUTHORIZED ACTION → EXECUTED CHANGE → OBJECTIVE EVIDENCE → REGISTER UPDATE → METRIC / CUSTOMER VALUE EFFECT → NEXT STATE`

If evidence does not change, learn and revise.

## 16. Permanent final law

The goal is not to produce a unicorn narrative.

The goal is to operate a company whose product, customers, economics, security, distribution, execution discipline, network effects, evidence, and capital efficiency make extraordinary scale increasingly rational.
