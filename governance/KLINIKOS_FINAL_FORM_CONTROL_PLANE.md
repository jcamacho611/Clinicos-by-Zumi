## 1. What we are building

Klinikos is a **governed healthcare operating network** with one shared substrate connecting:

- Care / EHR / EMR / Practice Management
- Patient
- Revenue / Financial OS
- Zumi / Intelligence
- Grid / healthcare capacity and opportunity
- Network / repeat governed relationships
- EDU / workforce development
- Enterprise / multi-organization governance
- Integration Hub / external authoritative rails
- Commercial acquisition / Operating Map / AI-operated digital business

The product is not a loose bundle of modules. The product is one operating environment whose surfaces are role-specific projections over shared governed truth.

## 2. Human-experience law

> **The complexity belongs to Klinikos, not to the person using Klinikos.**

Primary operating question:

Every surface should translate complex state into:

- what is happening

## 3. Business architecture

### Land

## 5. Zumi operating model

Zumi is one user-facing intelligence identity with multiple governed internal capabilities.

`OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → PREPARE → VALIDATE → ROUTE → EXECUTE WHEN AUTHORIZED → MONITOR → RECONCILE → ESCALATE → EXPLAIN`

Autonomy levels:

- L0 Observe
- L1 Recommend
- L2 Prepare
- L3 Execute after explicit authorized approval
- L4 Pre-authorized low-risk deterministic autopilot
- L5 Prohibited autonomy for professional/regulated authority

Examples of L4 targets where policy and evidence permit:

- tomorrow readiness checks
- eligibility checks
- status polling
- reminder/follow-up orchestration
- CRM stage updates
- payment reconciliation workflows
- low-risk support triage
- website/SEO health monitoring

## 13. Investor story vs lender story

### Venture/investor story

Fundability comes from:

- large market
- clear wedge
- repeatable customer value
- recurring revenue
- retention/expansion
- improving gross margin
- distribution advantage
- network effects
- data/evidence moat
- enterprise expansion
- credible leadership/execution

### Bank/lender story

Bankability comes from:

- operating business
- revenue/cash flow
- repayment ability
- creditworthiness
- contracts/receivables
- financial records
- documented use of funds
- guarantees/collateral where applicable

A compelling unicorn narrative does not replace repayment evidence.

## 14. Scale model

Do not use impossible revenue targets as proof of ambition.

A $1B valuation is a unicorn. $1B/month revenue equals $12B/year and is not required.

Use operating milestones:

- first $1 paid
- first 10 paying customers
- first $100K ARR
- $1M ARR
- $10M ARR
- $50M ARR
- $100M ARR
- $250M+ ARR
- $1B ARR long-term platform test

The final-form architecture should contain enough connected economic engines to make very large revenue possible without assuming it will happen.

## 16. Change-control law

New ideas are welcome and expected.

A new idea interrupts active P0 only when it is:

- required for safety
- required for a paying/signed customer
- required to activate revenue
- a blocking dependency
- a reusable primitive without which P0 cannot work correctly

Otherwise record it in the Unicorn Opportunity Register and continue current P0.
