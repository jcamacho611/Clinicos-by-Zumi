# KLINIKOS Company Stage Gates

Status: GOVERNING COMPANY MATURITY / CLAIMS / CAPITAL DISCIPLINE  
Date: 2026-08-25

## 1. Why stage gates exist

Klinikos has a very large final-form architecture. The company must not behave as though every final-form capability, commercial engine, network effect, enterprise control, or financial milestone already exists.

Stage gates separate:

- what the company can eventually become
- what the company is currently able to prove
- what it is allowed to claim
- what kind of capital deployment is rational now
- what the next evidence target should be

## 2. Stage 0: Truth Foundation

### Objective

Create reliable corporate, product, commercial, payment, security, integration, and evidence truth before scaling claims or irreversible commitments.

### Entry evidence

- current repository baseline
- current corporate evidence inventory
- current offer/pricing evidence

### Exit evidence

- authoritative company registers exist
- current/proposed/executed states are separable
- critical public claims can be traced to evidence
- payment and entitlement truth are separate from browser redirects
- integration lifecycle states are explicit
- security controls are described according to actual evidence

### Allowed claims

- verified current state
- explicit proposed state
- executed state with evidence

### Forbidden shortcuts

- planned control described as implemented
- pipeline described as revenue
- adapter described as production integration
- product roadmap described as current product
- market-size math described as company traction

### Capital behavior

Favor:

- essential production/security work
- near-term customer acquisition
- customer delivery
- low-cost reusable architecture
- evidence creation

Avoid large speculative fixed cost.

## 3. Stage 1: Cash Proof

### Objective

Prove a real outside buyer will pay for a defined Klinikos problem/solution path.

### Exit evidence

At least one of the following must be verified:

- real customer cash received
- executed customer contract with enforceable commercial value

And:

- delivery obligation is tracked
- first-value target exists
- buyer/problem/offer are documented

### Allowed claims

- actual paid customer/contract evidence
- actual cash received
- current approved offer performance

### Forbidden shortcuts

- internal/test Stripe payment as customer proof
- verbal interest as a contract
- proposal amount as revenue
- unpaid invoice as cash

### Capital behavior

Prioritize customer-funded proof and work that shortens the path from sale to first value.

## 4. Stage 2: Repeatable Value

### Objective

Prove Klinikos can repeatedly activate customers, deliver first value, deliver repeat value, retain accounts, and improve economics.

### Evidence targets

- multiple external customer/cohort activations
- measured time to first value
- recurring-revenue truth where applicable
- repeat-value evidence
- customer retention evidence over a meaningful window
- expansion evidence where applicable
- gross-margin drivers understood
- support burden measured

### Allowed claims

- MRR/ARR only from active paying recurring customers
- time-to-value only from measured cohorts
- verified/realized customer value only where evidence supports it

### Forbidden shortcuts

- annualizing one-time implementation fees into ARR
- early usage described as retention
- modeled savings described as realized ROI
- signed account described as activated customer

### Capital behavior

Venture capital becomes more rational when capital can accelerate a repeatable engine rather than merely fund discovery.

## 5. Stage 3: Network Proof

### Objective

Prove that Grid, Network, EDU, professional identity, capacity, and related network loops create repeatable real-world fulfillment.

### Evidence targets

By defined market cell:

`RESOURCE TYPE × GEOGRAPHY × TIME × ELIGIBILITY CLASS`

Measure:

- real demand
- eligible supply
- candidates
- match time
- fulfillment
- repeat fulfillment
- dispute/failure rate
- professional economic outcome where appropriate
- institutional EDU outcomes where promised

### Allowed claims

- liquidity for the measured cell
- verified fulfillment
- verified professional/institutional outcomes

### Forbidden shortcuts

- account count as liquidity
- listings as fulfillment
- matches as completed work
- course completion as licensure
- EDU participation as job placement

### Capital behavior

Concentrate capital in market cells with evidence of repeat fulfillment rather than attempting national liquidity everywhere simultaneously.

## 6. Stage 4: Enterprise Proof

### Objective

Prove Klinikos can satisfy serious enterprise procurement, implementation, security, support, governance, integration, and reliability expectations.

### Evidence targets

- real enterprise buyer requirements
- executed enterprise agreement or equivalent procurement proof
- security-assurance evidence
- enterprise identity/access model
- production integration evidence where promised
- repeatable migration/implementation path
- support/escalation model
- measured production reliability

### Allowed claims

Only enterprise capabilities supported by evidence.

### Forbidden shortcuts

- enterprise webpage treated as enterprise readiness
- SSO prototype treated as complete enterprise IAM
- planned SOC 2 treated as certification
- sandbox adapter treated as production interoperability
- generic support inbox treated as enterprise support operation

### Capital behavior

Spend on controls that unlock specific enterprise revenue, repeat procurement, or reusable enterprise capability.

## 7. Stage 5: Platform Scale

### Objective

Scale Klinikos as a multi-engine operating network with durable recurring revenue, enterprise adoption, network effects, partner leverage, and large economic flow.

### Evidence targets

- multiple durable revenue engines
- strong retention and expansion
- measured gross margin
- reliable large-scale infrastructure
- repeat network fulfillment
- enterprise adoption
- partner/developer leverage
- economic flow measured separately from company revenue
- capital allocation tied to proven unit economics

### Allowed claims

Platform-scale claims must be supported by current revenue, customer, enterprise, network, reliability, and economic-flow evidence.

### Forbidden shortcuts

- using TAM as traction
- using economic flow as revenue
- using valuation as company performance
- using financing announcements as product-market proof

## 8. Stage transition law

A company does not advance because a founder, investor, engineer, or AI believes it should.

A stage advances when the prior stage's exit evidence exists.

If evidence regresses materially, the company may temporarily operate under an earlier stage's discipline for the affected system.

## 9. Product maturity and company maturity are different

A subsystem may be technically advanced while the commercial company remains early.

A large customer may create enterprise requirements while Grid remains pre-liquidity.

Therefore maintain both:

- company-level stage
- domain-specific maturity/evidence

Do not flatten the entire company into one maturity label.

## 10. Stage-aware Zumi behavior

Zumi must adapt advice to current stage.

Example:

At Cash Proof, Zumi should prioritize:

- real buyer
- real offer
- real cash
- delivery
- first value

not elaborate national network forecasts.

At Network Proof, Zumi should prioritize:

- density
- eligibility
- time to match
- fulfillment
- repeat behavior

not vanity signup totals.

At Enterprise Proof, Zumi should prioritize:

- procurement blockers
- security evidence
- integration reliability
- implementation
- support

not generic enterprise branding.

The final-form architecture remains enormous.

Execution remains evidence-ordered.

That combination is the discipline that lets Klinikos pursue extraordinary scale without confusing ambition with reality.
