## 1. What Klinikos is

Klinikos is the **governed operating network for healthcare**.

Canonical plain-English thesis:

> Healthcare already bought the software. Klinikos makes the work, money, people, capacity, evidence, education, relationships, and intelligence actually connect.

Klinikos is not merely an EHR, EMR, practice-management system, billing tool, CRM, staffing marketplace, job board, LMS, patient portal, telemedicine app, med-spa CRM, AI assistant, analytics tool, payments product, or procurement product. Permanent operating question:

> **What is unfinished, who can act, what evidence is required, what is blocked, and what should happen next?**

## 5. Grid — governed opportunity and capacity network

Grid is the universal governed exchange substrate for appropriate resource classes.

Human-facing grammar:

`I NEED / I HAVE`.

Examples:

- `I need an RN Tuesday.`
- `I'm available for work.`
- `I have my own client.`
- `I need a treatment room.`
- `I have a treatment room open Friday.`
- `I need clinical placement hours.`
- `I can take students.`
- `I need imaging capacity.`
- `I have equipment available.`

Klinikos assembles valid combinations of people, authority, location, resources, evidence, agreements, and money around a healthcare or business need.

For regulated clinical opportunities, the conceptual product pattern is **Governed Clinical Opportunity Assembly**: the network may help a qualified professional bring demand and locate lawful clinical infrastructure, but qualification, prescriptive/clinical authority, product custody, supervision, location rules, documentation, payment structure, and liability remain jurisdiction- and resource-class-specific.

Permanent Grid laws:

- hard eligibility precedes ranking;
- patients are never public Grid supply;
- regulated clinical inventory is not ordinary public commerce;
- payment, popularity, sponsorship, AI preference, or reputation cannot override a disqualifying credential, scope, supervision, consent, privacy, jurisdiction, or organization rule;
- no universal percentage fee is assumed for regulated healthcare transactions;
- fee-splitting, referral, professional-corporation, employment/contractor, pharmacy/product, home-care, and state-specific rules require applicable legal/policy gates.

## 7. Universal unfinished-work model

Klinikos is converging existing domain truth into a shared operational question:

> **What still needs to happen?**

Existing Task, Referral, clinical, financial, Grid, EDU, and other domain records remain authoritative in their own systems. Shared unfinished-work projections read and interpret those records; they must not create a second competing authority.

The universal lifecycle vocabulary may include states such as:

`EXPECTED / OWNED / ACKNOWLEDGED / IN_PROGRESS / FULFILLED / VERIFIED / RECONCILED / CLOSED / BLOCKED / CANCELLED / EXPIRED / DISPUTED / REOPENED`.

Symphony should use that mindset operationally: a task is not complete because a message was sent; completion means the intended external or internal outcome is evidenced or the blocker/next owner is explicitly recorded.

## 13. Approval gates

Automation should remove repetitive work, not remove governance.

Founder/governing approval remains required before consequential actions including, unless a newer explicit governing rule narrows the gate:

- sending external email or attachments;
- accepting or scheduling meetings on the founder's behalf;
- submitting RFPs, RFIs, grant applications, bids, accelerator applications, or financing applications;
- spending money or committing company funds;
- changing production payment/financial configuration;
- executing financing, investment, vendor, customer, legal, or partnership agreements;
- disclosing confidential/proprietary Klinikos material;
- releasing PHI/PII or sensitive security information;
- making regulated clinical/legal representations;
- high-risk production deployments or destructive data/schema actions.

Safe research, monitoring, drafting, classification, internal task creation, evidence collection, read-only status checks, and preparation may be increasingly automated when permissions and confidentiality rules allow.

No automation may bypass the confidentiality or truth gates.

## 18. Completion and refresh law

A Symphony item is complete only when:

- the intended action/outcome actually occurred **or** the blocker is explicit;
- evidence/source is preserved;
- truth class is correct;
- next owner and next action are named;
- no confidential or unsupported claim escaped;
- downstream systems/tasks are updated where appropriate.

At the start of every material task, Symphony must assume its memory may be stale and refresh the relevant current sources.

**Never preserve optimistic stale status. Replace it with the newest verified evidence.**
